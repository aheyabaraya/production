## Extend / Variation Notes
- For longer 8~12s version: Add slow camera orbit around her while the reflection starts to smile differently.
- Alternative camera: Start extremely close on the eye in the mirror, then pull back dramatically.
- Energy variant: Make the light beams pulse subtly with the hook.

## Usage Recommendation
- Best tool: Kling 1.6 or Grok Imagine Video (high motion)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 3:2 vertical for Reels/TikTok
- Reference images: Attach only face-only reference of RENA + the passed review board as style/motion guide.
- Things to watch out for: Hand exactly matching the pointing gesture in the board. Avoid face drift in reflection.
