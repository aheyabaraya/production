# Front Planning-Centered MV Director Graph Engineering

Status: approved implementation contract
Scope: IDOL song and music-video production from Front Planning through 04
Internal authoring language: English
Migration policy: named prior productions remain historical evidence, but the
active graph engineering does not preserve authoring or execution compatibility with
their previous stage contracts or scene language.

## 1. Objective

The graph-engineering system exists so the Founder can provide a compact direction and selected
references, review the result as the company representative, and normally make
only `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT` decisions. Codex, or the Founder-selected Grok,
acts as the overall music-video director and expands that nucleus through its
story, visual, staging, choreography, image, motion, and edit specialist skills.

Graph Engineering is not a form-completion workflow. A stage or field exists only
when it preserves a real creative decision, makes downstream production
executable, or lets the Founder review the video at the right level.

Quality is the first objective. Token efficiency and speed are achieved by
locking decisions once, passing only the smallest sufficient context, and
rebuilding only affected dependents.

## 2. Authority And Roles

- The overall director owns the connection among song, target, message,
  references, member placement, section progression, core cuts, branch cuts, prompts,
  motion, and edit.
- Specialist skills act as the director's writer, visual director,
  choreographer, image-prompt director, motion director, and editor. They do not
  independently replace the governing direction.
- The Founder owns explicit creative locks, selected track, paid generation,
  final `PASS`/`HOLD`/`REJECT`, and public release.
- A Founder-authored replacement value is final for its exact fields and
  affected scope. It does not trigger an unrelated upstream rewrite or a second
  advisory veto.
- Schema validity never equals a creative pass.

## 3. Front Planning Is The Governing Creative Spine

Front Planning begins only after the actual selected song and its audio
sections exist. It owns the Founder-reviewable answers to:

1. who the content is for;
2. what the viewer should receive and retain;
3. the current album's member visual identity and visual system;
4. which member or non-member carrier belongs to each section;
5. the whole-video progression and each section's major scenes, actions, holds,
   object or space states, and transition;
6. how main, supporting, sequence, and scene references connect and which
   section owns each applied reference; and
7. whether the director-ready IDOL start control projects every recommended
   Major and exact reference-use proposal once in order, preserves explicit
   Founder locks, binds passed member visuals without repeat review, and has
   director-quality-passed Key Screen coverage; and
8. which production questions 01 must expand without changing the locked
   direction.

Front Planning must be concrete enough to stop a single thin premise from being
stretched across the video. It does not need to lock the final core-cut count,
branch-cut count, complete still-camera coverage, final image prompt wording, or
video camera route.

When the Founder assigns a direct scene or sequence reference, however, the
observed action, composition, camera geometry, subject placement, crop, and
ordered frame grammar become preservation conditions for that assigned
section.

When the brief is not yet a resolved film, Front Planning begins with the
director-led conversational method in
`wiki/director-room-sequence-discovery.md`. The director proposes the first
rough sequence, challenges its pacing, ownership, repetition, reference order,
message, sound, misread risk, and continuity, and returns each revision as a
complete judgeable flow. The result fills the existing Front Planning fields
through a director-ready IDOL Founder Sequence Control. The Founder speaks in
ordinary language or requests the first proposal; Codex or Grok transcribes the
file. It is not a pre-stage, worksheet, fixed scene quota, or separate approval
gate. Front Planning closes only after the one consolidated Founder final
PASS, with earlier intervention limited to named exceptions.

## 4. 01R Operates Inside Front Planning

`01R` and optional `01R-C` operate as Front Planning substages:

```text
Founder short start input or first-proposal request + selected song
-> director-ready IDOL start control
-> target and message
-> 01R source, derivative, audience-code, and screen-function research
-> optional 01R-C selected frame or crop lock
-> section reference and member assignment
-> director-proposed Major Scenes + passed current-title member visual binding
-> exception selection only for new/restyled member visuals or material conflicts
-> assigned-director Key Screen expansion and quality review
-> one consolidated Front Planning PASS / HOLD / REJECT
```

01R must connect rather than accumulate. It researches the main reference's
source context, relevant derivative works, target-audience codes, and the exact
screen function required by each section. Every unit closes as `applied`,
`combined`, `held`, or `rejected`.

Each usable component is classified as `major_scene_grammar_or_feeling`,
`background_and_space`, `still_camera_and_composition`,
`lighting_color_material`, `subject_state_or_pose`,
`visible_action_or_hold`, `object_environment_response`, or
`edit_or_transition`. Its analysis keeps the exact locator, observed fact,
selected component, transfer boundary, do-not-transfer rule, and downstream
targets. Each Key Screen binds one governing or primary named reference
function; add a support unit only for another named screen function that the
primary does not close. This is bounded component combination under the current
song, member, and title package—not a fixed blend, vague reinterpretation, or
whole-source recreation.
Founder-selected full-scene/sequence grammar remains governing.

After Front Planning passes, 01 consumes the bound interpretations and section
assignments without rereading or reselecting them. Reopening is limited to
changed bytes or meaning, an incomplete lock, an identity/safety/feasibility
conflict, or explicit Founder re-review.

## 5. Current-Album Member Visuals

### 5.1 INK Is A Package Pattern, Not A Reusable Look

The INK asset package demonstrates the required organization and review depth.
It is not a cross-album image source. No new album may reuse INK member sheets,
hair, wardrobe, makeup, background, object, palette, or visual-system pixels
unless the Founder explicitly selects an INK asset as a direct reference for
that exact new title.

Every album/title creates or binds each required member visual inside the same
review unit as its Major Scene, then uses that passed member visual as the
default Key Screen and downstream appearance base:

```text
assets/members/album-assets/ALBUM_KEY/TITLE_KEY/**
```

The active Front Planning `album_key` and `title_key` must match the selected
member asset set. A mismatched asset path, manifest identity, or
cross-album attachment is a hard `HOLD`.

An earlier album package is never inherited, substituted, or used as a silent
fallback. Omitting or replacing an applicable passed current-title asset
requires the exact Founder-approved exception.

### 5.2 Required Member-Visual Jobs

Before image production, the current title should provide the applicable:

- member-specific album visual anchors;
- unit or group album visual anchors;
- wardrobe and material anchors needed for member continuity.

Do not create a separate background/world asset package. Space, background,
palette/light, object behavior, and atmosphere remain observable Major/Key
Screen text and exact scene-reference bindings.

A member visual anchor is the title-specific board created to establish that
member's album appearance, hair, makeup, wardrobe, materials, silhouette, and
body continuity. It is built from the sole approved face-only facial-identity
source. Old `no-mic` files remain provenance or legacy-run evidence and are not
active inputs for new authoring. After the card passes, downstream work
receives the passed current-title card rather than a second raw face-only input.

The album member visual anchor never forces:

- frontal head or gaze direction;
- a visible or readable face;
- a portrait crop;
- the reference board's panel layout, border, or pose;
- the reference camera angle; or
- altered body proportions.

The 02A child and any direct scene reference own pose, head and gaze direction,
action, still-camera geometry, placement, crop, occlusion, and frame
proportions.

### 5.3 Selective Child Attachment

Front Planning assigns the authorized current-album asset pool and member set
to each section. 02A selects the smallest useful subset for each branch cut:

- If a member is meaningfully visible, select the matching current-album member
  visual anchor needed for appearance continuity.
- If no member is visible, do not attach a member visual anchor.
- A back view, silhouette, partial-body crop, or occluded face may still use the
  matching member visual anchor when hair, wardrobe, silhouette, or body
  continuity matters.
- Do not attach every member, background, or world asset to every branch cut.
- Prefer a current-title unit or group anchor for a multi-member frame. If no
  adequate group anchor exists and the execution surface cannot accept the
  smallest required set, hold instead of dropping or substituting an input.

Section assignment is an authorized pool, not an all-assets-per-child
requirement.

## 6. Sequence And Scene References

- A `sequence reference` is an ordered frame sheet captured from an approved
  source through the registered media route. The frame order must be inspected
  as a sequence.
- A `scene reference` is a selected frame crop or a separate image supplied by
  the Founder. It must be interpreted as a concrete scene.
- A whole contact sheet is navigation evidence. Every production-selected
  frame or crop receives its own path and SHA-256.

Front Planning assigns selected sequence and scene references to sections. 01
expands them into observable current-song direction while preserving the
selected source's action, composition, camera height/distance/direction/depth,
subject placement, crop, and ordered sequence grammar unless an explicit
discard rule says otherwise.

The current-album package and direct scene references have orthogonal jobs:

| Input | Governing job |
|---|---|
| Current-album member visual anchor | Member appearance, hair, wardrobe, materials, silhouette, and body continuity |
| Major/Key Screen text plus exact scene reference | Space, background, palette/light, object response, atmosphere, action, staging, composition, camera geometry, placement, crop, and ordered visual grammar |
| Direct sequence or scene reference | Action, staging, composition, camera geometry, placement, crop, and ordered visual grammar |
| 02A child lock | The exact current-song branch-cut result and intentional deviations |

No lower stage may let one input erase another input's governing job.

## 7. Stage Ownership

| Stage | Creative ownership | Founder gate |
|---|---|---|
| Song creation, selected-track analysis, and 00A | Finished audio, lyrics, section map, rhythm evidence, and the selected-audio lock required before visual planning | Track selection and song lock |
| IDOL start control + Front Planning + 01R/01R-C | Short Founder startup input; director-recommended whole story, Major Scenes, and reference uses; target, message, member/section allocation, whole-video spine, passed member-visual binding, reference-function connection, and director-QA'd Key Screens | One consolidated Front Planning `PASS`/`HOLD`/`REJECT`; earlier exception choice only for a new/restyled member visual or unresolved material conflict; no separate Major, Key Screen, or background asset gate |
| 01 | Section action, acting or choreography need, staging, still-camera coverage, space/object behavior, transitions, every Official Core, and exactly one passed primary image per Core | Only if a new global decision is required |
| 02A | Immutable import of every 01 Official Core plus internal Branch coverage allocation and each Core's ordered 1..N Branch-Cut realizations, visible action, still-camera geometry, composition, reference bindings, and continuity | Storyboard `PASS`/`HOLD`/`REJECT` |
| 02B | Natural-language translation of the passed 02A child into one executable image prompt and the smallest required image-input set | Packet/image review; no upstream replanning |
| 03V | Motion, timing, subject route, actual video camera route, and storyboard-assisted video prompt authored after passed images exist | Playback `PASS`/`HOLD`/`REJECT` |
| 04 | Song-timed source selection, edit, continuity, effects, and playback QC | Edit `PASS`/`HOLD`/`REJECT` |

The active route does not create separate `00`, `01A`, or `02` creative
artifacts. Their useful jobs are absorbed respectively by passed Front
Planning, 01's bound visual projection, and 02A's internal coverage plan. Old
artifacts with those labels remain run history only.

## 8. Core Cut And Branch Cut Definition

A core cut is the parent major event, action, effect, condition, or dramatic
beat inside a section. It may span several independently framed screens. It is
not required to equal one final editorial cut or one still-camera setup; 04
still owns the final edit boundary. Split a new core cut when the governing
event changes.

Each core cut owns `1..N` ordered branch cuts. A branch cut is one independently
framed and generatable screen realization derived from the parent event. It
earns its place by adding distinct observable screen information or a distinct
directorial job. Event phase, viewpoint, participant response, object or
spatial consequence, insert, aftermath, reprise, transition, and performance
translation are possible routes, not a closed taxonomy or quota.

A `start`, `middle`, or `end` label alone does not create a branch cut. Neither
does changing shot size while repeating the same action and information. When
the governing event remains the same but the screen reveals or extends it in a
materially different way, create a branch cut. When the event itself changes,
create a new core cut.

Front Planning provides the major section scenes and events. 01 expands their
staging, still-camera coverage, and branching opportunities, establishes every
Official Core event boundary, and owns exactly one passed primary image per
Core. 02A imports those Core bytes unchanged and makes only the ordered Branch
Cuts concrete enough for the Founder to judge before generation.

## 9. Still Camera Versus Video Camera

Front Planning, 01, 02A, and 02B may and should define the still-image result:

- camera height;
- camera-to-subject distance or shot scale;
- camera direction and angle;
- foreground/background depth;
- subject and object placement;
- crop and negative space; and
- the visible source-reference geometry that must be preserved.

They do not lock the final temporal camera move. Push, pull, orbit, track,
follow, pan, tilt, recoil, speed, envelope, start, stop, and transition motion
are authored in 03V after the actual passed Production Cut pixels of type
`official_core` or `branch` are inspected.
Upstream may state the dramatic need or rejected default, but 03V owns the
executable camera route.

## 10. 02A To 02B Prompt Translation

02B is not a schema dump and not a creative reset. It translates the selected
02A child, current-album inputs, and bound scene references into natural
generator-facing English.

02B authors five required prompt items in a fixed order, then serializes them
as five labeled lines:

```text
Identity reference rule:
Scene intent:
Visible details:
Action cue:
Avoid:
```

The first label remains stable for prompt compatibility. In the active route it
describes the current-album member visual input, or explicitly states that no
member visual is attached. Face-only remains the sole upstream facial-identity
source; this line does not create a separate downstream face-only lane.

Every active 02B packet receives a hash-bound model-authored translation. The
translation may choose clearer render wording, but it must preserve section,
core-cut and branch-cut order, visible members, action, still-camera geometry, composition,
reference order, current-album package identity, Founder locks, and expected
output path.

Before writing, the owner inspects the exact Branch Cut, every bound
current-title asset, and every selected reference derivative. The v1.5 delta
records the source hash, exact visual-binding order, current-title asset order,
authoring owner, and full component order. `Visible details` internally
separates background/space; camera height, scale/distance, direction/angle,
depth, placement, crop/negative space, and lens feel; and
light/color/material. `Action cue` separates subject state/pose, visible
action/hold, and object/environment response.

The compiler does not fill those values. It validates the receipt and adds only
fixed headings, component labels, separators, and punctuation to produce the
unchanged five render-facing lines.

Do not mechanically copy internal schema terms into the prompt. Avoid ambiguous
production words such as `landing`, `endpoint`, `final`, or `release` unless
they describe an observable physical state in that exact frame. Describe the
visible body, object, environment, light, crop, and still-camera result instead.

The prompt must include `4K`, `high quality`, and `high detail` without
replacing concrete scene direction.

## 11. Bounded 02B Context

One 02B worker owns one physical section shard. For each packet it receives:

- the compact target, message, and whole-video invariants;
- the complete current 02A branch cut and parent core cut;
- the immediately previous and next branch cut inside that parent when they
  exist;
- the immediately previous and next core cut within the same section when they
  exist;
- the current section's member and current-album asset assignments;
- the selected reference interpretations and exact child bindings; and
- hashes for every governing input.

It does not load another section's cuts, prompt packets, or raw reference pool.
At the first or last cut, the current section's own entry/exit continuity lock
is sufficient. Cross-section continuity is judged by the coordinator at the
section/whole-video review, not by expanding every packet's context.

## 12. 03V Storyboard Standard

After the typed Production Cut review passes, 03V groups one to three ordered
passed Production Cut images of type `official_core` or `branch` into one
storyboard board when they form one coherent motion cue. More than three
necessary screens become linked boards; the limit never caps a Core's total
Branch count.

A one-image board is valid when that Production Cut is a sufficient motion
anchor. It records why one anchor is enough and the temporal subject/
environment/camera route 03V will author from it; it is not restricted to a
hold or insert.

The reference binding is fixed:

```text
@image1 = storyboard board
@image2 = first original passed Production Cut image
@image3 = second original passed Production Cut image
@image4 = third original passed Production Cut image
```

Only the tags that have source frames are used. The board controls ordered
timing, blocking, subject route, and camera-motion intent. The original
Production Cut images remain the pixel, appearance, wardrobe, lighting,
composition, and visible-state authority. Storyboard labels, arrows, borders,
padding, and panel layout must never appear in the generated video.

03V authors the temporal camera route from the passed pixels: movement family,
direction, speed and envelope, motivation, start, stop, depth change, and edit
handoff. Even a one-image motion anchor uses the declared single-anchor record,
so the active handoff keeps one attachment grammar.

## 13. Choreography Reference Rule

Choreography is used only where Front Planning/01 marks it `primary` or
`supporting`. Those sections must bind an actual choreography, performance, or
fancam reference and derive ordered branch-cut body states from the inspected
movement phrase. A dance-style label or invented generic pose series is not
enough.

Sections marked `minimal_body_gaze_hold_guard` receive only the body evidence
needed for readability. Sections marked `absent` remain non-dance.

## 14. Founder Review HTML

Founder-facing review boards use one hierarchy:

1. target and message summary;
2. whole-video and section-flow summary;
3. section cards in timeline order;
4. core cuts inside each section; and
5. ordered branch cuts inside each core cut.

Every decision-bearing card names its affected section, core cut, and branch cut
scope, shows the actual references or generated pixels needed for judgment, and
supports `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `REJECT`.

The same shell serves Front Planning, 02A storyboard review, generated Branch
review, typed Production Cut review, 03V playback review, and 04 edit review.
The data depth changes; the review hierarchy and decision language do not.

## 15. Smallest-Owner Revision

A correction starts at the smallest canonical owner whose meaning changed:

| Change | Owner | Rebuild |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel defect only | Generated image correction | Exact image and pixel review |
| Final wording for the same child | 02B direct prompt revision | Exact packet, request, and image |
| One branch-cut action or still composition | 02A child | Exact child and dependent 02B/03V |
| One Official Core event or primary image | 01 Official Core | Exact Core, immutable 02A import, and affected dependents |
| One Branch roster | 02A imported Core | Exact Branch roster and affected dependents |
| One section's staging or coverage | 01 section | That section from 02 onward |
| One section's major scene, member allocation, or reference assignment | Front Planning section/01R row | That section from 01 onward |
| Target, message, whole-video spine, or current-album package | Front Planning whole-video owner | Only directly affected global and section scopes |

Every local revision binds the last passed baseline, changed field/value hashes,
affected identifiers, preserved boundary/reference order, and exact downstream
invalidations. It does not refill unaffected upstream forms or reopen other
sections.

## 16. Acceptance Criteria

The implementation is complete when:

- an unresolved brief is first developed and challenged as a complete screen
  sequence, then projected into the existing Front Planning contract without a
  new stage or fixed beat count;
- a short Founder brief, selected references, must-have/avoid, or first-proposal
  request becomes a director-ready whole-flow/reference-use start control;
- every new title creates title-specific member visuals and cannot silently
  inherit INK or another album's pixels or look; passed title visuals bind
  without repeat review and only new/restyled candidates use exception selection;
- every director-quality-passed Major has a coverage-complete, uncapped
  director-quality-passed Key Screen set, and every Key Screen binds one
  governing or primary named reference function plus only the optional support
  needed for another named screen job;
- Key Screens preserve detailed, varied camera/composition truth and do not
  collapse into repeated face-centered framing;
- 01 expands the passed sections without reference reselection, authors enough
  Official Cores to express the actual flow, and owns exactly one passed
  primary image per Core;
- 02A exact-imports every 01 Core unchanged and provides enough ordered Branch
  Cuts to judge the actual flow, with each Branch contributing distinct screen
  information;
- every active 02B prompt uses five required natural-language items, fixed
  five-line serialization, and a bounded translation from the current section
  and same-section cut neighborhood;
- every 02B packet proves exact visual inspection and directly authored
  structured Visible details/Action cue components before fixed serialization;
- only relevant current-title member and exact scene inputs reach each packet;
- no separate downstream face-only input, forced frontal gaze, portrait crop, or
  reference board layout enters the active route; the upstream identity source
  is always the approved face-only reference and never a retired `no-mic`
  variant;
- 03V authors camera motion after image completion and uses one to three passed
  `official_core|branch` Production Cut images per coherent storyboard board;
- choreography sections follow actual movement references; and
- a local correction rebuilds only affected dependents.

## 17. Prior-Run Cardinality Lesson

A prior production exposed a cardinality and expansion failure: one passed
section direction had been compressed into too few reviewable 02A states. The
reusable lesson is the ownership rule, not that run's scene, object, staging,
or composition. Front Planning binds the major direction and reference once;
01 expands the assigned sequence into observable staging, still-camera
coverage, and branching opportunities; 02A materializes every distinct screen
realization needed to judge the core event before 02B.

There is no fixed global core-cut or branch-cut quota. The selected audio,
screen events, reference grammar, and distinct screen contributions determine
cardinality.
