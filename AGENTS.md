## Production Spine

Build one coherent full selected-song MV timeline first; derive 90s+, 45s, and
short cuts only after that spine exists. PRE-00 combines audience/trend evidence,
locks one primary target and promise, and preserves the complete natural-language
MV flow as `pre00_storyline_original`. That original is the immutable whole-film
root: later reference-, song-, target-, or Founder-driven changes append a new
full-text version with parent hash, reason, changed passages, preserved invariants,
and reference-function receipts; they never overwrite or summarize away the
original. Target, audience promise, and message are PRE-FP locks, not repeated
downstream scores. One physical Founder-visible Song Room under the active CDD
route carries song, selected-audio, audio-motion, and 00A work. Front
Planning absorbs useful 00A context and owns message, title identity,
whole-video progression, reference connection, sections, and major screen jobs;
Front Planning returns exactly three complete routes `FP-A`, `FP-B`, and `FP-C`,
with ordered Section Major Stacks, `MJxx` Major ids, 2--4 complete Sequence
Composition candidates per material Major, and 2--4 actual still candidates plus
one selected exact reference binding for every material Core Slot. A section is
not capped at one event; Major count has no global cap. One event typically
leaves one or two key Cores, never a required start/middle/end set; 02A Branch
Cuts diversify each Core. A missing
Core reference may close only with a Founder-authored waiver naming the exact
Core and reason. New Sequence Units are exactly five frames at exact one-second
cadence with a contextual flow reading; older three-second units remain pinned,
read-only evidence and are never rewritten or promoted automatically. 01 owns
the Official Core Storyboard and exactly one passed primary image per Core; 02A
imports those Core bytes unchanged and owns only `1..N` ordered Branch Cuts per
Core. In Graph v2 after G-40, the Section-Cluster CDD reviews its cohort; one
`section_image_motion_worker` remains the 02B/03V output writer. 02B/image/03V
opens only for the exact approved production scope; 04 is a
separate Edit Director route; 05 packages the result. There is no active 00,
01A, or 02 creative artifact. Active reference roles are at most
`character_visual`, `scene_visual_package`, `motion_reference`, and optional
`prop_reference`. Every material Major requires real contiguous motion
evidence.

Message and target empathy progress across the whole video: a section may seed,
embody, complicate, contradict, release, resolve, echo, or withhold the
message. Do not repeat one thesis, caption, symbol, or explanatory action in
every scene. Every production-ready scene, prompt, board, or edit unit names
the primary audio/section cue, visible action or intentional hold, camera
geometry, composition, identity when visible, and continuity; abstract mood
alone is not execution truth.
