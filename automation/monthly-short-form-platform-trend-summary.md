# Monthly Short-Form Platform Trend Summary Automation Contract

## Purpose

Create one month-end HTML comparison from the weekly TikTok/Instagram short-form
reports and the month's biweekly trend pulses. The monthly summary shows which
platform behaviors emerged, repeated, saturated, faded, or diverged. It is an
archive/comparison surface, not the first current-market source for PRE-00 and
not a content-route decision.

## Required Reads

```text
AGENTS.md
wiki/production-system.md
source-materials/short-form-trend-reports/README.md
source-materials/short-form-trend-reports/weekly/latest.md
source-materials/short-form-trend-reports/monthly/TEMPLATE.html
source-materials/trend-pulse-records/latest.md
source-materials/idol-activity-reports/weekly/latest.md
```

Use local weekly reports and trend pulses first. Public web research/fetch is
allowed only for current public-source verification. The weekly contract's
no-login, no-cookie, no-personalized-feed, no-private-page, no-download, and
no-`yt-dlp` boundaries remain in force.

## Canonical Output

Create:

```text
source-materials/short-form-trend-reports/monthly/YYYY-MM-short-form-platform-trend-summary.html
```

Then update:

```text
source-materials/short-form-trend-reports/monthly/latest.md
```

## Summary Contents

- monthly conclusion and evidence coverage;
- index of weekly short-form reports;
- comparison of the month's biweekly IDOL trend pulses;
- TikTok-only, Instagram-only, and cross-platform repeated signals;
- hook, audio, edit, camera/crop, text/caption, participation, and replay
  pattern changes;
- trend lifecycle transitions: emerging -> growing -> mature -> saturated ->
  fading/reappearing;
- cross-check against weekly idol activity and current K-pop release behavior;
- signals that were promoted to a pulse, held, rejected, or later contradicted;
- AURORA-M/Loom transferable functions and source-application scopes; and
- next-month verification questions.

## Boundaries

The monthly summary cannot override the active biweekly trend pulse, Founder
locks, selected audio, current verified run research, Front Planning target
analysis, MV/CF direction, or publishing approval. It must not turn accumulated
examples into a durable creative default without repeated evidence and Founder
review.
