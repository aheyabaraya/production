### Cut Reference Role Groups

A Cut may bind at most four logical reference role groups:

1. `character_visual`;
2. `scene_visual_package`;
3. `motion_reference`;
4. optional `prop_reference`.

Graph checkpoints use the bound contextual-judgment profile before a
candidate, Decision Set, pixel result, or playback may return for Director
review. Each 0--4 row carries exact evidence and prose consequences rather than
a total score. Applied criteria require at least 3; motion and still

or observable realization. Graph v2 authors only

`composition_anchor` (still-camera height, distance, direction, angle, depth,
crop, negative space, foreground/occlusion), then compiles them into one Cut
input bundle. Still image versus video is a source media type; background,
light, composition, and motion are source functions and are not collapsed into
one axis. Wardrobe lives in `character_visual`. Performance is not a fifth
role; it is an optional `motion_reference` evaluation module when actual
performance exists.

One source may satisfy several roles. Register the bytes once and bind multiple
roles; do not duplicate attachment. A Pinterest still cannot substitute for
`motion_reference`. Video evidence may supply motion and a still/background
frame when the selected locators prove both functions.

## Audience And Trend Foundation To Front Planning

For new runs, PRE-00 begins from audience evidence and current trend evidence,
then tests:

- Founder reference audiences;
- reference artists, songs, worlds, and IP;
- genre/subculture and visual-world anchors;
- platform behavior;
- the standing global K-pop baseline;
- relevant local run evidence.

For each anchor, record what it attracts, what identity or reward it promises,
codes and literacy, participation behavior, expectations, rejection triggers,
evidence strength, freshness, and counterevidence.

For reference-led MV work, this anchor set has a strict precedence rule. The

the standing Loom/K-pop baseline, or a visually adjacent scene cannot silently

`verified_current_run`, `working_hypothesis`, or `research_gap`. Only
`verified_current_run` may close as an adopted base for a Founder-selected
radar.

Every proposed visual scene, world, performance, or campaign reference also
receives a source-bound audience profile before selection. Compare it with the
main-song base across five dimensions: viewer situation, desired reward,
literacy/codes, platform behavior, and rejection triggers. Close the scene
anchor as `aligned_narrowing`, `bounded_acquisition_bridge`,
`surface_only_test`, or `rejected`. The final title target is the supported
intersection, not the union of fandoms. A scene anchor may narrow the base or
provide a bounded entry bridge; it may not silently widen, replace, or
contradict the base. Missing evidence, an empty intersection, or an unresolved
conflict returns HOLD and requires better evidence, a different scene
reference, or an explicit Founder reopen of the main target.

The durable Loom brand baseline supplies context and comparison only. It is not
overwritten by one title's transplanted target, and a title target does not
become permanent group truth automatically. Founder-private reusable
pre-analysis may reduce repeated research, but each current run must bind its

profile. This task's approved bridge is limited to the reference-audience
method and statused profiles; it does not export unrelated business strategy or
grant external research, capture, generation, publishing, or release authority.

Draft two to four possible audience connections and type each as:

- actual audience overlap;
- shared-reward adjacency;
- acquisition bridge;
- surface-only similarity.

Validate target -> response mechanism -> expected song and screen reward before
the Founder locks one primary audience. Do not union every fandom or promote
surface similarity into a target without evidence. Record the audience promise,

Review identity is role + exact input/result digest + Founder decision epoch,
not session or model identity. A valid same-role review receipt carries forward
while those bytes and the owning scope remain unchanged. Re-review is legal only
for changed result bytes, changed governing input or Founder epoch, incomplete
evidence, a concrete conflict, owner/scope change, or explicit Founder
instruction. A new session, provider, or model alone cannot trigger another
semantic review. Overall judges the CDD-curated milestone and whole-work

closed by CDD without one of those concrete triggers.

Overall, CDD, and workers use event waits rather than status polling. A child
wakes its declared recipient only for `final`, `hold`, `authority_request`, or
`milestone`; the compact payload contains exact output/aggregate-manifest,
rejection-or-hold, material-risk, and unresolved-decision pointers plus hashes,
never raw commentary or a transcript. Overall sleeps after CDD launch and wakes
for a reviewed milestone or authority request; CDD sleeps after dispatch and
wakes for the bounded return. A worker `returned` event still requires its
declared recipient Director's creative review, and Director review still
requires Overall promotion.

`final` and `hold` enter terminal return/review closure. `authority_request`
and `milestone` preserve the same immutable attempt and cannot authorize a
successor. Before any later attempt launches, its canonical
`previous_attempt_id` must resolve inside the same run and prove the predecessor
closed runtime return, recipient-Director review, and Overall `promoted` or
`hold` disposition. Normal checkpoint continuation preserves the same-lineage
owner, full scope, parent boundaries, and consecutive `attempt_number`. A
targeted correction preserves the same owner, governing stage/section scope,
and parent boundaries but replaces the artifact-path scope with exactly the
predecessor correction's `invalidated_outputs`. `same_session_new_attempt`
preserves the exact session; `new_session_new_lineage` starts attempt one in a
fresh lineage while retaining the same review-cycle counter; `upstream_owner`
and `hold` forbid a direct child successor.

One review cycle permits at most three total submissions: the initial return is
submission 1, followed by at most two targeted corrections. This counter is
separate from `attempt_number`, so legal stage/checkpoint progression is not
capped. On every non-accept disposition, the recipient Director records the
complete observed problem set, exact invalidated output hashes, one bounded
`correction_action`, and explicit acceptance criteria in one correction unit.
The next worker Goal exact-copies that action, works only on those invalidated
paths, returns changed bytes with predecessor-hash lineage, and hash-freezes all
unaffected outputs. Submission 2 or 3 reopens the same section-worker line or
session that authored those paths; do not reassign the whole package to a new
random worker. Unaffected worker results stay hash-frozen. If only one of ten
cuts fails, the correction Goal and output scope contain that one cut; the
other nine are neither reopened nor regenerated. Submission 3 may pass, but if it does not pass it must route to the
upstream owner or HOLD; no fourth child submission is legal. A new session,
provider, model, or Founder epoch does not reset the counter for the same work
package. A genuinely new upstream assignment may start a new review cycle only
after its prior terminal route closes and its owner or governing scope
materially changes.
