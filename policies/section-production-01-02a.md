## 01 Direction Expansion

01 resumes the promoted development-block checkpoints from the existing Section
Dispatch Map; it does not launch another general research wave. A block owns one
section by default or two to three connected sections and still returns one
separate result per section. It consumes the selected integrated-reference and
current-title identity evidence, inspects every bound reference, and may perform
only the smallest named-gap/challenge search needed to reach professional
decision readiness under its assigned claim. Candidate and source counts are
adaptive evidence needs, never completion quotas.

01 expands each passed Front Planning section without reselecting its target,
message, members, title package, or reference grammar. Each active section
receives:

- an exact projection of the complete PRE-00-rooted full-storyline lineage,
  including original, active full text, revisions, density review, and
  reference receipts;
- exact Front Planning projection and source hashes;
- dramatic and screen job;
- observable action, hold, reaction, space, object/system, and relation logic;
- performer staging and gaze only when visible;
- still-camera geometry and composition alternatives appropriate to that
  section;
- motion-ready still requirements when video is plausible: action axis and
  screen direction, lead/travel/reveal room, depth layers, path/occlusion,
  crop/edge safety, and the continuity carrier, without naming an executable
  temporal move;
- a branching strategy for each major event, naming materially different
  screen contributions 02A may realize without turning one action into a
  start/end worksheet;
- sequence/scene-reference application;
- choreography source binding when `primary` or `supporting`;
- transition into and out of the section;
- concrete risks, rejected defaults, and continuity constraints.
- every Official Core Cut for the section, each bound to one material Major,
  the selected Sequence flow of that Major, one exact governing reference or
  Founder-authored named waiver, and exactly one passed primary image. A Core
  is a must-remain still marked on that selected Sequence, or a better still of
  the same moment, not a Sequence-frame dump. One event typically leaves one or
  two key Cores. Do not author a start / middle / end Core set. A new governing
  event is a new Major. A third Core on the same event is forbidden; route that
  need upstream as another Major. Section Major count and Core count have no
  global cap; richness comes from many events, then Branch diversification of
  each Core;
- the Core camera/composition/depth/motion-readiness progression and the named
  Branch expansion needs that 02A may address without changing Core meaning.

01 may strengthen a weak route, but a change to target, message, member
allocation, current-title member visuals, or governing reference returns to Front

## 02A Branch Coverage

02A owns the Official Branch Plan beneath the imported 01 Core Storyboard. It
must exact-carry each Core id, image path/SHA, reference binding or exact Founder
waiver, active storyline version, screen-progression node, Section/Major
placement, and camera/motion-readiness boundaries. It cannot edit, replace, or
silently regenerate a Core; a Core meaning/image change routes to 01 or Front
Planning. 02A exact-carries the Founder kinetic start as named transition
tendency, camera energy, and hold reason plus in/out. The named 2--3 second
tendency is a reference- or song-led creative range, not a global minimum
and not a Core or Branch quota. A 0.5-second passage is an on-screen /
final-edit example; it is not a sampling, generation-request, or
transition-effect example. Final-edit segments remain positive and
frame-aligned. Intentional longer holds are allowed. Source-reference
sampling, requested generation duration, and transition-effect duration
keep their own existing contracts; this paragraph does not copy those
numeric enums into a new timing policy.

A core cut is a must-remain still of one Major event, action, effect, condition,
or dramatic beat inside a section. It may span several independently framed
screens and is not required to equal one final editorial cut or one still-camera
setup. Start a new Major when the governing event changes, not merely because
the view changes. Sequence phases of one event, including start / middle / end,
are not separate Cores. One event typically leaves one or two key Cores. Branch
Cuts diversify each Core. 01 exact-carries
the Front Planning kinetic start as named transition tendency, camera energy,
and hold reason plus in/out, without a 2--3 second numeric quota, global
minimum, or Core/Branch count rule. Use the 02A timing rule above for the
on-screen / final-edit 0.5-second example and longer intentional holds.

Each core cut owns `1..N` ordered branch cuts. A branch cut is one independently
framed and generatable screen realization derived from the parent event. It
must add observable screen information or a distinct directorial job: for
example a new event phase, viewpoint, participant response, object or spatial
consequence, insert, aftermath, reprise, or transition. Those are examples, not
a required taxonomy. A start/end label, camera-size change, or repeated action
does not earn a branch cut unless the resulting screen contributes something
the neighboring branch does not. Angle diversity is additional, never a
substitute for that distinct-screen job: a new Branch Cut still needs a
distinct screen job. Distinct generated production images collected on the planning/production
storyboard (every Official Core primary and generated Branch still, not
only a later 03V 1--3 packet) must not share a camera angle. Camera angle
is facing-and-pitch (`direction_angle`); it is not shot size and not
height-only. Same image exact-carry is not a new image. A Branch that
restates the Core still as a new generatable image with the same angle
cannot close the full-storyboard camera-angle review as PASS unless an
exact-bound Founder lock names those images and the review remains HOLD.
The model authors the full-collection pairwise visual review;
`camera_diversity_review.collected_image_camera_angle_review` exact-binds
each still's geometry. Scripts check completeness and bindings only.

Every imported Core Cut states:

- section and audio range;
- the governing event and dramatic job;
- space, subjects, visible members, object/system, action or hold;
- what must remain true across all branches;
- the camera's governing position in the space, action axis/screen direction,
  and anchor-view height, distance, direction, angle, depth, subject-space
  composition, crop, negative space, and lens feel, plus any motion-readiness
  conditions the Branch Cuts must preserve;
- the ordered camera role of every Branch Cut plus protected continuity,
  allowed variation, and forbidden collapse;
- governing reference and choreography bindings;
- entry and exit continuity;
- why its branch set belongs to one event rather than separate core cuts.

The Core Cut camera anchor is a spatial coordinate, not a mandatory setup or
default frame. Branch Cuts retain broad creative freedom to change height,
distance, direction, angle, axis, depth, placement, crop, lens, and viewpoint
when each change reveals new information and preserves continuity or declares
an intentional reset. There is no closed shot taxonomy, camera-variation
quota, or requirement that Branch Cuts resemble the anchor. The exact Branch
Cut still-camera specification remains authoritative.

Every branch cut states:

- how it derives from the parent event;
- the distinct screen information or directorial contribution it adds;
- the exact visible state and subject/body/object/environment action or hold;
- still-camera height, distance/scale, direction/angle, and foreground/
  background depth;
- subject placement, crop, negative space, gaze when visible, light, material,
  and wardrobe consequence;
- selected current-title member and scene-reference inputs, including
  text/reference-bound space direction;
- expected output path;
- relation to adjacent branch cuts and its continuity or edit use;
- neighbor screen difference in event, axis, distance, and crop versus the named
  neighbor. Matching values are the same shot's variant, not a transition, and
  hold. Source bindings and selected locks arrive at 02B unchanged. An assigned
  human or model may author the five prompt lines in wording that preserves
  that source meaning and the required visible detail. Open prose never
  relaxes locked numeric or physical facts. The compiler
  exact-copies those already-authored components and never authors or repairs
  meaning.

When later video motion is plausible, the Branch Cut's existing still-camera,
composition, depth, continuity, and edit-use prose states the applicable
action axis/screen direction, lead/travel/reveal room, separated or deliberately
flat depth layers, physically legible path/occlusion, crop/edge safety, and
reveal or continuity anchor. These are visible still-frame prerequisites and
feasibility constraints, not a temporal movement instruction.

02A must be detailed enough for G-40 exact production-scope authorization after
the Branch plan and Draft Keyframe Proof exist. It does not author temporal
camera movement; it authors each Branch still view that 03V may later start
from.

Draft Keyframe Proof is an internal 02A review checkpoint, not a new canonical
stage. It does not change G-30 Hero proof ownership or meaning. G-30
representative/risk Hero outputs remain Front Planning visual proof. This
checkpoint uses the 01 Core anchors and the 02A Branch/reference bindings to
make a whole-video still preview before final 02B.

Reuse existing `fp_hero_core` as the noncanonical packet mode. Do not add
`02a_draft_keyframe` in this slice; no owned producer/consumer has proven a
distinct mode is required. The checkpoint, owner, and promotion barrier
distinguish these pixels from G-30 Hero proof and from Official Core or
Official Branch Production Cuts. Draft Keyframe pixels:

- are noncanonical preview stills;
- cannot promote into `official_core` or `official_branch`;
- are not 03V sources;
- generate only inside the exact approved scope and budget;
- become Founder G2 input for the 02B execution envelope.

P3-Ci authors the cohort-scoped requirements. 02A executes the proof. CDD
reviews actual pixels. Overall reviews the whole-video anti-flat result.
Founder G2 decides section/card PASS, HOLD, or REJECT, plus any exact card
correction or whole-route change. That PASS is designed to authorize only the
named section/Cut, provider, attachment group, output count, and budget. A
wider envelope is a separate authority request.

### Internal Close Before Founder G2

CDD closes these defects before Founder projection:

- repeated angle, distance, or composition as a default;
- a pose that does not read as a motion anchor;
- collage rather than combined reference transfer;
- background, light, or camera that kills the character;
- source-identity copy or current-title identity drift;
- optional-prop scale or contact error;
- missing Major/Cut change needed to carry the named 2--3 second transition
  tendency as enough screen-change energy or a named hold, not as a 2--3
  second shot floor. Missing screen-change energy or an unjustified hold is
  judged against the selected audio and reference. A fast editorial cut,
  including a 0.5-second on-screen / final-edit passage, is not this defect
  merely because it is shorter than 2--3 seconds. Do not require tempo
  rotation or a count-by-seconds quota. Do not treat Sequence sample
  spacing, a generated-clip request, or a transition-effect length as the
  final edit duration.

## Contextual 0--4 Draft Keyframe Review

Assigned Directors grade actual draft pixels and named neighbors. Scripts
never author a grade, average, total, or creative PASS. Grade 4 is admission
eligibility, not automatic PASS.

Draft Keyframe criteria:

1. Cut screen job and neighbor delta;
2. `character_visual`, only when a person is visible;
3. `scene_visual_package`, with separately judged space/light/material and
   still camera/composition;
4. action/pose and motion readiness;
5. angle, distance, depth, crop, and transition diversity;
6. reference-transfer fidelity and generation feasibility;
7. optional-prop interaction readability when a material prop exists.

Admission to Founder G2 requires every applicable item at least 3,
`scene_visual_package` still-camera/composition 4, and action/pose/motion
readiness 4, plus CDD pixel review and Overall whole-video review. A
character-absent Cut uses
`not_applicable_member_absent`. A Cut with no material prop uses
`not_applicable_no_material_prop`. Do not invent dummy references to fill
N/A. Performance readability is graded only when `performance_mode` is
`supporting` or `primary`. Target empathy and message remain PRE-FP locks;
this checkpoint asks only whether the lock is violated or unrealized on
screen.
