## 04 Edit Units And Production-Cut Lineage

04 does not inherit the word `cut` as a one-to-one timeline instruction.

- Official Core remains the 01-owned parent event, exact-carried through 02A and the
  downstream media chain without 02A reauthoring its meaning or primary image.
- Branch Cut remains an upstream framed screen realization carried through its
  passed image and 03V cue.
- Edit Slot is one planned position in `idol_edit_marker_plan.v6` with exact
  screen-progression and Production-Cut lineage.
- Edit Segment is one selected source range in `idol_edit_recipe.v3` that
  exact-carries the marker-plan screen-progression node.
- Edit Boundary or cutpoint is the exact join between Edit Segments.
- Video frame is a real timebase sample for timing and QC. Animation keyframe
  remains valid only for an actual animated parameter.

Every active v6 plan first exact-projects the complete PRE-00-rooted
full-storyline lineage and active `screen_progression_map` from one declared
03V v8 manifest. Every Edit Slot then
binds the exact passed Audio Production-Cut Placement, 03V manifest/playback
sources, and its 03V cue/Official-Core/Branch Production-Cut ids. A
current-run non-Production-Cut source may enter only with the Branch Cut whose
continuity job it replaces. One Branch Cut may support several Edit Segments;
several Branch Cuts may combine into one Edit Segment; a passed Branch Cut may
be omitted when actual playback, selected audio, story flow, and continuity
justify the decision. Neither scripts nor upstream timing may force a
one-Branch-Cut-one-Edit-Segment assembly.
