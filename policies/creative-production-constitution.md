## Production Principle

Creative fields are human/model directorial decisions. A script may carry one
only as a hash-bound exact copy with source path, JSON pointer, source-value
SHA-256, and human/model authorship receipt. A script must hold when the source
value is absent, stale, or fails integrity (missing path, pointer, hash, or
authorship receipt). Generic or too-thin destination evidence is a
human/model and recipient-Director judgment on existing authorship and review
receipts, not a script semantic score. Schema completion,
same-value fanout, placeholder prose, and script-claimed `model_authored`
provenance remain forbidden. Contextual creative review, not schema success,
decides whether the authored value is good enough.

Use one complete full-song storyline and screen graph. After that graph and its
three complete FP routes exist, the Founder may authorize a coherent teaser
subgraph for physical production before the held full MV. A teaser PASS never
implies full-MV PASS, and a full-MV HOLD never deletes a passed teaser. Short-
form hook quotas do not govern Front Planning, Major Scenes, Core Cuts, or
Branch Cuts:

```text
One strong song-backed creative spine lays the full selected song/audio timeline first,
builds one coherent cinematic MV graph across that whole timeline, then derives
the exact authorized physical teaser/full-MV scope without losing or duplicating
story, reference, Cut, path, hash, or pixel lineage.
```

The active creative spine is split deliberately: PRE-00 owns the
Founder-locked primary target, audience promise, target content contract,
current trend synthesis, and the complete natural-language opening-to-ending
MV storyline original. The original excludes timecodes, camera geometry,
Core/Branch Cuts, and prompts, but it is still the governing whole-film flow.
It is immutable. Later evidence may extend or revise it only by appending a new
complete full-text version with the preceding version id/hash, explicit change
reason, affected passages, preserved invariants, and reference-function and
exclusion receipts. 00A owns song constraints; and Front Planning exact-projects
the entire storyline lineage and target while owning message, current-title
visual identity, reference connection, whole-video execution progression,
members, and ordered Section Major Stacks. Its `screen_progression_map` is the
typed projection from the active storyline through Section/Major/Sequence/Core/
Branch nodes and is exact-carried through 04; it is not a second storyline.
`whole_film_progression`, `whole_film_spine`, section summaries, and
alignment readouts help navigate or execute the full text; none replaces it.
Together they keep choreography, performance direction, MV direction,
generation coverage, derivative planning, and Founder locks aligned before
generation or editing expands the work.

This target/trend-first order governs new runs. A run already beyond that
boundary retains its accepted target baseline unless the Founder explicitly
reopens it; the routing change does not invalidate active Front Planning by
itself.

Director topology is selected once per run. Existing runs and new runs before a
verified Graph default-switch receipt remain legacy-pinned. They use the single
durable CDD flow in the next paragraph. A new run admitted after that receipt
uses the mutually exclusive Graph v2 topology: one Overall; singleton P1/P2;
the minimum roster-derived number of P3-Ci CDDs covering contiguous
2--3-section cohorts; matching V-Ci cohort CDDs after G-40; section-local
image/motion writers; and one Edit Director. Each Graph CDD owns a disjoint
Decision Set, returns through typed review edges, and has at most one physical
primary plus one successor under CAS. No Graph CDD becomes a second
Founder-conversation or promotion authority, and a run never has legacy and
Graph active writers together.

In the legacy-pinned branch, the Founder starts with one short direction, selected song or references, one
must-have or avoid, or a request for the first proposal. The Overall Director
records the exact input, creates the initial Founder Kernel candidate without
turning a preference into an automatic lock, and immediately opens one separate
durable Creative Development Director room. That room develops the
recommendation, directs only the necessary bounded departments and development
blocks, reviews their source-backed Decision Sets, actual Hero/Core/Branch
pixels, Motion Storyboards, and 03V playback, and reports upward. Overall is the
only Founder-conversation owner. The Overall Director inspects
the surviving media and source-to-screen deltas, then alone promotes the
recommendation, requests Founder decisions, and owns final creative acceptance.
The Creative Development Director is not a competing authority, and no standing
Final Director exists.

Existing passed current-title member visuals bind without repeated approval.
Only a new or fully restyled member visual, a material departure from the
aligned direction, or a concrete identity, safety, source, or feasibility
conflict opens an earlier Founder exception choice. The stable Workbench
advances G-00/G-10/G-20 preparation, then G-30 compares exactly three complete
routes `FP-A/B/C`, ordered Major Stacks, 2--4 actual Sequence compositions and
Core visual candidates, exact Core-reference coverage, and actual
representative/risk Hero outputs. Reference coverage and Hero-generation
coverage are separate; either missing holds. 01 then owns each Official Core
and its one passed primary image. G-40 presents those immutable Cores plus the
02A Branch-only plan and authorizes only the exact derivative/production scope.
G-50 judges actual generated Cut pixels; paid execution authorization remains
separate. Front Planning creates
no separate background/world asset package;
space/background remains observable scene text and exact scene-reference truth.
Key Screen count is evidence-led and uncapped.
Message delivery is a whole-video progression, not a per-scene slogan contract.
Do not require every section, core cut, or branch cut to restate the central message or
target empathy. Selected anchors may seed, embody, complicate, contradict,
reframe, resolve, or echo it; other scenes may build character, relation, world,
tension, performance release, transition, negative space, or afterimage. Each
scene needs a dominant screen job and concrete audio/action/camera/continuity
truth, while the complete sequence carries the full message and empathy arc.
After the PRE-00 Founder lock, target empathy / audience identification is the first
creative judgment: define how the target viewer can receive the message as
their own feeling, situation, desire, or self-image.
The default IDOL audience baseline starts from global K-pop fandom: people who
already like idols and idol-group culture, with global fan behavior as the first
market literacy. This is an audience lock, not a stage/fancam format lock. The
planning baseline is the full selected-song MV timeline. Choreography,
performance, and member identity enter only where they serve the song, message,
section, and visual world. Any later short-form unit or 90s/45s output is derived
from that coherent timeline and never creates a planning quota or required
field. Read
`source-materials/audience-research/global-kpop-fandom-target-v1.md` before
PRE-00, new song brainstorming, or Front Planning unless the founder
locks a narrower target for the run.

For song-led starts, founder-provided reference songs or timestamps are the
preferred sound anchor. The assigned registered model may use them as creative references directly:
read function, structure, mood, energy, sonic grammar, meaning delivery, and
reference feel before recommending the active unit that can own that sound or
writing Suno prompts. Meaning delivery means how the reference song makes its
message, hook, emotional stance, or listener identification clear through lyric
placement, repetition, vocal emphasis, section order, silence, contrast, and
release. When the founder chooses a reference as the standard, preserve the
specific delivery method and carry it directly into the current run's song or
screen plan, naming what is being followed and why.
Abstract style-only prompting is not the first move when the founder is still
defining the song feel.

### Selected Reference Direct-Follow Preservation

Every Founder-supplied reference is active current-run input until it is
dispositioned. Inspect it before supplemental public research and record it as
`applied`, `combined`, `held`, or `rejected`, naming its exact function or
followed surface, target song section or decision, and reason. Never silently
omit it, replace it with generic house style, or reduce it to a loose vibe.
Default to strong use of every usable selected surface while recomposing it for
the current song, members, and complete-video timeline. This includes the selected
source's actual camera, framing, subject placement, crop, negative space, and
composition whenever those are the chosen follow surfaces. A reference is not an
unquestionable lock, and irrelevant or harmful source details do not need to be
copied when their disposition and stronger alternative are explicit.

For an `applied` or `combined` Founder visual, the application itself is always
direct: select a bounded frame/crop, full scene, or ordered sequence and carry
its canonical path, SHA-256, preserve/discard locks, and target scope through
01, 02A, and 02B. A named function explains why that exact visual is used; it
is never a separate function-only production route. If no usable direct visual
surface is selected, keep the item as upstream response/context evidence or
hold it rather than letting it direct a parent cut or prompt by prose alone.

Founder-selected or current-run external web research references must preserve
source identity and creative function together. Keep titles, artist names, MV
names, ads, games, venues, IP shorthand, brand names, URLs, or local source paths
in source refs and operator-facing notes so the origin is auditable. When a
reference affects Front Planning, `01`, `02A`, `02B`, review copy, image prompts, or video
prompts, keep the source name visible and state the exact followed element:
quality bar, shot function, world rule, camera route, object behavior, section
turn, edit logic, styling rule, emotion delivery, wardrobe logic, or full scene
grammar. Record this as a source id, selected surface or interval, preserved
directorial function, affected section, and current-member/song substitutions;
when teaching this operation, use the non-prescriptive example pattern below
rather than a ready-to-copy scene sentence.
Founder-selected references are allowed to pull the run's world, shot grammar,
styling, or edit behavior when that is the creative decision; the graph-engineering runtime
should record the source identity and the followed surface clearly instead of
suppressing it. A lower document that appears to describe source proximity is a
reference-following or mismatch note, not a ban on using the reference.
Current-title member visuals remain appearance continuity inputs; they do not
override the selected reference's action, staging, still-camera geometry,
placement, or crop.
No general anti-copy rule may reject a selected reference's composition, camera
geometry, framing, crop, subject placement, body route, or ordered scene
grammar. Those are direct-follow surfaces when selected. Only an explicit
Founder replacement/discard lock or a concrete identity, safety, or locked
output-format conflict may exclude one of them.
The reusable first semantic reading of a registered source must be receipted on
its source-analysis row. A current run binds that analysis revision to its own
selected locator, disposition, and use on the run-owned reference row. Later
stages consume that bound interpretation rather than asking a
new model or session to discover a different function. Targeted pixel checking
may verify a named crop, composition, material, or identity detail without
changing the receipt; any changed meaning must use an allowed reopen reason,
new revision, and downstream invalidation. The actual image remains the
provenance and generation-attachment source, while a contact-sheet handoff uses
only the selected cell/crop.
Automation must verify the local source-analysis registry and generated
`READ-FIRST.md` before a registered-video binding enters Front Planning.
Missing local source evidence, a stale analysis hash, an incomplete row, or a
run-owned substitute is a hold rather than permission to continue.
Historical `CF20` is frozen archive-only compatibility naming and is not an
active ordinary-MV entry.
The first semantic read is source-pixel observation shaped by media kind (still
versus ordered multi-frame sheet), not final generation text. Mood-only,
function-only, top-label-only, or whole-sheet mood answers are incomplete.
Exact still and sheet observation trees, nested leaves, and good/bad examples
are taught only in
`skills/idol-front-planning-director/references/director-operating-contract.md`
(top-level H2 `## 02B-Shaped Source Observation`); policies do not restate those
teaching trees.
Structured fields are evidence containers, not a scene formula. Reference
following must preserve the selected grammar and quality bar while the director
adds the current-song timing, member identity, adjacent-section continuity, and
ending consequence. When the Founder selects a reference for `full_scene_direct`
or `sequence_direct` use, preserve the source's visible action type, collision,
weapon or prop, magic or world effect, body route, expression/gaze, camera
geometry, composition, and ordered scene grammar unless the Founder explicitly
names an element to replace. In that route, reducing the source to an abstract
function or replacing its event with a safer metaphor is a contract failure.
When only a bounded surface is selected, follow that surface rather than replaying
unselected source content. Member identity, selected-song timing, safety, and
explicit replace/discard locks remain governing constraints.

A camera-only reference-direction assignment is conditional and named. It does
not silently reopen every current-title bound reference, and it does not require
changing every camera. Preserve the current-song role, the original event,
action, and emotion, and explicit locked geometry unless the exact assignment
names an authorized refinement. 01 and 02A own still distance, angle, and
framing only. After existing passed-pixel gates, 03V owns temporal camera
movement, push-in, and speed. New camera variation alone never earns a new
production cut.

An authorized still-camera or 03V refinement on the same cut must keep the
original contact and emotional relationship readable. Hiding that contact or
changing the perceived relationship is a revision of that camera, even if the
new frame looks more dramatic. Optional noncanonical camera-only teaching
examples, excluded from runtime packs and generation, live in
`policies/examples/reference-method-examples.md`.

### Current-Run Creative Source Isolation

Past-run artifacts, test fixtures, maintenance examples, and historical prompt
or scene prose are not creative inputs for a new or resumed run. A prior run
may contribute a named failure mode, comparison question, or quality invariant
only after its distinctive nouns, actions, staging, camera route, composition,
and prompt wording have been removed. Shared instructions may define decision
axes, evidence requirements, transport structure, and stop conditions; they
must not contain a complete scene or prompt sentence offered as the default way
to execute those axes.

#### Non-Prescriptive Teaching Example Contract

Excluding shared examples from execution context does not mean deleting them
from skills. A complex creative skill must keep enough examples to show how to
apply its method. These are instructional patterns, not current-run creative
truth, and they cannot supply a scene value, prompt value, reference binding,
or generation input.

A compliant teaching example:

- starts from an abstract Founder note or variable-bearing current-run input;
- shows the decision axes and more than one materially different carrier or
  route when explaining directing, camera, motion, or edit judgment;
- makes the current song, section job, selected reference, title assets, or
  passed pixels decide which route wins;
- ends with a role-labelled or placeholder output pattern rather than a
  complete reusable scene or prompt;
- states that it is noncanonical and must not be copied into a run.

The worked 01 decision-path example is owned once by
[`idol-mv-director`](../skills/idol-mv-director/SKILL.md#non-prescriptive-teaching-example).
It compares materially different dramatic carriers and still-camera functions
before the Director authors the current section. Keep the example there;
this policy owns the requirements above. A runtime projection need not load
that teaching example to apply those requirements.

Likewise, `make it more dynamic` does not imply one camera move. After passed
pixels exist, 03V may test whether the subject, camera, environment, or an
intentional hold carries the change, then author the exact direction, speed,
envelope, depth change, and stop from the current audio and images. No route in
this list is a default or quota.

Admit creative bytes only from the hash-bound current run, its current-title
member visuals, or selected source material that the current run has explicitly
dispositioned. Another run's file must first enter the current run through a
source-preserving intake and receive a current function; a repository path,
similar theme, prior success, test convenience, or maintenance history is never
enough. Context builders and execution request builders must reject test paths,
maintenance surfaces, historical prose, and un-intaken files from another run.

Reference expansion must solve named screen gaps without flattening the video.
For story-led or previously uniform work, inspect the selected pool across story
situation, physical space/background, visible subject relation, body/action
family, camera geometry/route, object/world system, and light/material behavior.
A popular candidate does not count when it repeats the same room, similar people,
similar action, and similar camera answer without a new section function. Seek a
minimum sufficient diverse set and preserve one coherent target/message/world
progression across that variety. For new or materially resumed 01R, sufficiency
is a contextual CDD judgment against named screen gaps, source-to-screen
difference, and actual reviewable visual evidence; a fixed unit,
source-identity, function, worker, or room count is never a discovery or
application quota. The exact-five one-second Sequence Unit, Core reference
coverage, Key Screen function-combination, and Pinterest FP-Close obligations
remain separate named evidence contracts. Within the compared set, every unit closes as
`applied`, `combined`, `held`, or `rejected` with a reason. A whole contact
sheet is not a unit; one
hash-bound selected cell/crop is one unit, and several cells that describe one
contiguous start-through-end event remain one sequence unit. Add or stop only

### Concrete Review And Prompt Language

Founder-facing review units, 02A storyboard text, 02B prompt packets, and
post-review video prompts must be grounded in observable screen language. Do
not leave internal analysis terms, undefined music/film jargon, or
impressive-sounding mood phrases as the only thing the founder or generator must
interpret.

Directorial explanation and render/contract prose have different jobs:

- Founder-facing directorial prose may open naturally with feeling, relationship,
  dramatic purpose, or a question of taste. Pair it in the same review unit with
  a separate screen-proof sentence or a clear pointer to the canonical fields;
  do not force every conversational sentence to carry the full technical read.
- 02A/02B/03V prompt prose and creative handoff fields are execution surfaces.
  They must carry the observable detail needed to make or continue the work.
- Automation status reports state, provenance, and gates. It must remain clear,
  but it is not a substitute for, or a compressed version of, creative prose.

For this rule, creative operator-facing fields include Cursor/Grok handoff text
and any field prose another agent must use to continue a creative decision.

When a selected subject, space, or object relation is the screen job, prove it
once through existing fields: the relation; the visible state change or
intentional hold that makes it read; the still-camera consequence that keeps
that relation on screen; and the adjacent-cut continuity or declared reset.
Place each fact in the field that already owns it. Adjacent-cut continuity
belongs in its existing continuity and review fields. Only a currently
visible continuity carrier belongs in the still prompt; do not author
future or next-shot narrative there. This is a review method, not
a new field, score, stock gesture, or a mandatory extra sentence on every
field. Music-cue and lyric-meaning translation stay on their existing teaching
patterns; do not duplicate them here.

#### Natural Language, Creative Registers, And Prompt Translation

Apply the language standard across Korean, English, and other working
languages. Conversation, explanation, analysis, review, and Founder-facing
prose should sound like language a fluent speaker would plausibly use in the
relevant conversation, writers' room, music session, or directing meeting.
Prefer clear subjects, ordinary verbs, and direct causal sentences over
habitual literal translation, inflated critical language, noun-heavy
abstraction, or decorative personification. An artistic idea may be complex;
its explanation should not sound obscure merely to sound cinematic.

Keep the creative and execution registers distinct:

- Lyrics, titles, hooks, slogans, and authored creative text may be abstract,
  metaphorical, ambiguous, fragmented, contradictory, surreal, repetitive, or
  driven by sound and image rather than literal explanation. Judge them by
  intention, voice, sound, singability, internal imagery, emotional effect,
  progression, and memory. Do not rewrite them into plain screen directions.
- Story concepts may be poetic, associative, nonlinear, unresolved, or
  intentionally withholding. A production handoff needs the state, event or
  intentional hold, transition, and consequence required to continue the work,
  but it must not force every story into literal cause-and-effect or explain
  away a selected ambiguity.
- Image, video, storyboard, and edit prompts may retain a short abstract intent,
  but must pair it with the visible conditions the surface can execute. For
  example, "the subject feels spatial pressure" is not sufficient by itself;
  name the wall or object spacing, subject placement, foreground occlusion,
  depth, lens or shot scale, crop, light boundary, and movement or hold that
  make the pressure visible.
- Music and style prompts may use an abstract feeling when it is useful, but
  pair it with the audible behavior that should produce it: rhythm, density,
  register, harmony, vocal distance, texture, silence, transition, or dynamic
  change. A lyric-writing prompt may deliberately request abstraction or
  ambiguity and does not need to convert each lyric image into a literal event.

Do not mechanically blacklist abstract words. Revise them when they conceal a
missing decision, create unnatural phrasing in the active language, could be
copied unchanged into unrelated work, or add an appearance of depth without
adding meaning. When translating a lyric, metaphor, or poetic story into
production direction, preserve the source meaning and add only the execution
information needed by that downstream surface. Schema and enum identifiers may
retain compact transport shorthand. Treat those tokens as transport labels only;
Founder-facing prose and generator prompts must restate the actual audible or
visible behavior in natural language.

### Contextual Creative Review Authority

Story, directing, planning, camera progression, Core-Cut/Branch-Cut event ownership,
reference application, repetition, reprise, visual richness, and plausibility
are contextual creative judgments. A deterministic rule may validate schema,
types, enums, ids, hashes, exact-copy transfer, source coverage, output
ownership, and approval state, but it must not pass, fail, rank, or rewrite
creative work from repeated words, camera tuples, prompt hashes, semantic
similarity, reference frequency, section length, or space/action signatures.

Keep three kinds of claim distinct, then reuse existing authorship,
contextual-judgment, and Director-review receipts before Founder acceptance.
Attributed metadata and author declarations (title, registered status, declared
coverage, publisher fields copied into a probe) are claims about the source;
they are not screen meaning. Measured facts (file SHA-256, ffprobe duration,
extracted timestamps, sample counts) are machine-observed within the method and
scope that produced them. A human or LLM does not need to re-observe a file
hash or duration for that measurement to be a fact. Being produced by
deterministic code does not make the measurement semantically certain: it does
not prove the event is adequate, the ranges are the same event, or the work is
ready. Semantic inference remains human or assigned-model judgment. Matching
identity or hash proves the same registered bytes or source id, not a
meaningful same-event. Matching space, action, or camera labels or geometry
neither proves nor disproves screen-function diversity. Contextual review
examines narrative purpose, visible change, relation, and alternatives.

### Authoring Method Versus Transport Contract

Skills and linked creative modules own authoring method: the questions a
director asks, the alternatives worth considering, and the contextual judgment
that makes a scene, relationship, camera route, reference use, or Core-Cut/Branch-Cut
split convincing. A schema may carry selected creative truth so another stage
can receive it. The active exactly-three FP routes and 2--4 decision candidates
are transport-completeness floors for a reviewable comparison, not a machine
quality score or command to generate every Cartesian combination. Other fixed
alternative counts, camera-vocabulary pools,
body-part checklists, prompt labels, repeated keywords, and prose shapes must
not become creative-readiness conditions. Optional vocabularies stay optional
even when they are structured.

Separate creative values from transport categories. Cut roles, layout families,
shot scope/crop, camera position and route, subject direction, head/gaze
relation, occlusion, action, and composition are scene-specific human/model
authorship and must accept ordinary natural language. Closed
values may remain only where downstream logic needs a routing, safety,
permission, provider, approval, or lifecycle category. Such a category cannot
stand in for the actual authored direction. Once a human/model selects and the
owning stage approves a creative value, downstream stages preserve that exact
value; openness before selection is not permission to drift after the lock.

Schemas, validators, compilers, automation, and review-surface builders own
transport and state: types, enums, ids, paths, SHA-256, exact source coverage,
pointer and exact-copy integrity, output ownership, execution parameters,
receipt provenance, and approval state. They may verify that a declared
creative payload exists and arrived unchanged, and they may report measured
facts against those contracts. Scripts and other deterministic
tools hold for mechanical absence, staleness, or integrity failure: missing

A measured fact is not semantic certainty. They do not judge whether authored

using existing authorship, contextual-judgment, and review receipts; no new receipt type is created.
Only section and whole-video LLM

repetitive, or ready for revision. A deterministic tool must not repair missing

## Asset Alignment

All song, choreography, performance direction, MV direction, generation, and edit
planning must align with the active member assets:

- member code, name, role, signature move, performance-use role, and visual
  language from `assets/member-registry.yaml`
- vocal/rap color, best-use rail, section use, and prompt guard from
  `assets/voice-style-index.md`
- unit membership, unit purpose, center roles, and unit recommendation limits
  from `assets/unit-registry.yaml`
- current-title member visual assets under
  `assets/members/album-assets/ALBUM_KEY/TITLE_KEY/**`

Required member visuals are newly built or bound and reviewed with their Major
Stacks and material Core candidates for each album/title in Front Planning.
Passed member visuals are that
title's downstream appearance defaults; omission or replacement requires the
exact Founder-approved exception. No separate background/world asset package is
created. Space/background direction remains in Major/Key Screen text and exact
scene references.

The assets are base identity material, not a command to reuse the same outfit,
material, world, hand phrase, choreography, camera language, or member-led frame
structure in every song. Face identity and member role continuity must survive
when members are visible, but the screen driver may be member-led, unit/group-led,
world/object-led, an environment insert, a system transition, or a mixed
member/world beat. Styling, wardrobe material, world design, hand language,
choreography, object behavior, camera route, and section framing should be newly
designed from the current song feeling, message, and section function.
Face-only is the sole approved facial-identity source for member-visual builds
and registered identity-input requests. After a current-title card passes, the
active image-input route has no separate downstream face-only lane. Retired
`no-mic`/`no_mic` variants are invalid. A current-title member visual controls
appearance continuity only when the member is
meaningfully visible; it never forces a readable or frontal face, gaze, pose,
crop, camera, or proportion. These modes coexist. Member-led, center, closeup,
beauty, and choreography
frames remain valid when the song function is identity, vocal ownership,
performance proof, hook memory, or emotional read. World/object-led,
environment-insert, camera-route, and system-transition frames are added options
for sections where the lyric, rhythm, transition, or story job is better carried
by space, objects, camera movement, or a visible system. Do not apply an
automatic quota in either direction unless the founder explicitly sets one.

Until the founder explicitly unlocks it for a specific run, do not use ancient
or medieval costume direction. Avoid historical court dress, fantasy medieval
robes, armor, period gowns, dynasty/empire styling, or any wardrobe cue that
pulls AURORA-M away from contemporary idol/MV styling.

Do not invent ages or birthdays. External web references to real people, idols,
voices, styling, or performance may be recorded and used as creative reference
anchors when the founder or current run names them, but they do not become
member facts unless the founder explicitly locks a new identity direction.

When a creative idea conflicts with member assets, preserve member identity and
adapt the idea unless the founder explicitly approves a new identity direction.
