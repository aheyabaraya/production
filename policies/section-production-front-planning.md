## Front Planning As The Section Spine

Front Planning is the canonical Founder-facing planning report and the root of
all section expansion, while the immutable PRE-00 full-storyline original is
the root of the film itself. Front Planning first exact-projects the entire
PRE-00 storyline lineage. New or materially resumed Front Planning then expands
that original into the working full-film text as an append-only complete
descendant: it plants Majors as events in that expansion, selects Sequence
parent sheets, marks Cores from those sheets, and adds further Cores or Majors
the expanded story requires. Selected-song evidence, inspected references,
target changes, or an explicit Founder intervention are causes of that
expansion, not a license to overwrite the original or to crop one Sequence
into fake Cores. Every FP revision keeps its parent hash, reason, changed
passages, preserved PRE-00 invariants, and exact reference-function receipts.
The original and all earlier versions remain present. A verbatim restatement
with no event-density expansion is CDD HOLD for new authoring. Frozen COLOR
STAY v17 may keep its existing lineage bytes. `whole_film_spine` and alignment
readouts are compact indexes and execution expansions, never replacements for
that lineage.

PRE-00 locks the primary target, evidence-backed audience connection, message,
audience promise, emotional result, and do-not-serve rules before Front
Planning begins. Front Planning exact-carries those PRE-FP locks, tests for a
contradiction, and may append a revision only through the registered upstream
change path. It then locks the remaining execution detail needed to prevent
downstream flattening:

- exact-carried primary target and evidence-backed audience connection from PRE-00;
- exact main-reference-song audience base, selected scene-reference audience
  anchors, and the resolved title-target intersection inherited from PRE-00;
- exact-carried message, audience promise, emotional result, and do-not-serve rules from PRE-00;
- Founder-authorized IDOL start control with the director's recommended
  whole-story order and exact reference-use proposal;
- current-title member visual identities and their actual assets;
- the whole-video message and content-role progression;
- exactly three complete, viable, materially distinguishable routes named
  `FP-A`, `FP-B`, and `FP-C`, one CDD recommendation, and a disposition for
  every unselected route;
- section timing and purpose;
- member allocation by section;
- major section scene, space, action, relation, object/system, and performance
  responsibility;
- section choreography policy;
- section reference assignments and exact dispositions;
- ordered Section Major Stacks using run-unique `MJxx` ids. A section is not
  capped at one event. Multiple material Majors are the default so the film
  can stay dense; Major count has no global cap. An authored audio/hold/bridge
  rationale is required only when a section deliberately has one Major;
- 2--4 formal Sequence Compositions for each material Major, after any
  worker-internal exploration drafts, then one selected composition with
  mismatch/risk and rejected dispositions; this is per material Major, never a
  film-wide 2--4 total;
- one independently CDD-reviewed Sequence Motion Analysis row on only the
  selected Sequence Composition, exact-bound by analysis path/SHA, row
  pointer/value SHA, and final DDC review bytes. The analysis remains advisory:
  01 records its typed adopt/partial/reject decision and exact existing Core,
  still-camera, and Branch-opportunity ids; 02A imports Core bytes unchanged
  and authors actual Branch still truth; 03V owns final temporal camera after
  pixel PASS. Identity disposition preserves a per-scene visibility intent
  separately from exact current-title fidelity for only the cues actually
  visible: face when visible, hair, styling, clothing, body silhouette, or
  member-specific detail. `member_absent` forces none of those cues; an
  intentionally unreadable face still preserves every listed non-face cue.
  Rear, silhouette, occluded, wide, fast-rotation, and nonfrontal views are not
  identity failures, and a member, readable/frontal face, or frontal gaze is
  never an every-cut requirement. A nonselected, stale, pre-copy-only, or self-reviewed binding
  is HOLD;
- 2--4 actual still candidates per material Core Slot and exactly one selected
  reference path/SHA/pixel inspection/functions binding. Only a
  Founder-authored waiver naming the exact Core and reason may replace it;
- representative/risk Hero Core outputs for G-30 visual proof, separately from
  reference coverage; a reference-only package cannot close G-30;
- sequence sheets and selected scene cells/crops when supplied;
- section entry and exit continuity.

Front Planning may enrich the locked intersection with selected-song and
source-pixel evidence, but it may not silently choose a different audience.
Every applied or combined scene reference names the PRE-00/Front Planning
audience anchor it serves and states whether it narrows the base, acts as a
bounded acquisition bridge, or merely tests a surface. A surface-only or
rejected anchor cannot become a governing section reference. If a later exact
scene reveals a different audience promise or rejection pattern, revise the
smallest upstream owner and hold affected sections until the intersection is
valid again.

01R and optional 01R-C are Front Planning subwork. New work compares as many
decision-ready actual units as its named gaps require; discovery counts are not
creative completion quotas. Every active visual section binds at least one
applied or combined contiguous Sequence Unit made of exactly five ordered
consecutive samples from the same event at exact one-second cadence. It carries
exactly five frame observations and one contextual flow reading covering entry,
trigger, visible transformation, peak/reveal, exit, camera/composition/depth
progression, continuity, observation versus inference, and transfer/exclusion.
Every sample keeps its exact timestamp/path/SHA-256 and the Unit keeps a
hash-bound manifest. Longer events use linked Sequence Units without silently
duplicating boundary frames. A full sequence sheet is navigation evidence and
counts as zero; one exact-five Unit counts once. Existing three-second Units are
legacy-pinned readers only: never rewrite, upgrade, or newly author them. If five
legal one-second samples cannot preserve one coherent event, hold instead of
padding, duplicating, or mixing events. A Founder-selected
`full_scene_direct` or `sequence_direct` reference remains governing.

`section_plan.sequence_reference_unit_ids` binds the selected bundle to its
section. Any Major Scene in that section may cite the same
`reference_unit_id` in `reference_unit_ids` and use its ordered grammar, while
02A may later choose the smallest useful hash-bound subset of its five frames.
The five source frames are an evidence floor, not five required Major Scenes,
Core Cuts, Branch Cuts, prompts, images, or edit beats. Sequence frame
observations may name still-camera geometry on the four catalog axes:
height, distance/scale, direction/angle, and depth. A locked or slowly
moving take may keep the same angle across all five frames. Do not require
those five frames to become five differently angled production images.
Distinct production-start stills that 01 will turn into Official Core
images, and that 03V may later collect onto one Motion Storyboard, must
have a meaningfully different camera angle from the other distinct
production-start stills on that later board. Camera angle is
facing-and-pitch, not shot size, not height-only, not a label, and not a
random rotation. Same image exact-carry is not a new image. If an explicit
Founder lock requires a conflicting repeat, record a Founder-exception HOLD
naming the exact stills and lock.

## Sequence Parent And Core Marks

A section may hold several events. Each event is a Major with its Sequence
parent sheet: how that event flows, as one exact-five one-second Sequence Unit
or linked Units of one contiguous event, shown as five-in-one / 01/05–05/05.
Major count has no global cap; many Majors are how the film gets dense, not by
splitting one Sequence into fake Cores.

Core is the center of that event, not a cropped dump of Sequence frames.
Sequence is the parent. From one event, leave the one or two stills that must
remain as images — the real peaks. Optional noncanonical Core-peak teaching
examples live in `policies/examples/reference-method-examples.md`. Do not
require a start / middle / end Core set, and do not treat frames 1, 3, and 5
of one Sequence Unit as three Cores. Those are `same_event_anchor` phase
choices after a hypothesis exists. They do not count toward the 2--4 Core
`creative_hypothesis` still floor. One or two key Cores per event is the
typical remainder; a third Core needs a new governing event, which is a new
Major. Branch, not extra Cores, diversifies each Core. When two Cores on one
Major will each receive a distinct 01 primary image, those bound stills
must not share the same camera angle. If the Sequence Unit is a locked-angle
take, do not mint two same-angle Cores from it. Mark one Core and leave
other angles to 02A Branch; or bind a better still of the same moment whose
inspected pixels prove a different angle; or HOLD for an explicit Founder
lock naming the repeat.

Author Sequence Composition candidates, select one flow, then mark those one
or two Cores on that selected flow. Gathering Core stills first and attaching
Sequence later is CDD HOLD. A Core reference is (a) one marked frame from that
Sequence or (b) a better still of the same moment. If the one-second sample
misses the peak, keep that one still; do not stretch the Sequence. Remaining
Core candidates must be related to that Major job and materially different
hypotheses, not a Sequence-frame dump. Expansion both marks Cores from the
selected Sequence and adds further Cores or Majors the expanded PRE-00
storyline requires. A still gallery does not replace Major.

The Founder kinetic start is planned from Sequence placement through Major
lock, Core expansion, and Branch expansion:
`이거 거의 2-3초마다 컷전환에 카메라는 계속 움직이는 느낌이 나야해. 이걸 같이 처음부터 고려해야해`
That is a named transition tendency plus continuous camera energy, not a 2--3
second min/max quota. An intended hold is legal when it names a reason and
kinetic continuity in and out. The criterion for adding a further Core is that
kinetic start: another must-remain still the fast tempo needs. Machines do not
count seconds and do not require one Core per 2--3 second interval. Sequence
frame 1/3/5 dumps are not that criterion. A new governing event is a new
Major. Same-event cut density that is not a new still lives in Branch.

### Kinetic Change-Point Slot Sheet

Add a change-point slot sheet as a new layer at internal FP Close / Graph P3.
Do not convert Sequence frames, `scene_phases.observable_change`, Core slots,
or Key Screens into that sheet.

A slot exists only where the event actually changes versus the previous slot
on one axis: `angle`, `action`, `person`, `background`, or an `authored hold`
with reason and continuity in and out. Silence is not a row. A locked-angle
take may be one slot plus hold, not a row per second. Sequence stays evidence
(`not_cut_plan`). Core marks 1--2 nails on that sheet. Remaining slots bind as
Branch for 02A. Production-start (Core-bound) stills carry four-axis camera:
height, distance/scale, direction/angle, and depth. Key Screen inherits that
Core-bound slot's camera and action; it does not re-author composition. Bind
Sequence frames and pin functions to slots when the reference is chosen so
01/02A do not hunt. Neighbor slots must declare a different axis or an
authored hold. Collected production-start still pairs are complete, not
adjacent-only. Scripts check those declarations; they never score uniqueness
or cinema. Do not raise v17 Founder-facing `required[]`. Founder Alignment
does not fill this form.

## Current-Title Member Visuals

Every title owns its member visual assets under:

```text
assets/members/album-assets/ALBUM_KEY/TITLE_KEY/**
```

Each required member visual is newly built or bound during Front Planning. A
prior album visual is never inherited or substituted. Passed current-title
member visuals are the default appearance inputs for Key Screens and downstream
sections and do not reopen review. Only a new or fully restyled candidate needs
Founder exception selection; an exact Founder-approved exception may omit or
replace a passed title visual.

The current-title asset set may contain member visual, group/unit, and
wardrobe/material assets.
INK demonstrates organization and review depth only. Never reuse INK pixels,
styling, hair, makeup, clothing, objects, or backgrounds for another title.

Face-only is the sole approved facial-identity source while Front Planning
builds a member visual or a registered identity-input request. The active
downstream route has no separate face-only or identity-reference input lane
after the current-title card passes. Retired `no-mic`/`no_mic` variants are
invalid. A member visual:

- is selected only when that member is meaningfully visible;
- controls appearance, hair, makeup, wardrobe, material, silhouette, and body
  continuity;
- may support a back view or silhouette;
- never forces a readable face, frontal face, frontal gaze, pose, crop,
  still-camera geometry, or proportion;
- is absent from world-, object-, environment-, or system-only branch cuts.

When a title needs a new or fully restyled member appearance, Front Planning
uses the active `idol-member-visual-identity` method. The build job preserves
only one approved face identity and replaces hair, hair color, makeup,
accessories, complete wardrobe, materials, footwear, and styled silhouette from
Founder-selected styling references. It produces one clean 16:9 card per
member with exactly three generated panels in face, upper-body, full-body order.
All three panels must show the same identity and styling, and the full-body
panel must remain visible through the footwear.

The face asset and styling references exist together only inside that Front
Planning build and its Founder review evidence. They do not create a downstream
face lane. Before Founder PASS the clean card is a candidate. After PASS, only
the clean card is promoted into the current-title package; face/style inputs
and comparison projections remain provenance. Downstream scene work attaches
the passed card and still lets the Branch Cut own pose, action, gaze, camera,
placement, crop, occlusion, and proportion.

02A records physical member presence separately from attachment need.
`visible_member_focus` names the member who is physically present in the frame.
The human/model-authored `member_visual_applicability` then declares one of
three transport states:

- `meaningfully_visible`: appearance, wardrobe, silhouette, or body continuity
  is readable enough to require the current-title member visual;
- `visible_but_member_visual_unnecessary`: a member or body fragment is present,
  but the frame does not need a member visual because identity/appearance
  continuity is not meaningfully readable or controlling;
- `not_visible`: no member is physically present.

The second state preserves member presence in screen truth while prohibiting a
needless member-visual attachment. A deterministic script validates the
declared state, policy, and attachment set only; it never infers the state from
body-part words, shot scale, occlusion, or distance.

No separate title-global background/space or world asset package is created.
A section- or Cut-scoped `scene_visual_package` is a reference role group, not
a second canonical world owner. Major/Key Screen text and exact scene
references still carry space, action, staging, still-camera geometry,
placement, crop, palette/light, object response, and sequence grammar without
replacing member identity.

## Sequence And Scene Reference Handling

A sequence reference begins from an ordered frame sheet or bounded playback
range normally derived from a registered public source. The broad sheet remains
navigation-only. Its active Front Planning reference unit is the exact
five-frame contiguous bundle defined above. Front Planning and 01 must
understand the frame flow, not treat the sheet as a single mood image. A
selected cell or crop is a scene reference and receives its own path and
SHA-256.

First-read is media-shaped: one nested still observation for a single
still/crop/selected cell; an active five-frame sequence bundle requires flow
observation across all five samples plus one nested still observation per
selected anchor cell only. Do not treat a whole sheet as one still or as one
mood, and do not infer movement between samples that the source playback does
not prove. Incomplete first-reads hold. Exact
trees and examples live only in
`skills/idol-front-planning-director/references/director-operating-contract.md`
(top-level H2 `## 02B-Shaped Source Observation`).

When a reference is `full_scene_direct` or `sequence_direct`, preserve:

- visible action or collision;
- body and object route;
- prop or world effect;
- subject placement;
- still-camera height, distance, direction, angle, and depth;
- crop and negative space;
- ordered scene grammar (sheet flow observation for sequence sheets).

Replace only current-member identity, selected-song timing, or explicitly
discarded source elements. Support references may fill an unowned function or
test continuity, feasibility, and risk; they may not dilute the governing
reference.

## PRE-FP Target And Message Handling

Target definition, audience promise, and message are PRE-FP locks. Front
Planning exact-projects those locks and expands them into whole-video content
roles, observable screen consequences, and section jobs. It does not silently
choose a different audience, and it does not re-score `target_empathy_fit` or
`message_fit` for each later candidate, section, Major, or reference.

After the PRE-FP lock, later review asks only:

1. Does this result violate the lock?
2. Is the lock realized as observable action, space, or state change?

If the first answer is yes or the second is no, HOLD regardless of any 0--4
grade. A schema PASS, worker self-score, or average vector cannot override
that gate.

## Roster-Derived Section Cohort Construction

P3-Ci and the later G-40 Section-Cluster CDDs use the same cohort identity.
For two or more ordered sections, partition the roster into contiguous
cohorts of two or three. Every section belongs to exactly one cohort. A
one-section title may use one explicit singleton. Capacity never creates a
cohort. If an already approved story/audio boundary makes a contiguous 2--3
split impossible, Founder escalation is required; do not invent a creative
partition.

Use this deterministic remainder-first rule:

- `n % 3 == 0` -> all threes: 3; 6=3+3; 9=3+3+3;
- `n % 3 == 1` -> two twos, then threes: 4=2+2; 7=2+2+3; 10=2+2+3+3;
- `n % 3 == 2` -> one two, then threes: 2; 5=2+3; 8=2+3+3; 11=2+3+3+3.

Cohort-boundary incoming/outgoing handles are read-only shared. Section, Cut,
and board decision write scope must not overlap. Image count, reference load,
motion difficulty, and review cost may inform worker dispatch inside a
cohort; they may not add, merge, or overlap cohorts.

## Optional Performance

Performance readability is not a standing Front Planning criterion. Record
`performance_mode` as `none`, `supporting`, or `primary`. Grade performance
only when the mode is `supporting` or `primary` and actual performance
evidence exists. When the mode is `none`, omit the criterion. Do not invent a
dummy performance reference to avoid N/A.

## Contextual 0--4 Director Review

Assigned Directors grade actual media and named context. Scripts validate
structure, provenance, and approval state only. They never author a grade,
average, total, ranking, or creative PASS.
The Graph-bound prose judgment records, for every criterion, the exact evidence
locator, observable claim, screen consequence, preserve/change/do-not-infer
boundary, correction action, and acceptance evidence. Its decision selects the
next legal graph edge. The checkpoint-specific profile supplies the stage
criteria inside this one common Graph judgment; it does not create a second
planning vector.

| grade | meaning | allowed state |
|---:|---|---|
| 4 | can govern the main judgment; actual screen evidence is sufficient | promotion eligibility only |
| 3 | strong, but needs combination or a small correction | internal combine/correct |
| 2 | valid comparison evidence, too weak as a production input | held evidence only |
| 1 | low value in the current work | reject |
| 0 | collides with a PRE-FP lock, source, identity, or execution condition | hard reject/hold |

Grade 4 is admission eligibility, not automatic PASS. Local review, Pairwise
review among viable nearest alternatives, and Overall Global review of exact
bytes remain mandatory. Optional or absent criteria stay `not_applicable`
with a reason.

FP Close reference review grades:

1. storyline and section/Major job causality;
2. selected audio and 2--3 second kinetic/edit usefulness;
3. motion source's actual contiguous change and landing;
4. still camera, composition, depth, and edit value;
5. coverage and synthesis across at most four role groups;
6. Major/Core and whole-video non-duplication;
7. direct-transfer boundary, provenance, and feasibility.

Admission to Overall promotion eligibility requires every applicable item at
least 3, motion item 3 at grade 4, still-camera/composition item 4 at grade 4,
selected contiguous motion evidence on every material Major, no hard-gate
violation, CDD Local/Pairwise PASS, and Overall Global PASS. Record the vector
with the exact `idol_graph_contextual_judgment.v2` profile carried by the Role
Context Pack; it cannot be reused as the generated Production-Cut pixel rubric.
`idol_planning_contextual_grade_vector.v1` remains compatibility evidence for
the still-active legacy writer and its historical reader; it is never
co-authored in Graph v2. Founder projection hides raw pools, URLs, worker
transcripts, and grades 0--2.

## Material Major Motion Evidence

Every material Major requires real contiguous motion evidence: one
exact-five one-second Sequence Unit, or linked Units of that same event. A
broad contact sheet counts as zero. If the chosen screen is a locked hold,
still inspect the contiguous interval and state why the hold is stronger than
movement. Promotion eligibility requires motion grade 4 and still-camera /
composition grade 4, each with an actual evidence locator. The Founder kinetic start remains a named 2--3 second
transition tendency plus continuous camera energy, not a machine duration
quota, and not one Core per interval.

## G-40 Planning Handoff And Cohort Carry

At G-40, after 02A Draft Keyframe Proof and the Founder G2 production-scope
decision, the Planning CDD Cell emits one hash-bound handoff and retires
P1/P2/P3-Ci as read-only rollback points. Planning lanes do not write 02B
pixels or 03V motion. Each later Section-Cluster CDD exact-carries the same
`section_cohort_id` and reviewed P3 Decision Set into its disjoint 2--3
section cohort. Silent repartition after G-40 is HOLD.
