# IDOL Section Production Policy

Status: active
Scope: owner model, smallest-owner revision, compact stage map, and activation
of stage-bound section-production chapters
Authority: `AGENTS.md` first, then this index, the bound chapter, the active
owning schema, and the complete owning skill

## Purpose

Section production exists so the MV director can expand a short Founder brief
into a complete, high-quality film without requiring the Founder to rewrite
every stage. It is an execution boundary, not a creative formula and not a fixed
section, core-cut, or branch-cut count. A section may hold several events;
Major count has no cap. Each Major is one event with a Sequence parent sheet.
That event typically leaves one or two key Cores, never a required
start/middle/end set; Branch Cuts diversify each Core.
Short-form extraction, per-Major hook quotas, and derivative fields are not
Front Planning, 01, or 02A readiness requirements.

The active MV route is:

```text
selected song and 00A
-> Front Planning with 01R and optional 01R-C inside it
-> 01 section direction and one-image Official Core Storyboard
-> 02A immutable Core import and `1..N` Branch planning
-> 02B image-prompt translation and generation
-> Generated Production-Cut Review
-> Audio Production-Cut Placement
-> 03V storyboard boards, subject motion, camera motion, and video prompts
-> passed playback
-> separate 04 edit/QC
```

There is no separate active 00, 01A, or 02 creative artifact. Their useful jobs
belong to Front Planning, 01, and 02A respectively.

## Owner Model

This section has two mutually exclusive routing branches. Existing runs and
new runs before a verified Graph default-switch receipt use the legacy-pinned
single durable CDD language below. A new run admitted after that receipt uses
the Graph v2 owner model instead: one Overall Director; Planning CDD-P1 and
CDD-P2 singletons; the minimum roster-derived number of CDD-P3-Ci lanes needed
to cover contiguous 2--3-section cohorts; matching post-G-40 V-Ci cohort CDDs;
one section image/motion writer per section; and one Edit Director. In each
logical Director lane, at most one physical primary and one successor may
exist per macro-stage, with single-writer CAS and no automatic third session.
This is not a global CDD ceiling: different disjoint P3-Ci lanes, and later
different disjoint V-Ci lanes, may be active concurrently while lower provider
and combined capacity plus Director backpressure permit. Within one
`lane_id + macro_stage`, the successor is read-only before CAS; the commit
atomically retires the primary before activating the successor as sole writer.
Graph CDDs write
disjoint Decision Sets and return through the registered P1, V-Ci, and Overall
edges; none gains Founder conversation or promotion authority. The two routing
branches may never be active writers for the same run.

- One Overall Director owns routing, canonical promotion, whole-video judgment,
  Founder-facing recommendations, and final creative acceptance.
- One durable Creative Development Director decision/workbench lineage opens at
  startup with exactly one active, separate Founder-visible physical CDD
  session. Overall remains the only Founder-conversation owner. CDD directs
  bounded departments and development blocks, reviews every Song, reference,
  current-title Member Visual Identity, FP/01/02A, image, Motion Storyboard, and
  03V playback return, synthesizes Decision Sets, and sends reviewed milestone
  packages upward without becoming a competing promotion authority.
- CDD review closes worker requirements against exact returned bytes. Overall
  reviews the curated milestone and surviving media for whole-video coherence
  and promotion; it repeats the worker-level review only when evidence is
  stale, incomplete, or conflicting.
- Ordinary non-media reference research and section development resolve the
  logical `worker_high` profile through the immutable run snapshot; new
  assignments prefer its readiness-valid Grok CLI provider and never fall back.
  Terra is not an ordinary creative-production route, and there is no standing
  Final Director. A bounded adversarial adviser review is advisory only when a
  named boundary warrants it; OD, CDD, and Edit Director remain
  `director_deep/xhigh`.
- The Song route uses one physical Founder-visible Song Room under CDD for lyric,
  arrangement, candidate, selected-audio, audio-motion, and 00A work. It resumes
  in the same task after HOLD or later 00A change and returns only to CDD.
- Before 02A, one bounded development block owns one section by default or two
  to three connected sections with separate section results. The Director opens
  only disjoint blocks justified by the current graph and backpressure; numeric
  runtime capacity is not an authority or quality gate.
- After exact 02A approval, one fresh Founder-visible provider-resolved
  `section_image_motion_worker / worker_high` lineage receives the compact
  approved checkpoint and owns exactly one physical section through 02B
  compile, generation, Audio Production-Cut Placement, 03V, and playback
  handoff to CDD. Pixel and playback review are a fresh artifact-only window.
  Its registered Cursor or Grok realization uses only that provider's registered
  model, effort, tools, auth, and output intake.
- Do not pre-open image-motion sessions while Front Planning, 01, or 02A truth
  is still changing.
- One logical artifact or section shard has one active owner at a time.
- Disjoint pre-02A block scopes may run in bounded parallel. A grouped owner
  loads only its assigned one-to-three connected sections and returns separate
  rows; a post-02A physical owner loads exactly one section. No worker loads,
  authors, or merges outside its dispatch scope.
- The coordinator alone performs ordered canonical merges and whole-video
  continuity/repetition review.
- Upstream holds from a post-02A section return to the continuing CDD with the
  exact affected scope; CDD revises only that block, reviews the replacement,
  and sends a milestone to Overall before the same section lineage resumes.
- After required 03V handoffs pass, Overall opens one fresh Founder-visible Edit
  Director. It owns 04 review and merge, may dispatch disjoint Edit
  `worker_high` section/chunk shards through the run-resolved registered
  provider when needed, and returns final 04/05 truth only to Overall. Edit
  `worker_high` shards never bypass Edit Director or become final edit
  truth. 04 begins only from passed 03V playback and the edit-owner contract.

Scripts validate schemas, paths, hashes, exact projections, status, coverage,
and output presence. They do not author target, story, action, camera,
reference interpretation, prompt prose, motion, or edit truth.

Every worker is launched and evaluated through the Department Dispatch Contract.
It identifies the required skills and linked modules to read to EOF and apply,
the exact sources and output, Anti-Flat evidence, decision epoch, authority, and
stop conditions, interaction surface, dispatcher, return recipient, and
same-lineage reuse policy. The runtime records the skill revisions plus
input/result digests; multi-file returns expose one aggregate manifest with
exact child paths/hashes, and no separate worker-authored skill-application
receipt is required.

Every CDD startup, successor attempt, and worker dispatch also binds the shared
provider-neutral Studio Kernel and its twelve-block launch command. The exact
`automation/harness-skill-load-profiles.yaml` profile/checkpoint, complete
owning skill, and linked reads are the reusable Department Recipe. Repeated
reference research, image creation/inspection, output, review, and ordinary
HOLD methods stay there and are not rewritten in every assignment.

The assigning director inspects the current frontier and authors the Current
Assignment Delta: the present creative job, task-specific work beyond the
recipe, and exact current-work quality proof. Fixed headings, ids, roles,
paths, hashes, permissions, recipe pointers, output/return fields, and the
already-authored delta may be exact-copied and hashed mechanically. No script
selects the recipe, summarizes evidence, fills the delta, or paraphrases it.
This command is not the 02B five-line image prompt.

Overall, CDD, and workers wait for events rather than poll. Only `final`, `hold`,
`authority_request`, and `milestone` wake the declared recipient. The wake
envelope points to exact returned output or aggregate-manifest, hold/rejection,
material-risk, and unresolved-decision evidence; it excludes raw commentary and
transcripts. `returned` is never creative PASS: CDD reviews its workers, and
Overall alone promotes a CDD-reviewed milestone.

Every section shard admits creative content only from the hash-bound current
run, current-title member visuals, and selected source material for that run. A
prior-run artifact, test fixture, maintenance example, or un-intaken file from
another run cannot supply action, staging, camera, composition, prompt, or
motion truth.

## Flat Delegation, Capacity, And Pointer Projections

Provider-native internal helpers are available by default to Overall, CDD, Edit
Director, and bounded workers for scoped reading, analysis, tests, or candidate
work. A helper is part of the parent Work Package: it never becomes another
logical role, canonical owner, Founder-conversation surface, writer, promotion
authority, or paid/external authority. CDD/FP/02A multi-leaf work must meet the
execution-contract `spawn_subagent` floor of 4. Post-song reference fetch must
open the floor of 6 named disjoint workers. A physical worker session is selected at
the start when media, long context, independent output, or lifecycle evidence
makes it more effective. Internal and physical owners cannot be active for the
same Work Package at once. If a scope is too large, the parent returns
`split_required` with exact unowned subscopes, proposed siblings, disjoint output
paths, a shared boundary, and risk; the recipient Director opens siblings.

The run's Director Dispatch Index and scoped views are pointer-only projections
over Department Dispatch attempts. A multi-file worker return exposes one
aggregate manifest with exact child paths and SHA-256 values; the index stores
only its pointer, counts, scope, lifecycle state, review disposition, board
links, and next actions. A deterministic Director Boot Pack projects only the
active stage, decision epoch, explicit locks, open/held tasks, canonical heads,
active board cycle, and next actions. It excludes raw pools, full transcripts,
and unrelated sections. One mechanical writer owns each index/board manifest,
and source-set changes abort a rebuild rather than silently merging.

CDD durability in the legacy-pinned branch means the decision/workbench lineage, not one unlimited physical
chat. Exactly one physical CDD may be active. Sequential rollover uses only the
registered `idol-director-boot-pack.v3` plus session-router route: first Founder
confirmation, a closed immutable hash-equal checkpoint, no-rollover-zone and
profile/read verification, a fresh zero-turn successor, and one read-only
seven-answer preflight while the prior CDD remains active. The successor must
open the exact allowed pointers/hashes and answer: the immutable PRE-00
original premise; what the active full-text revision changed and preserved;
target/audience promise, rejection triggers, and do-not-serve; Founder locks
versus proposal-only inputs; current Section/MJ progression and largest open
screen gap; applied/combined/held/rejected references and do-not-revive
decisions; and the active owner, review queue, next legal action, and stop
condition. Scripts check answer presence and exact pointers only. The
successor cannot create or review work, promote truth, or write canonical
state during this preflight.

After the returned final envelope and registered Goal admission pass, a second
Founder confirmation permits one exact writer compare-and-swap. Cursor uses its
external Goal/runtime receipt and Grok uses its native Goal. Direct Cursor-CLI or
Grok-CLI operation uses the provider-neutral router: current state SHA and
generation must match, A becomes inactive before B can receive a creative
operation, and rollback makes B inactive before A is reactivated. Grok
`complete/goal_completed` closes admission only. The switch changes no HTML,
review-board, canvas, storyline, Section, or canonical creative bytes. Routes
without exact admission, access denial, CAS, and rollback proof remain `HOLD`.

The same existing CDD may still progress through a legal registered checkpoint
after its prior attempt returns and receives director review, any required
Founder decision or intervention receipt is bound, the owner/scope stay fixed,
and a new immutable attempt refreshes the next checkpoint's reads, hashes, and
input digest. This continuation cannot infer PASS, skip any stage or approval
gate, widen scope, or bypass the registered successor route.

Provider-native resource observations are scheduling evidence, not hard-coded
project limits. New launches pause only when the next assignment consumes
pending returned bytes (especially image hashes), the index or board writer is
behind, or an owner/path collision exists. Available capacity
never overrides review backpressure, evidence floors, or Founder locks.

## Chapter Activation

`policies/section-production.md` is the owner and smallest-owner index plus the
compact stage map. Overall loads this index from startup with
`wiki/production-system.md` `## Active Route`. It does not load unused-stage
procedure. Each admitted CDD room loads only the film chapters for its exact
Planning lane or section cohort checkpoint; until the verified Graph v2 switch,
the pinned legacy one-CDD room keeps that boundary.
A worker loads only the bound chapter for the complete job. Every bound file
is read to EOF.

| Chapter | Path | Activate when |
|---|---|---|
| Front Planning spine, member visuals, sequence units, development blocks | `policies/section-production-front-planning.md` | CDD film work and Front Planning / reference / member-visual checkpoints |

## Smallest-Owner Revision

A Founder correction starts at the smallest canonical owner whose meaning
changed:

| Change | Owner | Invalidate and rebuild |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel defect only | exact generated image | that image and pixel review |
| Same-branch-cut render wording | 02B packet | that packet, request, image, and downstream use |
| One branch cut's visible action or still view | 02A branch cut | that branch cut and dependents |
| One core cut's event, reference, image, or branch need | 01 core cut | that core cut and dependents |
| One section direction or reference application | 01 section | that section's 02A onward |
| Target, message, member allocation, title identity, or section spine | Front Planning | only affected projected sections |
| Camera movement after passed images | 03V board/cue | that cue and playback |
| Edit-only timing or assembly | 04 | 04 output only |

The Founder intervention receipt preserves the verbatim input, last passed
baseline, exact changed fields, affected section ids, and downstream
invalidations. Unaffected sections and upstream artifacts carry forward
unchanged. A Founder direct replacement needs no second LLM taste veto, while
schema, hash, asset, safety, pixel/playback, and external-approval gates remain.

For a Founder-authored section reorder, the minimum accepted input is the
visible drag result plus one exact natural-language instruction. The Workbench
first saves the pending plan/canvas pair and binds both base digests. The
read-only Overall Director session then returns only the five mandatory
integrity checks; it neither writes files nor claims output hashes. The native
Workbench is the sole canonical writer: it exact-applies the visible order and
Founder sentence, advances the affected Founder epoch once, writes the v2
intervention receipt, declares affected invalidations, recompiles the owner
projection, refreshes matching current-next pending state, and rerenders the
same board in one rollback-protected transaction. Success requires exact
revised plan/canvas paths and hashes, a reconciled canvas, and a verified board
path/hash/revision before reload. A concurrent external-file change, digest
mismatch, stale owner feed, failed mandatory check, or incomplete rollback pair
is a HOLD; there is no silent retry or widened rewrite.

The Overall Director classifies the intervention as proposal/preference,
correction, explicit lock, or Founder direct replacement and increments the
decision epoch only for affected scopes. A worker return from an older affected
epoch cannot enter canonical truth. Workers do not independently reinterpret a
Founder message into canonical edits.

## Merge And Closeout

Before canonical merge:

- every physical shard validates against the active schema and source hashes;
- every section has one owner and disjoint paths;
- section order matches Front Planning and 02A;
- reference and current-title visual bindings exact-copy;
- Founder review status is present for every included section;
- no held or rejected shard enters the merge;
- whole-video continuity and repetition review passes.

Canonical merge is deterministic and does not author creative truth. Any source
or meaning drift returns to the smallest owner rather than widening context or
rebuilding unrelated stages.
