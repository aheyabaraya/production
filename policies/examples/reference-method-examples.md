## Camera-Only Reference-Direction Examples

Role: teaching example for a named camera-only reference-direction assignment
under `policies/creative-production-constitution.md` heading
`### Selected Reference Direct-Follow Preservation`.
Not a current-run camera value, prompt, or default.

Noncanonical examples, not scores: on a still, the performer keeps the same
action, emotion, hand-object path, and source event; an authorized 01/02A closer
distance or angle on that same cut still shows the contact. On playback, after
passed pixels, an authorized 03V gentle push-in still shows that contact.

perceived emotional relationship; revise that camera even if the frame looks more
dramatic.
