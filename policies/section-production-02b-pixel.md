`section_image_motion_worker / worker_high` production session just in time. It

### Four Production Role Groups

A Cut may bind at most four logical reference role groups:

1. `character_visual` — required when a character is visible;
2. `scene_visual_package` — required, including an intentionally designed
   empty or abstract space with an explicit composition anchor;
3. `motion_reference` — required upstream evidence for every material Major;
4. `prop_reference` — optional when the prop's shape, material, scale, contact,
   or state change is material to the Cut.

Do not invent a fifth role. Wardrobe lives inside `character_visual`.
Performance is not a role group; it is a conditional evaluation module of
`motion_reference` when actual performance is present. Decorative objects stay
inside `scene_visual_package.world_look_binding`. A hero prop that the body
must contact may use `prop_reference` without becoming a fifth role.

`character_visual` carries current-title identity, hair, makeup, wardrobe,
material, silhouette, and body continuity, plus pose visual when needed. It
does not own background, temporal camera, a forced frontal face or gaze, or a
separate wardrobe score. A character-absent Cut records
`not_applicable_member_absent` and must not attach a dummy identity still.

`motion_reference` is upstream contiguous-event evidence. 02B may attach the
smallest useful phase frames only when those stills make the pose or
motion-ready geometry readable. 02B does not author pan, orbit, push, speed,
or easing from that evidence. A material Major without selected contiguous
motion evidence is HOLD.

Target empathy and message are PRE-FP locks. 02B does not rescore them. It
asks only whether the pixels violate the lock and whether the lock is visible
as body, space, or state change. Either failure is HOLD regardless of grade.

### Scene Visual Package Compilation

A title-global background/world asset package remains forbidden. A Cut-scoped
`scene_visual_package` is a selected input bundle for this Cut, not a second
canonical title owner and not a downstream face-only or world-package lane.

Judge the two sub-bindings separately, then compile them into one Cut input:

- `world_look_binding`: background, space, light, color, material, and
  environment-fixed passive objects;
- `composition_anchor`: still camera height, distance, direction, angle,
  depth, crop, negative space, and foreground/occlusion.

Exactly one `composition_anchor` governs the still. `world_look_binding` may
be the same source or a different source. A different source requires the
cohort CDD to confirm horizon/axis, shot scale, perspective/depth, subject
placement/crop, and light-direction compatibility, then record the transfer
boundary. A full-scene source that also contains a person may serve space,
light, or composition, but that person's face or identity must not overwrite
current-title identity.

Each world/look, composition, and motion logical use exact-carries the selected
Front Planning `reference_unit_id`, `function_id`, purpose, applied/combined
disposition, unit/function pointers, and value hashes. `source_media_type`
describes only whether the evidence is an image, video/range, frame, crop, or
board; it never substitutes for a reference function. The compiler validates
and exact-copies this authored lineage and never infers a function from media
type, filename, provider, or transport attachment role.
Every material `prop_reference` attachment follows the same exact function
lineage rule; all prop attachment ids must be covered and duplicate lineage
rows are rejected. A non-material prop carries neither a dummy attachment nor
a function-lineage row. `character_visual` keeps current-title identity-package
provenance rather than manufacturing a Front Planning function.

Compile the character and scene inputs in exactly one of these modes:

```text
separate = character_visual and scene_visual_package are independently
           selected or produced
combined = one source governs both character_visual and scene_visual_package
hybrid   = combined governing source plus exactly one role's correction input
```

Do not flip a global `separate_background_asset_generation` flag. `separate`
is Cut-scoped production of the two role groups, not a title-wide second
canonical world owner.

### Multi-Reference Attachment Dedupe And Order

Attach only the selected governing input or correction input for each active
role group. Do not attach the raw pool. One source may satisfy several roles;
register that file once and bind multiple roles. Never attach the same image
or video-derived frame twice.

Preserve the exact combined attachment order and every source path/SHA-256 as
an exact-copy receipt. If the provider attachment cap would be exceeded, the
cohort CDD selects the smallest governing set that still covers the required
roles and carries the remainder as authored observable consequence in the
five-line prompt. That reduction is a Director decision, not a compiler
default.

Director context, Role Context Packs, and 02B execution packs carry source id,
selected derivative path/hash, locator or Sequence id, role binding, and
observable transfer. They do not carry raw URL lists, search-query history, or
full source-analysis prose. URLs live once in the source-intake manifest and
are unfolded only for recapture, eligibility audit, or provenance dispute.

02B must preserve appearance/identity when visible, visible scene state,
background/space, lighting/color/material, still camera/composition/depth/crop,
action/pose, motion-ready travel/reveal room, and prop interaction when
applicable. 02B must not author a temporal camera route, speed/easing, an
unseen rear world, generic choreography, body mechanics absent from the
source, or a face/wardrobe/scene replica beyond the selected role transfer.

## Pixel Review And Audio Placement

Every generated Hero/Core/Branch image enters the owning run, receives a byte
hash and full-size pixel review, and is judged against its exact owning Cut and
five-line packet. Generation returns bytes and envelope; pixel review is a fresh
artifact-only window and self-assessment is not PASS. The Generated
Production-Cut Review records observable pixel truth. When a bound Founder G2
`draft_keyframe` envelope names the exact section, Cut, provider, attachment
group, output count, and budget, the cohort CDD records PASS/HOLD/REJECT inside
that envelope. Founder PASS/HOLD/REJECT remains required for material drift
outside that envelope, for any cycle without a bound G2 envelope, and for any
intake adapter that still requires an explicit Founder all-PASS export.

The section owner also inspects the immediately previous and next generated
Branch-Cut images in same-section order when they exist. Generation does not
wait for a neighbor image hash unless that packet attaches it. 03V waits for
the passed Production Cut image hashes on that board. Use the existing
review fields: Branch-Cut `observable_pixel_truth` and `still_camera_result`
record the actual axis/direction, room, layers, path/occlusion, crop safety,
continuity carrier, and physically supportable or unsupported broad motion
families; Core-event and distinct-contribution results preserve the screen job;
section `continuity_observation` records preserved, transformed, intentionally
reset, or broken neighbor carriers; and `repetition_observation` challenges an
unmotivated repeated camera default. `still_camera_result` must also state
whether the pixels realize the authored facing-and-pitch camera angle
(`direction_angle`) rather than only a shot-size or height change. Pixel
comparison is the full collection of generated production stills in this
section, including non-adjacent stills, together with Official Core
primaries in the same section. A section worker does not author other
sections' pixel closures. `collected_image_camera_angle_pixel_review`
must rebind actual passed 02B image hashes for this section and remain
`generated_pixel_closure: false` with `incomplete_pixel_bindings` until
those bindings exist. 03V/G3 cannot treat the 02A planning review as
generated-pixel closure. Label-only wording with identical geometry is
not a distinct angle. 02B does not invent a new angle; a shared 02A
geometry HOLD returns to 02A; a pixel that fails to realize the authored
angle HOLDs this image.
This is contextual pixel judgment, not a
term quota, similarity test, or temporal-motion selection. When the Cut's
selected subject, space, or object relation is the screen job, apply the
constitution `### Concrete Review And Prompt Language` relation-proof
method in the review fields already named above. Do not add a field, score,
or a sixth prompt heading.

After PASS, keep the reviewed source bytes unchanged. The registered local
non-generative LANCZOS upscale is invoked only by the shared post-pass library
after either a complete explicit Founder all-PASS Workbench intake or an exact
G2 delegated close. G2 requires its hash-bound authority envelope and the
Section-Cluster CDD review: every applicable grade is at least 3,
scene-visual-package still-camera/composition close and motion-anchor readability
are both 4, and target/message violation plus material
drift are false. Neither intake script authors grades or a verdict. It is not a
free-running post-02B task or a separate operator command. The validated intake
may promote only the bound generated review's `founder_review` status and
post-pass placement boundary; HOLD, REJECT, partial, stale, and other-cycle
intakes perform no status promotion, no upscale, and open no 03V route. Every
passed generated Branch source must own a validated
`SOURCE_IMAGE.upscale.json` receipt that binds its source hash to an aspect-
preserving PNG with a long edge of at least 3840 pixels and explicitly disables
crop, sharpening, and generative enhancement. A missing, stale, or undersized
Branch receipt is a mechanical HOLD while the exact captured Founder decision
remains preserved. Audio Production-Cut Placement consumes that receipt-bound
upscale for a `branch` row. An `official_core` row instead exact-preserves its
01 passed original pixel bytes as the placement image and must not carry a
local-upscale receipt. Both row types exact-copy the reviewed source's
observable visible-state truth to selected-audio timing and continuity; neither
authors camera movement.

A generated-image or 03V HOLD returns only to the owning V-Ci Section-Cluster
CDD after the verified Graph v2 switch, or the pinned legacy CDD before it. A pixel
defect stays at the exact image/review; same-Branch wording stays at 02B;
visible action or still-view meaning returns to the exact 02A Branch;
Core-event/image/roster meaning returns to the exact 01 Core; section direction
or reference application returns to 01; and temporal motion stays at 03V. The
same owning CDD routes and reviews only the exact affected scope, sends the
reviewed milestone to Overall, and resumes the section lineage from the new
hash. The post-02A owner never bypasses that chain.

The typed Production Cut gate joins each passed 01 Core primary image with every
actual passed generated Branch-Cut still beneath it; the local upscale gate
applies only to those Branch stills. The same local, non-generative upscale may
process an exact generated image on another active Workbench cycle,
including a current-title member clean card, only when its exact card records
Founder PASS. Face-only/style inputs, planning/reference evidence, previously
promoted title assets, video, and HOLD/REJECT cards are excluded. A non-Branch
visual returns to its owning promotion or stage continuation after receipt
validation; only an exact approved Production Cut scope opens 03V.

### Generated Production-Cut 0-4 Review

The assigned model authors a 0--4 grade per applicable criterion against the
actual pixels. Scripts validate presence, locator, and range only. They never
author a grade or creative PASS. Averages and totals are forbidden. Grade 4 is
not automatic PASS.
The co-loaded Graph prose judgment additionally records observable pixel claim,
screen consequence, preserve/change/do-not-infer boundary, correction action,
and acceptance evidence, then routes only to Director review, same-owner
revision, smallest-owner return, or stop.

This is the downstream generated Production-Cut pixel rubric. Do not import,
rename, or reuse a PRE-FP/FP planning vector here; planning grades and pixel
grades answer different evidence questions even when both use a 0--4 scale.

Criteria:

1. character identity and visual continuity, when a character is visible;
2. background, light, material, still composition, and depth;
3. action/pose and motion-anchor readability;
4. multi-reference synthesis, and avoidance of unauthorized copying or
   identity overwrite; selected authorized `bounded_surface_direct`,
   `full_scene_direct`, `sequence_direct`, or named reference-follow
   remains permitted inside its exact scope and identity locks;
5. neighbor Cut distinct contribution;
6. artifacts, anatomy, crop, and provider/input stability;
7. optional prop scale, contact, and state continuity, when material.

Entry to CDD promotion eligibility requires every applicable item `>= 3`,
scene-visual-package still-camera/composition close `= 4`, and motion-anchor
readability `= 4`. Character-absent Cuts use
`not_applicable_member_absent`. Prop-absent Cuts use
`not_applicable_no_material_prop`. Do not invent dummy references to fill N/A
rows. Target/message scores are not used.

Passed pixel bytes only may enter 03V. A collage, identity overwrite, missing
motion anchor, or unmotivated neighbor repeat is HOLD.

### Pixel Correction Loop And Return

The cohort CDD returns one complete correction unit for every observed defect:
all problems, exact invalidated output hashes, correction action, and
acceptance criteria. The section worker authors the corrected output in a new
immutable attempt for only those invalidated paths. Unaffected outputs stay
hash-frozen. A review cycle has at most three total submissions, including the
initial return; a third failure routes upstream or HOLD and cannot open a
fourth child submission.

The worker returns only to its one cohort CDD. That CDD reviews exact prompt
bytes, attachments, outputs, and full-size pixels, then sends one aggregate
cohort manifest to Overall. The manifest carries lane and cohort ids, stage,
generation, active section list, per-section Decision Sets and output hashes,
Local/Pairwise dispositions, correction units, pixel evidence pointers,
boundary status, open holds, input/result digests, and next legal action. It
excludes raw pools, full URL lists, worker transcripts, superseded prose, and
other-cohort media. A structural PASS or worker claim is never creative PASS.
