# IDOL Choreography And Performance Director

This specialist uses one source-inspection and choreography-authoring method
whether it is called directly for a study/test or behind 01, 02A, and 03V. It
is not the default MV director and does not turn film-, object-, environment-,
or system-led sections into performance clips.

## Required Reads

Always read completely:

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `wiki/production-system.md`
3. `policies/creative-production-constitution.md`
4. `modules/choreography.md`
5. `modules/performance-direction.md`
6. exact choreography/performance/fancam source and selected frames/segment

For a full choreography phrase that requires its mandatory image board, also
read `skills/idol-choreography-board/SKILL.md` completely.

Inside the active MV graph-engineering route, the body/performance analysis runs at
`section_01_choreography_detail`. Only an approved `primary` or `supporting`
full phrase advances to `section_01_choreography_board`; a bounded readability
repair stops without loading the board skill.

When projecting into an active MV run, also read completely:

1. `policies/section-production.md`
2. active Front Planning and 01 section
3. `schemas/song-choreography-performance-mv-map.schema.json`
4. exact selected-song section and audio cue
5. current-title member visual package

For a direct choreography study or target-member adaptation without an active
MV handoff, bind the declared test scope, exact source, any supplied audio cue,
the target performer/member identity inputs when applicable, and a noncanonical
output path. Front Planning, 01, and a current-title package are not required
unless the result will enter the active graph-engineering route.

Read 02A or 03V schemas only when authoring their fields.

## Activation

Use this skill for:

- a direct source-bound choreography study, target-performer adaptation,
  choreography storyboard, or image-to-video choreography prompt task;
- `primary`;
- `supporting`;
- repair of visible member/body/performance readability.

For `minimal_body_gaze_hold_guard`, provide only the acting/body evidence needed
for readability. For `absent`, do not author choreography and do not add dance
terms to positive or negative prompts inside that active run. A direct study
may still exist outside the run, but it cannot enter an `absent` section unless
Front Planning and 01 first change that section to `primary` or `supporting`.

## One Workflow, Conditional Handoff

Do not create separate standalone and production authoring methods. Always:

1. bind and inspect the exact source phrase;
2. separate observed source motion from target-performer or current-song
   adaptation;
3. author detailed ordered movement states;
4. author expression, gaze, breath, lighting-readability, hair, garment, and
   object response where visible or physically consequential;
5. select three or four materially different coverage cuts for a full
   choreography phrase;
6. complete a labeled image-based multi-angle choreography storyboard;
7. author cut-specific image-to-video motion guidance when requested;
8. apply the handoff gate below.

The multi-angle storyboard is mandatory for a full choreography study,
adaptation, `primary`, or `supporting` phrase. It is not required when this
skill is used only for a bounded `minimal_body_gaze_hold_guard` or a local
readability repair that does not contain choreography.

### Handoff Gate

Project into canonical 01, 02A, or 03V only when all of the following are true:

- the exact active run and target section are named;
- Front Planning and 01 authorize `primary` or `supporting`, or the task is a
  bounded visible-body readability repair;
- the required run sources and current-title assets are bound;
- the destination stage and output ownership are explicit.

Otherwise stop with a noncanonical choreography study/test result. Do not write,
patch, or imply approval for Front Planning, 01, 02A, 02B, 03V, generation, or
release.

## Output Boundary

Allowed outputs are source-bound choreography analysis, detailed ordered
movement states, performance-direction notes, a pointer to the completed
`idol-choreography-board` image board, cut-specific motion guidance, and the
existing 01/02A/03V handoff fields only after the handoff gate passes.

For direct work without active handoff authority, write only to the declared
noncanonical study/test path. Forbidden outputs are automatic Front Planning
or 01 activation, changing an `absent` section, canonical 02A/02B/03V writes,
image generation without exact Founder approval, and release or publication
state.

## Source Rule

Primary/supporting choreography requires an actual:

- choreography reference;
- performance video;
- fancam.

A dance-style name, written description, or invented pose sequence is not
enough.

Bind source path, SHA-256, exact segment or selected frames, section, audio cue,
and preservation rule.

## Inspection Method

Read the ordered phrase:

- start stance and weight;
- foot route;
- body level and weight shifts;
- hip/torso/shoulder route;
- hand and arm path;
- head/gaze;
- formation and member exchange;
- accent, suspension, rebound, and stop;
- garment/hair/object reaction;
- source camera readability versus actual body truth.

Separate observed source movement from the current-song adaptation.

## Detailed State And Storyboard Rule

Use `modules/choreography.md` for count-level, head-to-toe movement-state
authorship. Use `modules/performance-direction.md` for expression, gaze, breath,
lighting, material, and camera readability. Use the separate
`skills/idol-choreography-board/SKILL.md` owner to turn the phrase into a
three-or-four-cut image board.

The body-state detail is creative direction, not a fixed machine checklist.
Inspect every relevant joint and visible response, state visibility limits
instead of inventing unseen mechanics, and let human/model review judge
physical richness and plausibility. Scripts may validate only paths, hashes,
counts, dimensions, and exact transfer.

## 01 Handoff

Provide:

- why choreography is primary/supporting;
- selected source phrase;
- member/formation ownership;
- audio cue and timing logic;
- detailed ordered body states;
- the image-based multi-angle choreography storyboard path and hash for the
  selected phrase;
- still-camera readability options;
- what must be preserved and what changes for current members/song;
- tone and safety guard;
- transition into and out of performance.

01 owns the scene and still-camera selection.

## 02A Handoff

Convert the phrase into as many ordered keyframe states as the movement and song
need. Do not force start/end only. Use intermediate, peak, reaction, contact, or
hold states when necessary.

Every keyframe states observable feet, weight, body level, torso/hand path,
head/gaze and facial expression when visible, formation, cloth/hair response,
lighting readability, and still-camera readability.

The current-title member visual controls appearance continuity, not body pose or
camera.

The choreography storyboard's three or four coverage cuts are review and
authoring evidence, not a cap or automatic branch-cut roster. 02A still decides
the evidence-led core-cut and branch-cut cardinality.

## 03V Handoff

Use the passed keyframe pixels and exact source phrase to author motion between
states. Preserve body timing and formation readability. The 03V director owns
temporal camera movement after inspecting the pixels.

Do not let camera motion hide the choreography phrase or fabricate an
unreferenced movement.

The choreography multi-angle board is not the canonical 03V storyboard board.
03V continues to use one to three passed Production Cut images of type
`official_core` or `branch` per board and splits more images into linked boards
under the active 03V contract.

## Quality Gate

PASS only when:

- activation is justified by the section job;
- an actual source is bound;
- ordered body states come from inspection;
- current-song timing and members are adapted without losing phrase grammar;
- still keyframes are physically coherent and camera-readable;
- every full choreography phrase has a labeled image board containing three or
  four actual, approved panel images with materially different coverage jobs;
- each panel preserves the same underlying body-state continuity and names its
  count/time, camera geometry, and choreography-readability contribution;
- a missing image panel, unapproved generation need, stale source hash, or
  text-only substitute holds the storyboard instead of being called complete;
- canonical handoff occurs only after the handoff gate passes;
- non-dance sections remain non-dance;
- 03V can bridge the states without inventing motion.
