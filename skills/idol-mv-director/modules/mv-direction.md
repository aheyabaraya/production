# Active 01 MV Direction Module

Use this module only with active
`idol_song_choreography_performance_mv_map.v3.0`.

## Direction Stack

For each section, work in this order:

1. complete PRE-00-rooted full-storyline lineage, immutable original first and
   active full-text version second;
2. audio and message function;
3. whole-video consequence;
4. director-quality-passed Major Scene and Key Screen Anchor from the
   consolidated Founder-passed FP bundle;
5. governing reference-function combination and direct grammar;
6. this album/title's passed member visual package and text/reference-bound
   space direction;
7. space and visible subject;
8. action, hold, relation, object/world change;
9. performer staging and gaze when visible;
10. still-camera height, distance, direction/angle, depth, placement, and crop;
11. choreography source when active;
12. transition in/out;
13. 02A must-show and reject guards.

Do not start with camera vocabulary and retrofit a scene. Camera is the selected
view of a screen event.

## Route Alternatives

When the Front Planning section is still broad, test at least two credible
routes. Compare them against:

- selected audio;
- target empathy;
- message progression;
- section distinction;
- title visual identity;
- member allocation;
- governing reference;
- generation/shot feasibility;
- continuity and ending consequence.

Choose one and record why the other route was rejected. Do not create
alternatives just to fill a count.

## Screen Carriers

The section may be carried by:

- character or relation;
- world/system;
- action or collision;
- object;
- space/image;
- performance/choreography;
- transition or negative space;
- a deliberate hybrid.

Performance is optional. One carrier may lead while another supports.

## Still Camera Versus Motion Camera

01 authors the still-image result:

- height;
- distance/scale;
- direction/angle;
- depth;
- placement;
- crop and negative space;
- light/material balance.

03V later authors temporal movement from passed pixels. Do not write a push,
pull, pan, tilt, orbit, track, speed, envelope, or stop as if 01 owned video
motion.

## Directorial Branching

For each major event, identify the materially different screens it can support
before 02A fixes the cut roster. A useful opportunity must state what new
observable information it contributes while keeping the parent event intact.
It may reveal another phase, viewpoint, participant response, object or spatial
consequence, insert, aftermath, reprise, transition, or another evidence-led
screen job. These are examples, not required categories.

Also state why that contribution is necessary to the audience read. It may
serve as direct message or empathy evidence, or as a named setup,
complication, reaction, result, intentional withholding, relation, world,
performance, or continuity bridge that makes later evidence legible. It does
not need to restate the message. It does need to add new viewer knowledge,
participant response, object or spatial consequence, emotional landing, or a
necessary edit/continuity job.

Begin from the Key Screen Anchors already reviewed in Front Planning. Preserve
each anchor's visible action or hold, still-camera geometry, composition,
current-title visual inputs, and named reference-function bindings. Expand
from those anchors; do not replace them with a new portrait-led default.

Do not propose `start`, `middle`, and `end` as if labels alone were coverage.
Do not create a new branch merely by changing shot size while repeating the
same action and information. A lens, angle, shot-size, or beauty variation by
itself is coverage decoration, not a new Branch Cut opportunity. When the
governing event changes, return upstream so Front Planning can plant a new
Major; do not invent a third Core on the same event.

## Reference Directness

`full_scene_direct` and `sequence_direct` preserve action, staging,
still-camera geometry, placement, crop, and ordered grammar. Current members and
song timing are substitutions, not permission to redesign the source grammar.

## Section Closeout

A section is ready for 02A when its action, space, subjects, still camera,
reference application, title assets, choreography policy, boundaries, and
directorial branching opportunities are concrete enough that 02A only needs to
select core cuts and author the final branch cuts.
