# Integration Module

Use as the required final pass after the route-relevant song, choreography,
performance-direction, and/or MV-direction fragments exist. This is not a
separate execution skill; it is the rule module the parent
`idol-mv-director` skill must apply before writing the
final map.

## Priority

```text
founder locks
-> target empathy / audience identification
-> member asset identity / asset alignment
-> passed Front Planning target/message and whole-video progression
-> song lock: selected audio, pulse, section timing, lyric/music function, song feeling, audible triggers, meaning guards, and unanswered screen questions
-> opening arc across the actual intro boundary and first-full-hook-to-middle-return consistency
-> whole-video director control: direction form, message/viewer-information flow, sequence progression, still-frame camera progression, and ending memory
-> Front Planning section role, major scene flow, member/reference assignment, and choreography presence policy
-> selected section carrier / director route: performance_body, story_narrative, object_system, camera_composition, environment_insert, or hybrid
-> carrier-specific readability: member/body/performance evidence when visible; world/object/system evidence when non-member-led
-> choreography presence policy: primary, supporting, minimal body/gaze/hold guard, or absent
-> still-camera coverage strategy: concrete shot scale, height, direction/angle, depth, composition, crop, lens feel, and transition progression tied to story function; repeated and changed views both preserve or reveal a named screen job; temporal movement waits for 03V after passed pixels
-> MV direction strength: world, motif, camera/composition, memory frame, and the chosen sequence grammar
-> MV insert strength: environment insert, foreground detail, or empty-scene cut with one clear dramatic/screen job and handoff
-> performance readability when the chosen carrier uses member, unit, group, or body evidence: camera-readable face, hands, feet, spacing, formation, body line, and pose landing
-> choreography clarity when the chosen carrier uses dance or body-action evidence: point phrase, route, weight transfer, impact, and release
-> edit section arc and multi-trigger logic
-> derivative convenience
```

## Rules

### Song First

Song feeling and section function guide choreography, performance direction,
and MV direction after Founder locks, target empathy, member assets, passed
Front Planning, and the 00A song lock are preserved. If a visual or
choreography idea fights the song section, adapt the idea first. If the selected
audio changes pulse, lyrics, section timing, audible triggers, meaning guards,
or hook behavior, update 00A before revising 01.
Use selected audio and the whole-video progression as the scene source. Lyrics
guard meaning or trigger a scene only when the line is the actual audible turn;
do not illustrate each lyric. Audio energy and pulse create pressure and
questions; passed Front Planning and 01 decide how the body, environment,
object, material, and still view answer them.

### Target Empathy

The message is what AURORA-M wants to leave behind. Target empathy is how the
viewer receives that message as their own feeling, situation, desire, or
self-image. Design that reception as a sequence arc with selected anchor
moments. A member does not need to act as the viewer's emotional proxy in every
beat; distance, withholding, observation, contradiction, world process, or
performance release may be necessary for the later anchor to land.

### Asset Alignment

Member role, visual identity, vocal/rap rail, and local references are hard
constraints unless the founder approves a new direction.

### One Hook

The song hook, optional choreography point phrase, performance image moment, and MV memory
frame should converge toward the same core message. They do not need to use the
same literal moment or repeat the same explanation. Together they should move
the viewer through the locked message distribution while retaining song feeling
and member identity where visible. If they diverge, use founder locks,
target empathy, member identity, and the passed Front Planning message and hook as
the tie-breaker in that order.

Across the actual intro boundary, ordinarily around 8-12 seconds when supported
by the selected song, establish the opening's music-and-screen identity,
complete the viewer promise or question, and place the hook seed or intentional
hold. The first full hook and at least one middle return preserve one
recognizable musical and visual memory carrier; the middle return develops
meaning, knowledge, relation, camera relation, object/world state, member
ownership, scale, or consequence.

### Storyline-Led Direction

01 must direct the track as a coherent full-song music video across the selected
song timeline. Each
meaningful sequence unit should advance the viewer's relation through change,
withholding, accumulation, contrast, release, or afterimage. An individual
section may deliberately hold a story/object/member state when that duration
builds pressure or lets performance land. Repeated hooks can repeat the musical
hook, but across their returns the screen meaning, knowledge, camera relation,
object state, member ownership, scale, or consequence must develop.

### Camera-Readable Choreography

At choreography keypoints, face, hands, full body, spacing, weight shift, and
pose landing must remain readable. Performance direction owns readability when a
member/body route is the carrier. For story-world, object-system,
camera-composition, environment-insert, or empty-scene sections, performance
direction is only an identity/readability guard and must not force a dance
phrase into the cut. MV direction can intensify member-led moments but should
not hide the required body evidence.

### Distributed Completeness

Choreography is one possible physical realization of a cut, not the whole cut.
Each cut owns one dominant job: story information, relation, world/object state,
performance release, suspense/hold, transition, visual memory, or a selected
message/empathy turn. It must still carry the concrete audio cue, readable
screen event or intentional hold, composition/camera decision, identity when
visible, and continuity/edit handoff needed to make that job producible.

The sequence—not every cut independently—must carry target empathy, song
feeling, member identity, MV world, style, message progression, and ending
memory. Do not fill `message_function` or `empathy_function` merely to make a
cut look complete. Repetition is valid when it develops or deliberately
withholds knowledge, consequence, ownership, scale, camera relation, screen
meaning, or rhythmic pressure. Variation is valid only when it creates a
readable screen function rather than novelty alone.

When a cut is informed by external reference mining, keep the raw source
capture traceable in the research artifact and integrate only the recombined
decision into 01. Do not flatten raw scene, outfit, action, and camera details
into vague mood words before 02A.

Environment inserts, foreground details, and empty-scene cuts are valid when
they carry one clear job, music cue, visible subject or state, still-view behavior,
edit trigger, and handoff. An explicit message role is optional. They may build
tension, hide information, alter rhythm, establish material/world state, or
leave an afterimage; they must not become generic filler between member shots.

### Camera Coverage

02A should preserve the chosen route as a camera decision, not only as a mood or
scene label. When there is a real unresolved risk of repeated close-ups,
repeated wide reveals, or unclear same-window packets, compare the camera routes
that could materially change the result: shot scale/distance, height,
direction/angle, foreground and background depth, crop/negative space,
subject/object placement, and why a rejected route would weaken the section
cue. Do not invent a second route to satisfy a count. The selected route must
name the storyline alignment before it reaches 02A/02B; 02B then carries the
selected still view without inventing a new camera idea. Temporal movement
starts in 03V.

### Section Arc

Editing should preserve the section arc. Cuts may follow beat, pose landing,
gaze change, lyric/emotion turn, energy shift, or formation snap, but should not
turn the master into disconnected highlights.

### Meaning Equivalence

When a movement or scene is rewritten for song notes, choreography notes, performance
direction, MV direction, image prompts, video prompts, or edit plans, the action
meaning should stay equivalent. Wording can change for the medium; body path,
level, direction, impact, and release should not silently become a different
idea. A `movement_feel_adjective` may travel as physical motion quality, but it
must not replace the body path, keypoint state, or camera-readable detail.

### Keypoint Continuity

When using a compact three-keypoint structure, the keypoints should read as one
continuous phrase: opening action, amplification or beat hit, then release,
arrival, or intentional hold. They should not become three unrelated image
prompts. Stillness is valid when it serves gaze, emotion, memory, or section
arrival.

### Camera And Visual Progression

Shape camera moves, transition families, light effects, and impact level from
each section's screen job. Do not vary or repeat them by default. Preserve an
exact recurrence when comparison, withholding, escalation, return, rhythmic
pressure, or changed screen meaning requires it; change the route only when the
new route reveals a named screen contribution.
