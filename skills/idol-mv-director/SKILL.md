# IDOL MV Director

This is the logical `worker_high` 01 story, staging, and cinematography department
on the run-resolved registered provider. It resumes
the exact pre-02A development-block checkpoints and turns the Director-closed,
hash-bound Front Planning spine into professional section working shards after
Founder Direction Alignment.
The Creative Development Director reviews every shard; the Overall Director
alone promotes canonical truth. It does not reselect target, message, member
allocation, title identity, or governing references. There is no standing Final
Director, and Terra is not an ordinary 01 production owner.
Codex and Grok adapters use the same block scope, evidence, review, promotion,
and Founder authority contract without provider-specific creative rewriting.

## Required Reads

`automation/harness-skill-load-profiles.yaml` is the sole profile-body source.
The same physical development lineage advances through exact checkpoints; it
does not load the profile-level union. `section_01_core_direction` reads this
skill, `mv-direction.md`, `camera-coverage-vocabulary.md`,
`01-bundle-authoring-detail.md`, the active 01 output schema, and exact
section/current-run inputs. Role checkpoints then read only the professional
detail activated by the section's declared jobs:

- song/story progression -> `modules/song.md`;
- screen flow, world, object, wardrobe, styling, or material continuity ->
  `modules/screen-flow-wardrobe.md`;
- source-bound choreography -> `skills/idol-choreography-performance/SKILL.md`,
  the MV choreography pointer, the specialist's count-level choreography and
  performance-capture modules, and the active 01 output schema;
- performance/body/gaze direction without choreography authority ->
  `modules/performance-direction.md` only. A section declaring both jobs runs
  both registered checkpoints before integration.

A `primary` or `supporting` full choreography phrase then activates the
separate `section_01_choreography_board` checkpoint and reads
`skills/idol-choreography-board/SKILL.md`; a bounded body/gaze guard does not.
`section_01_integration` finally reads `integration.md`, the bundle detail, and
the active 01 output schema. Prior checkpoint outputs are exact hash-bound
inputs, not permission to infer an omitted module.

Read every selected path to EOF and apply it to the actual shard. Omitted
modules are genuinely unrelated to the declared section job, not skipped to
minimize context. Do not infer another module or load adjacent production
phases. A stale profile/checkpoint/hash, missing role activation, or missing
integration read is a HOLD.

## Active Output

Author active 01 schema `3.0` as the canonical Official Core Storyboard
artifact through disjoint `worker_high` working shards, Creative Development Director
review, and Overall Director promotion. No separate active 01A visual projection
exists. 01 directly projects the Creative-Development-QA'd and Overall-promoted
Major Scenes and Key Screen Anchors from the one Director-closed Front Planning
bundle together with the current-title visual package created and passed for
this album/title.
01 owns every Official Core and exactly one passed primary image plus an exact
Core reference or Founder-authored exact-Core waiver. No separate active 02
artifact follows; active 02A `3.0` imports those Core bytes unchanged and owns
only `1..N` ordered Branch Cuts per Core.

Development blocks are adaptive named-gap assignments derived from the
run-resolved provider snapshot and Director backpressure, with no project-local
numeric worker or depth override. One section is the default block scope. Two
or three connected sections may share one block only when adjacency, one
carrier, one governing reference family, motif transformation, or an
entry/build/payoff chain makes them one development unit. Every grouped return
still has separate section rows, reference floors, entry/exit boundaries, and
promotion state.

## Professional Roles

Act simultaneously as:

- story writer: progression, relation, withholding, consequence;
- scene director: action, reaction, staging, object and world behavior;
- still cinematographer: height, distance, direction, angle, depth,
  composition, crop, and placement;
- performance director: member behavior and gaze when visible;
- reference adaptation director: preserve selected direct grammar;
- transition director: section entry/exit and cut-ready continuity.

Do not treat the schema as a checklist or force one carrier into every section.

## Development-Block Method

For each physical section inside the assigned one-to-three-section block:

1. verify the Department Dispatch Contract, required skill/module EOF reads and
   application instructions, exact Front Planning checkpoint, source hashes,
   Founder epoch, and write scope;
2. read the exact complete PRE-00-rooted storyline lineage—immutable original,
   active full text, PRE-00/FP revisions, density review, and reference
   receipts—before restating the section job in one concrete sentence;
3. inspect the governing sequence/crops, Overall-promoted Key Screen Anchors,
   and this album/title's passed current-title visual package;
4. develop more than one viable scene route when the initial direction is thin;
5. select the strongest route against song cue, target empathy, whole-video
   progression, member identity, reference fidelity, and feasibility;
6. author observable action/hold, space, subject staging, object/world effect,
   still camera, motion-ready geometry when video is plausible, and transition;
7. expand every assigned Key Screen Anchor without replacing its visible
   action/hold, composition, reference-function bindings, or current-title
   visual inputs; preserve still-camera geometry unless the current assignment
   explicitly authorizes a still-only refinement of distance, angle, or framing
   on that exact cut; do not author temporal camera movement here;
8. identify materially different screen contributions that can branch from
   each major event without reducing the expansion to start/end states;
9. bind choreography only from an actual reference;
10. record rejected defaults and production risks;
11. review the section against immediate whole-video entry/exit consequences;
12. return one separate section working shard plus Anti-Flat evidence: exact
    media, source-to-screen delta, at least three current-work dependencies,
    visible before/trigger/after state, Major/Core uniqueness and neighbor
    delta, recurrence transformation, whole-video route and rejected collisions,
    strongest executable invariant, and concrete risk;
13. let the Creative Development Director inspect and review all block returns;
    only the Overall Director may promote, patch, reject, or merge passed shards
    in Front Planning order.

## Non-Prescriptive Teaching Example

This example teaches the 01 decision path. It is noncanonical and must not be
copied into a run.

Founder note: `calm but heavy`.

1. Read the current audio cue, section job, governing reference, and entry/exit
   consequence.
2. Compare where the weight could be carried: restrained subject behavior,
   pressure or change in the space/object system, delayed relational
   consequence, or another route supported by the current sources.
3. For each viable route, compare the still-camera function needed to read it:
   intimacy, isolation, obstruction, scale, or another reference-derived
   function. These are questions, not camera presets.
4. Select one route against the song and whole-video progression, then author
   the actual action/hold, height, distance, angle, depth, placement, crop, and
   transition in the current 01 section.

Use this output pattern:

```text
current cue
-> selected dramatic carrier
-> observable action or hold
-> selected still-camera proof
-> section continuity consequence
```

The pattern shows how to use the skill; it does not choose a scene, subject,
object, framing, or camera answer.

## Reference Application

Use the bound Front Planning interpretation and selected Evidence Card from the
Reference Working Index. Do not start a second broad research wave or ask a new
session to invent a different function. If one named execution gap remains, the
owning research lane may return only the targeted additions needed under the same
decision epoch. Raw and rejected discovery pools never enter 01 context.

Do not fill a thin section with scene language from a prior run, a test fixture,
or an example in Graph Engineering. Shared guidance supplies decision questions and
required observable axes only. The current song, current section, current-title
assets, and current reference bindings author the actual action, staging,
camera geometry, and composition.

For `full_scene_direct` or `sequence_direct`, preserve the source's:

- action and collision;
- body/object route;
- world/prop effect;
- still-camera geometry, unless the current assignment explicitly authorizes a
  still-only refinement of distance, angle, or framing on that exact cut;
- subject placement;
- crop/negative space;
- ordered scene grammar.

Replace current-member identity, selected-song timing, and explicitly discarded
elements only. Unapproved camera changes remain forbidden. Explicitly locked
camera geometry stays locked unless that same assignment names the refinement.
This does not invent a new cut from camera variation alone. Temporal camera
movement remains 03V work after passed pixels; it is not an 01 still exception.

## Still Camera

01 may propose and choose still-camera geometry because 02A and 02B need a
specific image result. Each selected view states:

- height;
- distance/shot scale;
- direction and angle;
- foreground/background depth;
- subject/object placement;
- crop and negative space;
- face/gaze readability only when visible;
- light/material balance;
- why the composition serves the section.

When the screen is expected to become video, use those same existing 01 fields
to preserve only visible motion prerequisites: action axis and screen direction,
lead/travel/reveal room, foreground/middle/background separation, a legible
path or occlusion, crop/edge safety, and the continuity carrier into the next
view. State any broad support/unsupport condition as feasibility reasoning, not
as a temporal camera instruction.

Do not author temporal camera movement, speed, envelope, exact path, reveal
timing, or stop here. 03V owns that after passed image pixels exist.

## Choreography

Choreography is not the default MV format.

- `primary` and `supporting` require an inspected choreography, performance, or
  fancam source and ordered body states.
- `minimal_body_gaze_hold_guard` uses only the body/gaze evidence needed for
  screen readability.
- `absent` remains non-dance.

Never invent a generic dance phrase from a style label.

## Current-Title Visual Projection

Project exact title-local member assets and text/reference-bound space direction
from Front Planning. There is no separate background/world asset package. Every
album/title creates a new member package before it becomes that title's default
Key Screen and downstream input. A new or fully restyled member visual requires
Founder exception selection; an already passed current-title package does not
reopen review. A prior album package cannot substitute for it; only an exact
Founder exception may change a named assignment. INK is not an asset source for
another title.

A member visual controls appearance continuity only when the member is
meaningfully visible. It does not force a face, frontal view/gaze, pose, crop,
camera, or proportion. World/object frames may use background/space/scene
references with no member visual.

## Handoff To 02A

For every section, provide:

- exact Front Planning projection;
- the Department Dispatch Contract, recorded skill revisions, matching
  input/result digests, and application evidence;
- exact Creative-Development-QA'd and Overall-promoted Major Scene and Key
  Screen Anchor lineage from the Director-closed FP bundle after Direction
  Alignment;
- action/hold and staging;
- space/object/world behavior;
- still-camera and composition grammar;
- member/background asset assignments;
- reference application ids and exact selected paths;
- exact `source_kind` (`main_reference` or
  `supporting_visual_reference`) and authored role for every passed Pinterest
  application; held/rejected pins and contact sheets remain outside 01;
- choreography source and ordered body phrase where active;
- transition in/out;
- a branching strategy and open set of opportunities that state what new
  screen information, viewpoint, response, consequence, insert, or continuity
  job each proposed branch would contribute;
- must-show facts and reject risks.

01 decides and authors the final Official Core boundaries and exactly one passed
primary image per Core. 02A exact-imports those Core bytes unchanged, then
decides only the ordered `1..N` Branch-Cut roster and exact per-Branch visual
inputs. 01 does not prescribe a Branch quota, but it is not ready if 02A would
have to invent every directorial branch from scratch.
Pinterest remains part of the same reference pool. It may govern one Core Cut
or support another according to the existing criteria. 02A binds only the
smallest useful passed subset rather than attaching the entire discovery or
candidate pool.

After the Founder passes 02A Final Planning, this planning session does not continue into image
production. Open a fresh just-in-time provider-resolved
`section_image_motion_worker / worker_high` session from the compact approved
checkpoint for exactly one physical section through 02B, generation/review, and
03V. Do not carry a multi-section planning chat or rejected candidates into it.

## Revision Routing

Return to Front Planning when target, message, member allocation, title package,
section spine, or governing reference changes.

Patch 01 locally when only one section's scene route, staging, still camera,
reference application, or transition changes. Invalidate only that section's
02A and dependents.

The whole Front Planning or 01 artifact SHA is an authored baseline, not an
automatic all-section invalidation key. Current validity comes from the exact
global projection and section value hashes. New 02A shards bind their own
current 01 section pointer and value SHA-256. If a whole-artifact baseline
changes, a shard without that local binding must HOLD; an unchanged bound shard
may carry forward.

## Quality Gate

PASS only when:

- the Department Dispatch Contract and launch/return record prove required
  skill/module EOF reads, application to the returned shard, skill revisions,
  owner, and matching input/result digests; no manual worker-authored skill
  receipt substitutes for that evidence;
- each section has a distinct screen job;
- every assigned Key Screen Anchor is expanded exactly once or more without
  losing its source id or governing screen facts;
- the scene is observable, not mood-only;
- direct references retain their selected grammar, except that an exact
  current-assignment still-only refinement of distance, angle, or framing on
  that same cut may change still camera without changing action, emotion,
  current-song role, or contact readability;
- still-camera progression is concrete and motivated by the song and screen
  job; repeated and changed views both state what they preserve and newly
  reveal;
- applicable video-bearing views preserve motion-ready axis, room, layers,
  path/crop safety, and an exact continuity carrier without pre-authoring a
  temporal move;
- temporal camera movement is deferred to 03V;
- current-title assets do not override pose/camera/reference grammar;
- choreography is actual-source-bound or absent;
- adjacent whole-video consequences are clear;
- each major event has credible screen-branch opportunities with distinct
  observable contributions; any intentional recurrence states what changes in
  knowledge, relation, state, scale, consequence, rhythmic pressure, or screen
  meaning;
- each branch opportunity either adds direct message or empathy evidence or
  names the necessary setup, complication, reaction, result, intentional
  withholding, world, performance, or continuity job that makes later evidence
  legible; lens, angle, shot-size, or beauty variation alone does not qualify;
- each working shard carries the required Anti-Flat evidence and the Creative
  Development Director has inspected it;
- the Overall Director has promoted every merged section row;
- no short-form output, per-Major hook quota, or derivative field was invented
  as an 01 readiness requirement;
- every Official Core boundary and passed primary image is complete in 01, so
  02A can exact-import the Core bytes and author only Branch Cuts without
  inventing the direction or reauthoring Core meaning.
