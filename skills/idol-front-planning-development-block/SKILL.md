# IDOL Front Planning Development Block

## Mission and authority

This skill owns one noncanonical pre-02A section shard. One physical section is
the default. Two or three sections are allowed only when the dispatch names a
shared mechanism, adjacency/carrier, governing reference, motif, or
entry-build-payoff that genuinely crosses their boundary. Separate section
rows, evidence-coverage summaries, entry/exit boundaries, and holds are
mandatory even in
a connected set.

The Department Dispatch Contract is binding. Copy its Founder epoch, locks,
source bindings, reference mode, external permission, expected paths,
   section ids, input digest, and stop conditions into the launch receipt. This
worker is not the CDD, Overall Director, Founder, source hub, prompt compiler,
generation executor, or promotion authority. It cannot interpret Founder chat
into a lock, reselect target/song/member visuals, rewrite another section,
merge canonical bytes, or spawn a worker. Return to the CDD; only the existing
director route may review and promote.

After Founder Direction Alignment, the same physical block and Overall/CDD
decision epoch proceeds through internal FP Close, 01, and 02A. Phase receipts,
fresh input digests, and Director review still exist, but there is no ordinary
Founder refill or approval stop between them. Interrupt only for a new/full
restyle member visual, a material departure from the aligned direction, or a
concrete identity, safety, source, or feasibility conflict. The exact FP Close
storyline remains hash-bound and is shown again with 01/02A at 02A Final
Planning.

registered baseline is:

4. Exact current-run CDD checkpoint, selected audio/section map, 00A/Front Planning
   checkpoint, Founder locks, title member-visual bindings, and the declared
   `section_reference_set` derivatives.

At 01, do not keep this development-block skill in every detail checkpoint.

post-02A section lineage.

Record each path, revision, SHA-256, EOF completion, and activation checkpoint
in the runtime receipt. Fresh phase input digests are required when the same
block advances FP Close -> 01 -> 02A. Role-specific 01 modules come only from
the selected profile checkpoints; do not treat omitted unrelated modules as a
quality shortcut. A stale hash, missing read, missing role activation, or
missing exact checkpoint is a hold.

## Boundaries and source use

Consume the already accepted target, message, selected audio, current title
visuals, governing references, and section dispatch. Do not repeat broad
research. Search is allowed only for dispatch-granted unresolved named
functional gaps and only against the supplied source families. Search count is
adaptive to professional decision readiness, not a floor or ceiling.
Every selected visual uses its exact derivative path/hash and locator; actual
still pixels or a full readable ordered sequence must be inspected. Whole-sheet
mood, a contact-sheet citation, or a function-only summary is not a reference.
Preserve a Founder `full_scene_direct`/`sequence_direct` action, body route,
object/world effect, camera geometry, placement, crop, and ordered grammar
unless an explicit lock or concrete identity/safety/format conflict permits a
change. A current-title member visual controls appearance when the member is
meaningfully visible; it does not force a frontal face, pose, crop, camera, or
body proportion. Face-only is not a downstream 01/02A input lane.

## Phase procedure

### FP working block

Build a section-row shard from the exact Front Planning checkpoint. For each
section, state the cue/lyric or instrumental anchor, dramatic carrier, visible
action or intentional hold, current-song/member/world dependency, selected
reference ids and directness, transition in/out, entry and exit conditions,
evidence-coverage summary, unresolved choice, and hold. Keep the whole-video
message
progression in view: a row may seed, embody, complicate, contradict, release,
resolve, echo, or withhold the message, but may not repeat an unchanged thesis.

The row must show an actual screen event, not a question or mood label. Name
what changes state, what responds, and what the next section inherits. Maintain
decision-ready actual visual evidence for every material Major and Core Slot
without a fixed discovery quota. A section may hold several events; plant as
many Majors as there are events. Sequence is the parent sheet. Select that
flow first, then mark one or two must-remain Cores. Do not count frames 1, 3,
and 5 of one Sequence Unit as three Cores, and do not add one Core per 2--3
second interval. A third Core needs a new Major. Every active visual section
binds at least one applied/combined exact-five one-second Sequence Unit. Author
one change-point kinetic slot sheet per Major at this Close as
`<fp-stem>.kinetic-slots/<MJxx>.yaml` (`kinetic_change_point_slot_sheet.v1`):
a slot only where angle, action, person, or background changes, or an authored
hold with in/out. Bind `section_id` to the selected-route section that owns the
Major in `ordered_major_placements`. Bind `sequence_evidence.sequence_unit_ids`
to that Major's selected Sequence Composition `typed_sequence_unit_ids` as an
identifier exact-cover. Bind `key_screen_inheritances` to that Major's existing
`key_screen_id` values. Those binds are identifier checks only. Do not invent
ids. Sequence stays evidence. Core marks 1--2 nails. Remaining slots are Branch.
Key Screen inherits the Core-bound slot camera; do not re-author composition
there and do not convert Sequence frames, scene phases, or Key Screens into
the sheet. If a
named screen function, exact Core reference, or Sequence flow remains
undecidable, return that named HOLD rather than inventing a candidate.

### 01 section direction

At the named 01 checkpoint, use the exact FP row and current title bindings.
A new governing event is a new Major, not a third Core on the same Sequence.
For every Major/Core event, author a distinct observable screen contribution:
subject state/pose, action or hold, object/environment consequence, still-camera
height/distance/direction/angle/depth, placement, crop/negative space, light or
material, and continuity to adjacent rows. A branch is valid only when it
reveals or extends the same event with new screen information; a lens change,
start/end label, or repeated pose is not a branch. Temporal motion is deferred
to 03V; 01 supplies the still-camera and editorial route. When later video
motion is plausible, preserve action axis/screen direction, lead/travel/reveal
room, depth layers, path or occlusion, crop/edge safety, and the adjacent
continuity carrier inside the existing still-camera/continuity prose. These are
motion-readiness conditions, not a move, speed, envelope, path, or stop lock.

Use current-title member visuals only where the member controls visible
appearance and continuity. Choreography/performance is optional and source
bound; never assume a generic dance insert. Retain exact reference identity,
locator, directness, and source-to-screen delta. If a governing Founder
reference conflicts with feasibility, identity, safety, or output format, hold
and name the conflict; do not weaken it by paraphrase.

For every passed Pinterest unit, exact-copy the FP `source_kind` and authored
role into the 01 reference application. It remains an ordinary main or
supporting reference under the existing disposition rules, not a new Pinterest
application type. Held/rejected pins and contact sheets do not enter the shard.

### 02A coverage shard

At the named 02A checkpoint, exact-import every 01 Official Core projection for
this section unchanged, including its source pointer and source-value hash, and
author only `1..N` ordered Branch Cuts inside each imported Core's
`compile_seed.branch_cuts`. Do not create, remove, split, merge, reorder,
paraphrase, or extend Core truth. Each Branch Cut is an independently framed,
generatable realization with its own observable contribution, still-camera
geometry, composition, and continuity use. Add
event phase, viewpoint, response, object/spatial consequence, insert,
aftermath, reprise, or transition only when it changes what the viewer learns.

For every video-bearing Branch Cut, state the applicable motion-ready still
conditions in its existing camera/composition/continuity/edit-use fields:
action axis and screen direction; lead/travel/reveal room; separated or
deliberately flat depth layers; visible path, threshold, or occluder;
crop/edge safety; reveal or continuity anchor; and any broad unsupported motion
family with a physical reason. Also state a broad supportable family only when
the visible geometry proves why it remains feasible. Do not add an unregistered
field or author the actual temporal move before 03V pixel inspection.

Within the existing Core/Branch camera and continuity prose, make the camera
application reviewable as one contextual chain: screen job -> compared
relations or views -> selected physical geometry and lens/focus consequence ->
continuity carrier -> motion-readiness support or constraint. This is not a new
camera card, schema field, technique quota, or similarity score.

Bind the smallest exact visual-input set in contiguous declared order. Every
Branch Cut requires at least one exact scene or Sequence derivative. When a
member is meaningfully visible, bind the passed current-title member visual
first, then the exact scene/Sequence derivative, then only additional references
with named functions. Preserve paths, hashes, identity, action, space, camera,
crop, and composition. An exact-scene omission is a HOLD, never an empty
binding list. Do not
author 02B's five prompt lines, call an image provider, use `gpt-image-1`, or
generate for schema completion. Return the 02A Branch-Cut evidence only to the
CDD. The CDD reviews the exact shard, Overall alone promotes it, and only the
exact Founder 02A Final Planning approval lets Overall open a fresh post-02A
owner for that physical section's 02B/image/03V lineage.

For each Branch Cut, bind only the smallest useful subset of Founder-passed
Pinterest units and exact-copy each application's `source_kind` and `role` into
the visual binding. Any upstream adaptive candidate pool is decision evidence,
not an attachment quota.

## Anti-flat review

Before each phase return, verify from the actual rows and cuts:

- at least three current-work noun-swap dependencies spanning the selected song,
  current member/product, and world/system/section;
- distinct Major/Core state, action, hold, or consequence, with branches adding
  information rather than camera decoration;
- transformed recurrence and whole-video continuity into the adjacent rows;
- exact source-to-screen deltas and a reason each weaker neighbor was held or
  rejected;
- a reviewable screen-job -> camera-relation/geometry -> continuity ->
  motion-readiness chain for every applicable camera decision, without term
  quotas or a parallel camera card;
- feasibility choices that preserve the strongest idea, not a generic safe
  substitute.

Self-assertion, yes/no checkboxes, abstract mood, and generic caution do not
pass this gate. Write the observable proof or hold the row.

## Output and return

Write only the dispatch-declared noncanonical output path(s), normally one
`harness_section_slice.v1` shard per section plus an aggregate manifest when
more than one file is returned. Preserve separate section rows and exact
section ids. Include dispatch id/epoch/revision, worker lineage/attempt,
phase, input digest, required-read evidence, referenced source paths/hashes,
evidence-coverage summaries, entry/exit boundaries, unresolved holds,
`stop_reason`,
and result byte hashes.

Allowed stop reasons are `decision_ready`, `hold_missing_evidence`,
`hold_permission`, `hold_overlap`, `hold_read`, `hold_scope`, and
`hold_checkpoint`. Stop at the smallest decision-ready depth. Return each
internal checkpoint to the CDD for single-flight review, then continue in the
same physical lineage when accepted. Do not promote, present an intermediate
phase to the Founder, alter canonical Front Planning/01/02A, or continue beyond
02A without the 02A Final Planning decision. Unaffected sections and upstream
baselines must remain unchanged.
