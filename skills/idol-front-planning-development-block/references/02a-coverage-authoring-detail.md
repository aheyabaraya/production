## Branch-Cut Test

A Branch Cut earns its place only when it gives the parent event a distinct
observable contribution: another phase, viewpoint, participant response,
object/spatial consequence, insert, aftermath, reprise, transition, or another
evidence-led realization. These are examples, not a taxonomy. A start/end
label, shot-size change, repeated pose, or lens-only change does not qualify.

Every Branch Cut states:

```text
parent Core Cut and derivation
distinct screen contribution
audience-read necessity or named supporting job
where and who or what
visible state, action, or intentional hold
still-camera height and distance/scale
direction/angle and foreground/background depth
placement, crop, and negative space
light/material/wardrobe consequence
current-title and selected-reference bindings
previous/next continuity
expected output ownership
```

When the Branch Cut is expected to support meaningful video motion, add the
applicable readiness facts inside those existing camera/composition/continuity
and edit-use values:

```text
action axis, screen direction, and camera side
lead/travel/reveal room
foreground/middle/background layers or deliberate graphic flatness
visible path, threshold, obstacle, reflection, or occluder
protected crop and edge safety for body, identity, prop, and wardrobe
possible reveal/attention target
continuity carrier into and out of the Branch Cut
broad supportable and unsupported motion families with physical reasons, when relevant
```

This is a still-image feasibility record. Do not author a pan, tilt, push,
track, orbit, rig, speed, envelope, exact path, reveal timing, or stop. 03V
selects temporal motion after it inspects the passed pixels.

If it introduces a different governing event, HOLD and return upstream for a
new Major; do not split a new Core inside 02A. If removing it changes neither
screen information nor directorial function, remove it.
