# Reference-Bound Provider-Neutral Image Authoring

Read this module for active 02A Branch-Cut work, actual visual-reference
binding, active 02B delta v1.6 / prompt-packet v2.6 section-sequential
structured translation,
or a Codex/Grok image-generation handoff. It is
the detailed production contract behind the compact route in `../SKILL.md`.

## Authority Split

- Front Planning owns the selected direction, the new per-album/title visual
  package, reference dispositions, director-quality-passed Majors, and
  director-quality-passed Key Screen Anchors inside the consolidated Founder-
  passed bundle. 01 preserves and expands them.
- 01 owns every Official Core and exactly one passed primary image per Core.
  02A imports those Core bytes unchanged and owns coverage, every ordered 1..N
  Branch Cut, and each Branch visual-reference binding. It does not author
  Official Core pixels or final prompt prose.
- Active delta v1.6 / prompt-packet v2.6 exact-covers one approved production
  scope of `fp_hero_core|official_core|official_branch`. A human or assigned
  model inspects the exact named Cut and every bound
  current-title/reference image, then authors all structured 02B components.
  The compiler may only validate hashes/receipts, exact-copy bindings, and add
  fixed labels, separators, and punctuation. Hashes, paths, internal ids,
  review fields, and provider-neutral execution metadata remain outside render
  prose. The direct prompt revision cannot
  change the retained Branch-Cut lock,
  timing, identity/reference order, explicit Founder locks, or output path.
  Current-title member visuals are attached only when a member is meaningfully
  visible and appearance continuity matters. Major/Key Screen text and exact
  scene/composition/action/object/material/space/camera/light references carry
  the scene world. A Cut-scoped `scene_visual_package` keeps world/look and
  still-composition functions separately reviewable and compiles them together
  only after compatibility review; it is not an independently promoted
  background-only lane. No separate face-reference lane or prior-album fallback
  may be introduced.
- A provider adapter packages an already approved request. It never becomes a
  creative stage or grants approval.

The Graph v2 topology is admitted only after a verified default-switch receipt;
until then these assignments remain shadow candidates and the legacy one-CDD
route stays pinned. After admission, one frontier Overall remains the sole
promotion and Founder-conversation owner. Before G-40 it routes one physical

review and 03V playback; a nine-section title therefore resolves to three
cohorts, not nine CDDs. Each lane/stage allows one active primary and at most one
physical successor admitted by single-writer CAS; a third claimant holds.
Workers remain below CDD and never gain promotion authority.

One explicitly authorized bounded forensic adviser may inspect an actual source
or pixel risk and return evidence to the assigning CDD; it does not decide how
that evidence changes the video. Each cohort receives the causal-only
whole-video spine, exact adjacent boundaries, lossless approved 02A projections,
selected actual references, source hashes, paths, and stop conditions. Each
physical selected file appears once while every Branch-Cut/Core-Cut/packet
binding survives. It does not receive or reopen unrelated cohort cuts, raw
research/history, prior chat, a whole contact sheet, or the full 01/02A/02B
monolith. The cohort lineage performs 02A validation, v1.6 section
compile/review, Production Cut generation/review, and 03V motion/video work,
then returns one cohort completion or HOLD receipt to Overall. It pauses after
generated Production Cut inspection for its V-Ci CDD review and may resume only
through the registered successor CAS. 04 edit/QC begins separately from the 03V
handoff.
The adviser never fills missing creative truth, touches another section, or
rewrites the video. Direct scripts, not an LLM validator, check schema,
hash, path, exact-copy, and output presence. A missing lock or a new
action/camera/reference/style/message decision returns to the continuous CDD
lineage. A broad synthesis profile is reserved for three or more named stages,
a whole flow, or cross-artifact production-changing validation. Ultra is never an
ambiguity escalation and requires the separately registered Founder-selected
complete full-flow route. Switch models or sessions only at a durable hash-bound
artifact or section boundary.

For actual references and generated Production Cut images, an explicitly
authorized bounded forensic adviser may return observable
pixel facts, locked-rubric compliance, technical defects, uncertainty, and a
reversible technical pass/hold/rerun. It may not choose among creatively valid
images, apply a reference, rewrite prompt/motion intent, or accept a section or

promotes. The same split applies to later 04FX/05 evidence: direct scripts
first, then one bounded adviser pass only
for a named actual-asset risk that the existing section lifecycle cannot cover.

For the Director decision handoff, the adviser sends source-hashed actual-frame decision
sets and their receipts: the smallest decision-ready set of one through ten
selected frames/crops. It may inspect a wider pool but must never send a whole contact sheet,
unbounded pool, all generated frames, or prose in place of pixels. The current
song's audible turns, actual core-cut composition, and named review question
determine the count; no fixed count, run average, or per-section quota is
allowed. Every
decision-bearing action, camera,
composition, world, reference application, or boundary change maps to a frame.
A family is contiguous cuts that truly share one Director decision for those fields.
Every frame carries actual path, SHA-256, source/generated id, mapped core-cut

boundary. If more than ten directionally distinct decisions need Director

time-bounded clip or ordered start/change/peak/end bundle. Completed 03V and

diagnostic stills do not substitute for motion/edit evidence.

## Active Core-Cut / Branch-Cut Flow

```text
director-proposed Major -> passed album/title visuals or exception-selected new visuals
-> director-quality-passed Major and Key Screens
-> Founder Direction Alignment
-> internal FP Close with the exact detailed storyline
-> role-routed 01 current-song direction expansion
-> 01 Official Cores and exactly one passed primary image per Core
-> 02A immutable Core imports and uncapped ordered Branch Cuts
-> actual visual_reference_bindings on every Branch Cut governed by a selected visual reference
   (omit only when no visual reference applies to that Branch Cut)
-> CDD reviews the exact 02A bytes and sends the reviewed milestone upward
-> Founder records the exact G-40 decision on the FP Close + 01 + 02A projection
-> Overall promotes that exact approved boundary
-> fresh provider-resolved post-02A image-motion section lineage with spine, boundaries, references, and hashes
-> exact visual inspection -> active delta v1.6 structured authoring / packet v2.6 fixed serialization -> noncanonical 02B section artifact
-> numbered Founder section/Core-Cut/Branch-Cut review; repeat sections in song order
-> lossless merge of all passed sections -> 02B schema + authoring validation
-> exact request selection -> derived provider-neutral image-generation requests
-> explicit Founder review and exact current-job generation/attachment/paid approval when applicable
-> current-session Codex imagegen or Grok grok_image image_edit/image_gen,
   explicit OpenAI gpt-image-2 API, or explicitly authorized xAI API adapter;
   no cross-provider alias or fallback
-> bounded-parallel dispatch ledger -> run-owned output SHA-256 and pixel inspection
-> passed Production Cut Review -> 03V motion/video work -> CDD completion/hold return
-> separate 04 section edit/QC execution unit
```

Prior packet generations are historical evidence only and are not accepted as
active authoring inputs.

## What The Model Must Consume Or Inspect

Before authoring a Branch-Cut prompt blueprint, verify every selected reference's
bound `interpretation_receipt`: owner path/SHA-256, row JSON pointer, canonical
value digest, revision, selected cell/crop, and current 01 application. The
first-read must already satisfy the media-shaped observation contract in
`skills/idol-front-planning-director/references/director-operating-contract.md`
(top-level H2 `## 02B-Shaped Source Observation`): nested still observation for
each selected
surface, and sheet flow observation when the parent is multi-frame. Function-
only, mood-only, whole-sheet mood, or incomplete nested leaves are holds—reopen
first-read rather than authoring 02B from purpose labels.

Read its matching `active_unit.visual_references[]` row and follow
`inspection_action` exactly:

- `consume_bound_interpretation`: use the receipt without reopening the pixels
  for a new meaning;
- `inspect_and_create_or_revise_receipt`: inspect the exact original or selected
  cell/crop only for a new source or an allowed reopen, then write the new
  receipt revision and invalidate affected downstream projections;
- `inspect_current_generated_asset`: inspect the current generated image for
  review or prompt bridging without changing its upstream reference meaning;
- `hold_missing_interpretation_receipt`: stop authoring until the owner creates
  or restores the receipt.

An interpretation may reopen only when source bytes or the selected cell
changed, the lock is incomplete, selected-audio meaning changed,
identity/safety/feasibility conflicts, or the Founder explicitly requests
re-review. A model, effort, agent, or session change is not a trigger.

Use the bound interpretation together with:

- the parent Core Cut's primary audio/section cue and visible event;
- the Branch Cut's distinct directorial job, action, composition, still camera,
  shot scale, space/object consequence, required
  visibility, action cue, and continuity;
- the current album/title's passed member visual when meaningfully visible,
  followed by the governing scene/sequence reference and any supporting
  component reference in the one combined order;
- the selected reference's source identity, role, preserve locks, discard
  locks, target cut/section, and order;
- the smallest relevant current-title member-visual subset when a member is
  meaningfully visible;
- adjacent Branch Cuts under the same Core Cut and immediate same-section Core
  Cuts needed to prevent repeated composition or a broken handoff.

The legacy `must_inspect_pixels: true` flag means the receipt must prove the
required pixel inspection; it does not order every downstream worker to
reinterpret the image. A reference remains usable only while its canonical path
and SHA-256 match. The original stays available for provenance and separately
approved attachment. A contact sheet contributes only its exact selected
cell/crop; a missing selection is a hold, never a whole-sheet fallback.

## Model-Authored 02B Prompt Blueprint

Every active 02B delta packet owns:

```yaml
direct_prompt_revision:
  authoring_owner: model_authored_02b
  authoring_inspection_receipt:
    source_02a_branch_cut_sha256:
    inspected_visual_reference_binding_ids: []
    inspected_current_title_asset_ids: []
    authored_component_order: []
  prompt_blueprint:
    identity_reference_rule:
    scene_intent:
    visible_details:
      background_and_space:
      still_camera_and_composition:
        camera_height:
        shot_scale_distance:
        direction_angle:
        depth_design:
        subject_placement:
        crop_negative_space:
        lens_feel:
      lighting_color_material:
      render_quality: 4K, high quality, high detail
    action_cue:
      subject_state_or_pose:
      visible_action_or_hold:
      object_environment_response:
    avoid:
```

The fields are written by the assigned human/model after inspecting the actual
02A Branch Cut and every selected image. Scripts may validate the exact
inspection receipt and serialize fixed labels, but may not select a sentence
fragment, prepend a camera idea, infer a gesture, add a style phrase,
manufacture a negative prompt, or claim that an image was inspected.

- `scene_intent` names who or what is visible, the concrete space/object
  system, the exact Branch-Cut action or intentional hold, and the selected
  reference/current-title consequence when applicable.
- `visible_details` separately authors current-title background/space,
  still-camera height, distance/scale, direction/angle,
  foreground/background depth, placement, crop/negative space, lens feel, and
  light/color/material. Face/gaze appears only when visible.
- `action_cue` separately authors subject state/pose, visible Branch-Cut
  trigger/change/hold, and object/environment response. Temporal camera response
  remains 03V truth. Raw BPM, timecode, process ids, or lyric text are not
  substitutes.
- `avoid` holds Branch-Cut-specific visual drift, rejected camera defaults, actual
  reference discard locks, anatomy/render risks, and prohibited layout/text.

Before sealing the model-authored 02B packet, its owner reads the five rendered
lines once as generator prose. Reject sentence fragments, dangling modifiers,
slash-delimited keyword heaps, duplicated clauses, placeholder brackets, broken
bilingual grammar, internal ids/paths, or a negative phrase that contradicts a
positive lock. A resumed section lineage does not reopen this authorship pass:
it verifies the packet hash, named fields, upstream locks, and expected output.
Compact means one coherent instruction per field, not telegraphic language.

The complete 02B prompt blueprint must preserve the Branch Cut's exact
`space_or_object`, `visible_action`, `composition_lock`, `camera_treatment`, and
`render_facing_action_cue` in their owning fields. It must also preserve the
selected 01 reference direction and structured camera axes. This exactness is
how compact 02B storage avoids thinning the plan.

If `choreography_presence_policy` is `absent` or
`minimal_body_gaze_hold_guard`, every generator-facing blueprint field omits
all dance/choreography tokens, including negative wording such as “no dance.”
Write only the actual body, gaze, object, environment, hold, or camera event.
For `primary` or `supporting`, use only the upstream-authored movement; no
script may supply generic idol choreography.

Treat closed values in this route as routing or permission categories, not
creative choices. `frame_subject_mode`, choreography-presence policy,
face-readability, identity-reference policy, attachment permission, provider,
and approval status may remain normalized because downstream behavior depends
on them. Cut role, layout family, shot scope/crop, head/gaze relation,
occlusion detail, action, camera geometry, and composition remain open
scene-specific authorship. Legacy labels may summarize an already chosen
decision but may neither propose it nor replace its observable prompt prose.

## Actual Visual-Reference Binding

When a selected visual reference governs the Branch Cut, add an ordered
`visual_reference_bindings[]` row to that Branch Cut; a function-only note is not a
substitute and returns to 01/01R as upstream evidence or a hold:

```yaml
- binding_id: VRB_...
  asset_id:
  source_owner:
  actual_path:
  sha256:
  role:
  reference_scope: scene_visual
  reference_class: # current_title_world_or_background or selected scene class
  order: 1
  must_inspect_pixels: true
  preserve: []
  discard: []
  attachment_permission: planning_and_prompt_only
  source_approval: # only for exact current-job approval
```

This list carries current-title background/world and
scene/composition/action/object reference use. Face-only is the sole approved
facial-identity source for member-visual construction, and retired `no-mic`/`no_mic`
variants are invalid. After promotion, member appearance is controlled by the
smallest relevant current-title member-visual subset on the Branch Cut; no
separate downstream face-reference lane exists. Use the
canonical actual file. Do not copy the same byte into a run merely to
make a handoff; run artifacts keep the pointer and hash. The matching derived
context row's receipt ids and inspection action control semantic reuse;
changing model or session never changes them. `preserve` names the
visible properties that must survive in `scene_intent`, `visible_details`, or
`action_cue`. `discard` names properties that must appear in `avoid`. The
binding role is an open, scene-specific directorial-use label; legacy role names
are optional shorthand. Order describes how multiple references are viewed
together. Neither field implies a fixed number of references or Branch Cuts.

Planning use and external attachment permission are separate. A reference can
be fully inspected and followed while remaining
`planning_and_prompt_only`. `attach_after_exact_founder_approval` is still a
hold. `exact_founder_approved_current_job` requires a run-owned approval source
bound to the current packet, provider, prompt hash, 02B hash, ordered reference
hashes, external execution, and paid generation.

## 02B Structured Translation Compile

Use `provider_neutral_one_cut_one_image_translation_v2_6` with prompt-packet
delta v1.6. The compiler must:

- verify the raw 02A file hash, parent Core-Cut value hash, section exact-cover,
  Branch-Cut
  exact-cover, operational shell hash, readiness evidence, and every actual
  reference file/hash;
- copy the complete Branch-Cut lock and ordered bindings;
- validate the authoring-inspection receipt against the exact Branch Cut,
  binding order, and current-title asset order;
- preserve exactly five authored prompt items and serialize them into five
  canonical labeled lines: Identity reference rule, Scene intent, Visible
  details, Action cue, Avoid;
- add only fixed labels, separators, and punctuation;
- record revision, binding, and final prompt hashes;
- leave all Founder review, external execution, upload, and paid-generation
  gates unresolved.

The validator rejects prompt drift from the hash-bound 02B structured
blueprint, stale or missing files, incomplete inspection receipts, broken
reference order, Branch-Cut/member-visual mismatch, and structured
camera/reference transfer loss. The contextual section review—not a keyword,
substring, or token scan—judges thin or abstract screen truth, whether
preserve/discard meaning is actually carried, and whether an absent/minimal
choreography decision was respected in the authored prose.

When revising render wording is the efficient corrective action, update the
v1.6 `direct_prompt_revision`. Preserve the same Branch-Cut lock and all ordered
visual bindings. The revision records its author, inspection receipt, source
Branch-Cut hash, structured components, and true preservation assertions. The
final prompt is a fixed serialization of this declared revision, not a provider
rewrite. Any member-visual decision, binding/order, Branch-Cut/Core-Cut
ownership, Founder lock, or output-path change is not a v1.1 correction and
returns to 02A. A no-face Branch Cut may retain applicable scene/background
bindings, but it must not receive a member-visual attachment.

## Provider-Neutral Request And Adapters

After 02B validation, `scripts/idol_prepare_image_generation_request.rb`
derives one `idol_image_generation_request.v12` read-model per active
named Cut packet. It copies
the exact prompt text/hash, the current-title member visuals selected by the
Branch-Cut lock, and the applicable scene-reference
order/path/hash; it cannot rewrite the prompt
or execute generation. An active v7 context pack and FP v17 planning source
remain exact correction and authoring context, while the v12 request itself
binds the approved packet, execution snapshot, exact attachments, approval,
and output. One section approval file may exact-bind several
packet rows, so repeated per-packet approval files are unnecessary while every
prompt and input digest remains packet-specific. An explicit selection file
names the only request paths allowed into the worklist, and the shared dispatch
ledger enforces bounded-capacity claims, one packet per call, output-path
isolation, hashes, and the pixel-inspection barrier.
The ready request verifies `4K`, `high quality`, and `high detail` inside the
exact prompt hash and requires highest available quality plus maximum supported
dimensions for the locked aspect ratio.

- `codex_imagegen`: the exact current Codex assignment uses only its registered
  `imagegen` tool. Its backend model identity is not inferred or substituted.
- `grok_image`: the exact current Grok assignment attaches the approved current-title member
  visuals in member order, then the approved visual references in binding order, and
  pastes the prompt unchanged. Grok uses this repository contract instead of a
  Codex-only global image plugin.
- `openai_api_gpt_image_2`: uses the active v12 Founder-gated executor only
  after explicit Founder selection. Preflight
  verifies credential presence without loading a secret or contacting the
  provider; execution additionally requires the exact runtime Founder
  authority and external/paid confirmation. The OpenAI model
  remains `gpt-image-2` only. Every attached current-title member visual and
  scene reference must be allowed by the exact current-job policy and approval;
  unsupported locked inputs hold the request.
- `xai_api_media`: remains exact Founder-selected and default-disabled. The
  executor admits it only when the central lane binds the exact tracked
  provider-profile path/SHA, profile id, model, API version, endpoint,
  request/response contract, and needed image operation. Missing, stale, or
  mismatched registration holds before secret access; it never infers a literal
  or falls back.

Every adapter is called from one checkpointed provider-resolved post-02A image-motion
lineage for exactly one execution section; it does not reuse the Overall or CDD
director session or another section lineage. The lineage remains responsible
through 03V handoff and may resume from an exact durable checkpoint in another
physical session. 04 is a separate edit/QC execution unit. Overall and CDD
director sessions are forbidden as the image-execution controller. Use the
adapter-native surface or an exact provider-resolved section execution from
sealed hashes; Luna XHigh remains optional and never substitutes by fallback. Disjoint
sections may run asynchronously only when packet and output ownership are
disjoint; the central coordinator owns pre-dispatch hash verification, output
hash and pixel intake, whole-video repetition/continuity review, and merge.

Every executing adapter requires a hash-bound current-job approval artifact and
the explicit command flags before a request can become ready; API-specific
upload rules apply only when an API payload is actually constructed. Preparing
a request never performs an upload, tool generation, or paid call. If a surface
cannot accept a required reference or parameter, the request stays on hold.

Choose by execution need, never by a desire to reinterpret the shot. Use the
existing OpenAI route for manifest-driven direct/Batch submission when its
allowlist fits; built-in ImageGen for bounded interactive packet generation in
Codex; a section-scoped LLM session when many locked packets in one section
benefit from chunked execution; and registered Grok `grok_image` when that lane is
explicitly selected. Support for the exact ordered images, current approval,
output intake, and review capacity outrank convenience or nominal speed.

## Chunked Session Contract

- Central coordinator: Overall retains the whole-video spine, section order,
  promotion, and final reconciliation after cohort image-motion and separate 04
  receipts. P1 is the only planning integration writer before G-40.
- CDD creative owners after the verified Graph v2 switch: P2 owns visual and
  reference coherence; each P3-Ci owns the 01/02A screen truth for one
  contiguous two- or three-section cohort; its matching V-Ci exact-carries that
  cohort through pixel and playback review. The legacy one-CDD route remains
  pinned until the switch receipt exists. Overall approves only hash-bound
  milestones and does not become a second writer.
- provider-resolved section worker: one checkpointed post-02A image-motion lineage with one declared
  section, its causal-only whole-video spine, exact adjacent boundaries, lossless
  approved 02A projection, ordered unique selected files with every binding,
  source hashes, output paths, and stop condition. The
  Context Pack v7 section projection and digest prove this slice against the
  canonical source without opening the monolith. It
  performs 02A validation, section 02B structured compile/review, image work,
  and 03V motion/video handoff without filling a missing creative decision.
  04 section edit/QC begins from a separate hash-bound execution unit.
- Image execution adapter: one validated packet, exact prompt, ordered approved
  images, expected output, and approval receipt per generation call, all inside
  the exact provider-resolved section worker. No broad run history or
  all-section 02B input; Luna XHigh is admitted only by an exact selected route.
- Direct scripts: verify schema, hash, path, exact copy, and output presence;
  they do not select creative prose or make creative review decisions.

Each worker returns a hash-bound artifact or receipt. Its owning cohort CDD
checks section boundaries, repeated-hook variation, reference use, identity,
world/object progression, camera variation, mandatory motion anchoring, and
continuity before Overall accepts the reviewed milestone. Never switch models
or sessions halfway through one unresolved creative decision.
