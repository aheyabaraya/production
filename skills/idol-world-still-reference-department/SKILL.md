# IDOL World / Space / Object Still Reference Department

Own one physical image-only reference assignment under the Creative Development
Director. Find and compare still images that make the video's spaces, world
rules, objects, materials, light, and still compositions executable. Return
noncanonical evidence to CDD; never select Founder truth or promote canonical
bytes.

## Required Reads

`automation/harness-skill-load-profiles.yaml` is the sole profile-body source.
The launcher selects
`world_still_reference_department.world_still_pass_a` or the named Pass-B
checkpoint, exact-copies its skill/read bindings into the Dispatch, and
hash-binds the registry/profile/checkpoint in the Compiled Launch Prompt. Read
every dispatched path to EOF; do not maintain or infer a second read list here
and do not load the full reference catalog. Current target, message,
selected-song/audio map, section boundaries, Founder references, current-title
visual constraints, and shortlist/exact-source pointers remain hash-bound
current-run inputs. Missing, stale, or profile-mismatched bytes are a HOLD.

## Authority And Media Boundary

This worker owns image discovery, pixel inspection, comparison, and a bounded
source-to-screen proposal for these functions:

- `background_and_space`
- `object_environment_response`
- `lighting_color_material`
- `still_camera_and_composition`

World/system evidence uses `background_and_space` when the rule is established
by spatial organization and `object_environment_response` when the rule is
established by visible behavior or consequence. Keep `world/system` as a
coverage-matrix axis, not a new downstream purpose-class token.

It may use Founder/run-owned images, registered still-image sources, pointer
shortlists, official public image pages, archives, museums,
photographer/creator pages, and public image libraries when the Dispatch grants
external discovery. Official/original professional primary sources remain the
default. Pinterest discovery, capture, comparison, classification, and quota
coverage belong exclusively to `idol-pinterest-reference-worker`. If this lane
encounters a useful public pin incidentally, return only its stable URL/id as a
lead to that owner; do not open/capture it or count it as World/Still evidence.

The media boundary is hard:

- do not search Bilibili, YouTube, TikTok video, Vimeo, or another video source;
- do not load `idol-video-reference-hub`, video `READ-FIRST`, video analysis,
  playback, contact-sheet, or `yt-dlp` instructions;
- do not download, open, play, sample, screenshot, or extract frames from video;
- do not create a video-derived frame sheet or sequence analysis;
- an already selected Founder/run-owned still frame or crop may be inspected as
  an independent image, but its source video may not be reopened;
- do not research member identity or styling except where an already passed
  member card constrains scale, placement, material contrast, or world fit;
- do not create a background/world asset, author 01/02A, or generate an image.

## Dispatch Inputs

Require one disjoint whole-video or section-cluster territory, named missing

Discover and compare enough independent images to make the

raw search thumbnail on a review board.

## Workflow

### 1. Map Function Coverage

Before searching, write a `world_space_object_coverage_matrix` that assigns each
active section or connected cluster the required space/set, world/system,
object/environment, material/light, and still-composition functions. Name what
is already solved and the exact gaps. A generic mood request is insufficient.

### 2. Discover Still Images

Search existing local/registered still evidence first. External discovery uses
two passes:

1. `visual_ignition`: visible situation, spatial scale, threshold/path,
   architecture/set, object mechanism, material behavior, light, sensory state;
2. `idol_translation`: retain the strongest seed and test its fit with the
   current song, target, member scale, performance/world needs, and section job.

Keep unread search thumbnails temporary until a readable still is selected.
For survivors, resolve creator, publication, brand, archive, or source page as
the lane requires and return `unresolved_origin` HOLD when that required origin
cannot be verified.

### 3. Inspect And Curate Individual Images

Inspect every shortlisted image at readable pixels before comparison. Record:

- exact source identity, source page, local image path, SHA-256, dimensions,
  and provenance state;
- observable spatial geometry, foreground/midground/background layers,
  thresholds and paths, scale, subject/object placement, crop/negative space;
- world/system behavior and the visible evidence that establishes the rule;
- object/prop/material state and its possible environmental consequence;
- light, color, texture, weather, and still-camera/composition function;
- current-song/world/section dependency and what would fail under a noun swap;
- proposed section/function binding, source-to-screen delta, preserve/discard
  boundary, feasibility, mismatch risk, and weaker-neighbor reason.
- one causal Reference Connection: named need -> observed pixel evidence ->
  bounded transfer -> observable screen consequence -> exact section placement.

Atmosphere alone does not pass. An image must solve or test a named screen job.

### 4. Build And Inspect The Mandatory Contact Sheet

Every comparative decision involving two or more World/Still images must use a
deterministic contact sheet. This applies when the bounded worker prunes the pixel-inspected
shortlist, when CDD compares Pass A or Pass B, and when CDD projects four to
eight Founder Discovery candidates. Do not rank, eliminate, combine, or
recommend candidates from loose files or an unbound search-result grid.

Every Pass A or Pass B CDD return must therefore include one or more contact
sheets covering every candidate considered in that comparison. Build them with
`skills/idol-world-still-reference-department/scripts/build_contact_sheet.py`
and the manifest contract in
`references/world-still-contact-sheet-contract.md`.

The sheet must:

- show the full uncropped image with contain-fit;
- display stable candidate id, function, section ids, source identity, and a
  concise authored comparison caption;
- group no more than 16 candidates per sheet and split by function when useful;
- hash-bind every individual image and the generated sheet;
- preserve candidate order across manifest, sheet, receipt, Decision Set, and
  CDD board projection.

Inspect the completed sheet as one comparison surface, then reopen any candidate
whose detail is not readable at sheet scale. Record why each survivor beats its
nearest neighbor. A candidate absent from the sheet cannot enter the CDD return.
A stale or cropped comparison sheet is a HOLD.

The contact sheet is mandatory comparison evidence, not a reference unit or
generation attachment. Downstream selection always binds the exact individual
image/crop path and SHA-256; a whole sheet counts as zero toward the section
reference floor.

## Output Boundary

Return only the Dispatch-declared paths:

- one noncanonical `decision_set` and one pointer-only
  `reference_working_index_delta`, using only active Department Dispatch v8
  output kinds;
- inside those declared outputs' aggregate manifest, the authored
  `world_space_object_coverage_matrix`, contact-sheet manifest, generated
  sheets, and mechanical receipt remain exact child evidence rather than new
  dispatch output kinds.

The Decision Set preserves one row per individual image with
`applied`, `combined`, `held`, or `rejected`, exact reason, function, and target
section/sequence. Include dispatch/epoch/revision, lineage/session/attempt,
input/result digests, required-read/application evidence, unresolved holds, and
the declared recipient `creative_development_director`.

CDD inspects the contact sheet and individual survivors, merges only accepted
pointer rows into the Reference Working Index, and curates the Founder
workbench. Overall alone promotes; Founder alone locks or passes. Raw pools stay
out of director context and the Founder board. After return, wait without
polling for a compact `final`, `hold`, `authority_request`, or declared
`milestone` wake; a structurally valid return is not creative PASS.

## HOLD Conditions

HOLD when video was searched or opened; any comparison of two or more images
occurred without its complete hash-bound uncropped contact sheet; a candidate lacks readable pixels,
source identity, local path, SHA-256, or function; the contact sheet/receipt is
missing, stale, cropped, or incomplete; candidate order differs across
artifacts; function coverage is missing; source territory overlaps another
worker; external permission is absent; a lane that requires verified origin
has unresolved origin; a whole sheet is proposed as the selected unit;
Pinterest was opened or captured instead of handed to its dedicated owner; or
the worker attempts canonical
write, Founder presentation, generation, 01/02A authoring, or child
delegation.
