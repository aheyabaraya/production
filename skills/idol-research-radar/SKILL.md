# IDOL Research Radar

Use this skill before song direction to combine audience and trend evidence,
adopt one main reference song's verified audience as the title-target base,
resolve selected scene-reference audiences as an evidence-backed intersection,
help the Founder lock one primary target, and develop route candidates that can
serve that target. It also supports lightweight PRE-00 inspiration cards,
recurring research radar, and the
conversion of the weekly image-led idol activity report into evidence-bound
PRE-00 questions and, only when requested, independent concept routes.

PRE-00 owns the new-run audience and trend foundation. It does not lock song
production or MV direction. Before a selected route goes to song direction,
the Founder locks one evidence-backed primary target and the radar records the
current trend opportunity, durable signal, expiry/volatility, and do-not-copy
boundary. Only actual selected audio, its analysis, the audio-motion map, and
00A can enter canonical Front Planning, but they consume this locked target
rather than selecting another one.

Profile each material audience, trend, song/artist, genre/subculture,
visual-world/IP, Founder, platform, and market anchor by audience draw, sought
experience/identity, codes, behavior, content expectations, rejection triggers,
evidence strength, and freshness.
Map supported overlap, productive tension, and explicit non-priority audiences;
never add every fandom together. Research is incomplete until it yields an
audience promise, content pillars, familiar-versus-novel balance, observable
screen consequences, do-not-serve rules, and gap-search vectors bound to one
audience intersection and missing content job.

Do not leave that intersection as a vague fandom
overlap. Draft two to four connection hypotheses and type them as actual
audience overlap, shared-reward adjacency, acquisition bridge, or surface-only
similarity. Use the three-layer chain `target -> response mechanism -> screen
translation`; preserve separate evidence and counterevidence from the
song/idol side and the adjacent-content side. Validate context in the original
or official work. AMV, MAD, and fan edits are prohibited from autonomous query,
search, opening, download, capture, playback, inspection, analysis,
registration, citation, response-pattern use, nomination, binding, or direct
use without exact current-task Founder approval naming the source identity/URL,
purpose, and allowed action. A supplied link/file is not approval; edit-rhythm
permission is not scene-binding permission. Leave incidental results unopened
and return `founder_permission_missing` when such a source is indispensable.

Make each connection visually judgeable before the Founder locks it. Curate one
main music-video or other visual reference and one or two supporting references,
each with exact source/locator, why the target would respond, its proposed
transfer function, and mismatch risk. The main candidate demonstrates the
governing audience reward; support fills a distinct function and does not become
an equal direction by default. These are target-selection previews, not final
Front Planning section bindings.

For a reference-led MV, distinguish two meanings of “main.” The selected main
reference song owns the base audience. The governing visual reference owns a
candidate screen grammar. Research the song-specific audience first, then give
every visual scene/world reference its own audience profile and test whether it
narrows that base, provides a bounded acquisition bridge, is surface-only, or
must be rejected. Do not average fandoms or let a visual surface replace the
main-song audience.

Default those previews to official/original professional primary sources:
official MVs, visual/concept films, official teasers/trailers/highlight films,
then only when functionally needed official performance, campaign/editorial,
director/production-company, official film/series/anime/game, or
official/verified creator sources. Reposts are navigation only until origin
verification.

Record the result first as a target content contract: audience promise, content
pillars, familiar codes to retain, novelty or tension to add, song reward,
screen reward, platform behavior, rejection triggers, and do-not-serve rules.
Translate validated connections into later whole-MV content roles: narrative
situation, character, relationship/emotion, world/system, action/conflict,
object/symbol, space/image/material, camera/composition, edit/time, and optional
performance/choreography. Every later reference vector names its connection and
content-role ids. Performance is one possible carrier, not the default answer
to an MV reference gap.

## Default Posture

- Fast PRE-00 cards are the default discovery surface. They should help the
  Founder compare target connections and route ingredients quickly.
- Do not create contact sheets, run reference-song metrics, perform deep web
  research, extract audio features, or write a structured radar YAML
  unless the founder asks for that deeper pass or has selected a route for
  run-owned intake.
- Song direction stays held until one primary target and its trend synthesis
  are Founder-locked in the structured/run-owned PRE-00 contract.
- The LEFT IN THAT NIGHT style starts after Founder selection through a
  run-owned `research/harness-intake-brief-vN.md` that preserves concrete
  situation, message, references, visual objects, lyric seeds, and guards.

## Mandatory PRE-00 Story Spine

Every new run must pass a causal Story Spine inside PRE-00 before Song Room,
selected-audio work, Front Planning, 01, or any downstream production opens.
Candidate routes may use compact comparison fields, but the selected route must
author `storyline_lineage.pre00_storyline_original`: one uninterrupted,
natural-language opening-to-ending MV flow. It is not a `whole_film_progression`
summary and cannot be replaced by one. The full original states:

- situation and feeling;
- locked target -> desired response -> song reward -> observable screen reward;
- the world or relationship mechanism that causes change;
- a compact beginning -> change -> escalation/complication -> peak or plateau ->
  ending-afterimage progression;
- first-screen hook, participant functions, reference functions and exclusions;
- Song Room/audio validation questions, counterevidence, risk, uncertainty, and
  the later owner of every deferred decision.

Five to seven causal states is ordinary authoring guidance, not a cap, schema
cardinality, or machine validator quota. Keep the Story Spine at premise and
causality level: PRE-00 must not decide exact timecodes, final sections, camera,
shot count, choreography, Core/Branch Cuts, prompts, member distribution, or
videoization.

Hash the raw UTF-8 full text and preserve it forever as V001. Never overwrite
or delete it. When a later reference, song, target, or Founder intervention
changes the flow, append a new complete full-text version with immediate parent
id/hash, trigger and reason, changed passages, preserved story invariants, and
authorship. The active pointer may advance; the original never disappears.

Every reference that shapes the storyline carries an exact research-input
snapshot/hash, locator, storyline function, transfer boundary, explicit
do-not-transfer scope, affected story passage, and disposition. PRE-00 linkage
does not authorize direct pixel use. CDD/Overall reviews creative density
contextually across opening-to-ending flow, continuity, state/turn specificity,
reference influence, ending afterimage, and correct deferral of execution
detail. Scripts validate structure and hashes only; no numeric density or
semantic-similarity score decides creative adequacy.

HOLD when a route lacks a beginning, change mechanism, causal progression,
peak/plateau, or afterimage; survives target/song/member/world noun swaps;
leaves Song Room to invent the dramatic premise; uses references as vibe only;
or describes audience reward only as an adjective. Creative readiness is CDD/
Director contextual judgment; scripts only verify the existing required homes,
lineage, and non-empty values.

### Weekly Visual Research Route

This route runs once per week under the cadence and paired-report contract in
`references/weekly-visual-idol-report-contract.md`. This is the narrow
Founder-locked exception to the ordinary model router. Resolve it through:

```text
ruby scripts/idol_harness_shadow_eval.rb route weekly_visual_idol_research --execution-scope weekly_research_report --json
```

The resolved `idol_luna_studio_department` Luna/Max/High automation owner selects
exactly one brand from idols with an official comeback released in the covered
week, records its comeback title/date/evidence, why it was selected, and whether
it was recently featured, then performs the weekly public-source
research, source and image analysis, both detailed self-contained HTML reports,
each report's traceable PRE-00 candidate
interpretation, and any explicitly requested conversion into three to five
independent concept routes. There is no routine Sol handoff or co-authoring pass. Sol/XHigh may
independently verify the skill/contract or judge update quality only when the
Founder explicitly assigns that validation, as in a harness-maintenance
review. The report remains current evidence, not a selected route or creative
lock.

The weekly route is deliberately staggered in `Asia/Seoul`: the integrated-idol
report runs at Monday 22:00 KST, freezes the representative-comeback selection
receipt, records the fixed Loom reference-pool delta, and recalculates the Loom
target/member-demand section from a rolling 28-day window. The one selected
brand deep dive runs at Monday 23:00 KST. The
one-hour separation is mandatory, so the deep dive can consume the completed
field report instead of racing it. Monday 23:00 is the bundle completion
forecast. At that point both reports must have complete evidence, embedded
images, visual analysis, the shared selection receipt, status metadata, the
integrated report's audience/demand calculation, and
report-scoped PRE-00 questions/candidates. One missing companion report, image,
provenance field, or material claim makes the bundle `hold_incomplete`; it
never becomes a thin on-time report. This repository contract does not create
an external scheduler. Complete report images are source-bound, display-sized,
and compressed before base64 embedding, with a per-image optimization receipt;
compression may not erase readable image evidence.

For later research, the completed HTML is a first-class reference evidence
package: read its image and its evidence/meaning/direct
application scope together. A selected figure may guide any declared current
run role, including wardrobe, world, camera/composition, action, performance,
motion, edit, or PRE-00, when Front Planning records the selected surface and
current-run translation. The same-week reference-image ledger resolves the selected figure,
its hash/provenance, followed surface, and adaptation scope. Selection remains
explicit and does not automatically create a creative lock, current-title
asset, or generation attachment.

Use the latest completed paired weekly reports before a concept pass that depends on
current group positioning, competitor pressure, image language, fashion fit,
fandom proxies, or an idol reference. For a Founder-requested concept pass,
turn the report into three to five mutually exclusive cards. Every reference
idol named by a card must be explained through its image dossier: brand
identity, audience, visible evidence, success/rejection pattern, transferable
function, and direct-application scope. Do not turn the weekly reports into a generic
trend list or a simultaneous five-track operating plan.

## Boundaries

- Fast scans use current local context, the founder's prompt, and light known
  reference families. State uncertain current-market claims as hypotheses.
- Do not browse, Bilibili-mine, download, make contact sheets, or run extraction
  scripts by default for a fast scan.
- Reuse active trend pulse, weekly idol-activity HTML, weekly short-form-
  platform HTML when relevant, fashion-season, local IDOL, and still-relevant
  narrative records before duplicate research.
- TikTok/Instagram trend reports use public no-account sources only and store
  links plus observations, not platform media. No logged-in/personalized feed,
  cookies, private pages, bulk download, upload, or platform sync is authorized
  by the report lane. Named single-URL Instagram reference capture uses the
  separate registered hub route outside that bulk observation lane.
- Trend pulse records are starting hypotheses. Current-run research overrides
  them when fresh source support conflicts. Separate durable audience reward
  from an expiring platform expression and record what must not be copied.
- A fast card or generic demographic is not a target lock. Song direction begins
  only after the run-owned PRE-00 contract records a Founder-locked primary
  target, current trend synthesis, and passed causal Story Spine.
- A selected radar requires one `verified_current_run` main-reference-song
  audience, at least one verified selected scene-reference audience, and a
  resolved intersection. `working_hypothesis`, `research_gap`, surface-only,
  rejected, empty, or contradictory lineages remain HOLD.
- Main/support previews must remain source-bound and visually reviewable. Their
  PRE-00 selection does not bypass Front Planning reinspection, disposition, or
  exact section binding.
- PRE-00 findings remain short-term route support unless founder selection,
  review results, or repeated run validation promotes them into
  `source-materials/**` or `reference-library/**`.
- Keep source notes light: title, path or URL, short note, and useful function.
- Public reference mining follows the registered Bilibili and Instagram
  public-source boundaries in `policies/repository-media-security.md` and the
  detailed policy block in `references/research-radar-authoring-detail.md`.
