weekly visual idol activity and representative-idol-brand deep-dive reports. This is the detailed
paired-report route inside PRE-00; it does not replace the active biweekly trend

## One Representative Comeback Brand Per Week

Select exactly one representative idol brand before deep-dive research begins.
The eligible pool is limited to idols whose official comeback release date falls
inside the report's `coverage_window`. Require the official comeback title,
release date, and official source evidence. A debut, pre-release announcement,
unconfirmed comeback rumour, tour, anniversary, SNS activity, reissue, or an
older active promotion does not qualify by itself.
The integrated field scan may compare any number of relevant groups, but the
companion deep dive owns only the selected brand. Record the same selection
receipt in both HTML headers:

- `representative_brand_name` and filesystem-safe
  `representative_brand_slug`;
- `selection_reason`: why this brand is the most useful current case, not just
  the most familiar name;
- `selection_evidence_ids`: exact integrated-report cards, source ids, or
  current events used to choose it;
- `previous_featured_week`: the latest prior deep-dive week or `not_available`;
- `selection_freshness`: `new_brand`, `material_new_event`, or
  `founder_explicit_repeat`.
- `comeback_release_title`, `comeback_release_date`, and
  `comeback_evidence_ids`: the official evidence proving eligibility inside
  the covered week.

Rank only eligible comeback candidates through current release/activity relevance, distinct and
inspectable brand-system strength, target or competitive adjacency to Loom,
available public reviewable images, and what new PRE-00 question the deep dive can
test. Do not select by fame, convenience, or source volume alone. Do not repeat
the prior representative brand without a material new event or explicit Founder
instruction. MEOVV may be selected only when it has an eligible comeback and
wins these criteria; it is not the default. If no eligible comeback brand
clears the evidence floor, set the entire bundle to
`hold_incomplete` instead of producing several partial brand reports.

## One-Week Cadence And Completion Forecast

Use KST throughout.

| Checkpoint | Target | Owner | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Observation cutoff | Sunday 23:59 | report inputs | No later public activity is treated as part of that week unless marked late evidence. |
| Integrated report run | Monday 22:00 | `idol_luna_studio_department` / Luna / Max / High | Execute and finish-or-hold the integrated field report, freeze the evidence-backed representative-comeback selection receipt, and complete the fixed-pool plus Loom target/demand refresh. |
| Representative deep-dive run | Monday 23:00 | `idol_luna_studio_department` / Luna / Max / High | Exactly one hour later, consume the integrated report and selection receipt to execute the one-brand deep dive. |
| Expected bundle completion | Monday 23:00 | `idol_luna_studio_department` / Luna / Max / High | Both source-linked self-contained HTML reports, image analyses, shared status record, and both report-scoped PRE-00 bridges are complete. |

The schedule timezone is always `Asia/Seoul`; local device or worker timezone
must not shift either Monday checkpoint. The one-hour stagger is part of the
research contract, not a suggestion. The representative deep dive must not
start early or in parallel with an unfinished integrated report. If Monday's
report cannot freeze a valid comeback-selection receipt, the Monday 23:00 pass
holds rather than selecting independently.

Each HTML header must state `report_kind`, `report_id`, `bundle_id`,
`companion_report_path`, the nine representative-comeback-brand selection fields above,
`coverage_window`, `source_cutoff`, `schedule_timezone`,
`integrated_report_run_at`, `representative_report_run_at`, `stagger_interval`,
`started_at`, `expected_complete_at`, `completed_at`,
`integrated_report_status`, `representative_report_status`, and bundle-level
`status`, plus `reference_image_ledger_path` and `reference_reuse_order`. The
two reports use the same bundle/timing/status/ledger values and point to one
another. The
default forecast is the original Monday 23:00 bundle target above and must not
be overwritten after a miss. If image provenance, required source verification,
or a material competitor update is unresolved, set `status: hold_incomplete`,
name the missing item in `blockers`, and add `revised_expected_complete_at` at
the next declared Monday 23:00 KST recovery checkpoint. Never publish a thin
placeholder report merely to meet the clock.

Use only these status values:

- `not_started`: no report pass has started; `started_at` and `completed_at`
  are `not_available`.
- `in_progress`: `started_at` is present, the original Monday 23:00 forecast
  remains visible, and `completed_at` is `not_available`.
- `hold_incomplete`: at least one named source, image, provenance, evidence,
  structure, or companion-report blocker remains; `blockers` and
  `revised_expected_complete_at` are mandatory and neither file becomes the
  active completed bundle.
- `complete`: both files exist and exactly one representative-brand companion
  is named; their shared `completed_at` is present; every
  required dossier, material section, and PRE-00 trace row is present; all
  report images are embedded and decodable; the paired reference-image ledger
  contains a matching row for every evidence-bearing figure; and no placeholder
  or unresolved completion blocker remains. Completion after Monday 23:00 keeps the original
  forecast and records the variance; it does not invent a separate status.

Apply the same vocabulary to both per-report status fields. After the Monday
pass succeeds, use `integrated_report_status: complete`,
`representative_report_status: not_started`, and bundle `status: in_progress`.
If the Monday pass holds, use `integrated_report_status: hold_incomplete`, keep
the representative report `not_started`, and set the bundle to
`hold_incomplete`; the Monday 23:00 pass must not start. The bundle becomes
`complete` only when both per-report statuses are `complete`.

## Required Integrated-Idol Report Shape

Use the weekly template and retain this order. The section names intentionally
match the proven integrated-report reading flow.

1. **Integrated conclusion** — this week's market statement, evidence strength, and what
   changed from the prior report.
2. **Style and branding map** — a comparative map of each relevant group's identity:
   audience promise, fashion codes, stage/MV grammar, and the emotion or social
   position it sells. Explain causes in natural language; labels such as
   "fashion fit" are not conclusions by themselves.
3. **Loom fixed reference-pool delta** — one row each for BLACKPINK, MEOVV,
   aespa, i-dle, BABYMONSTER, and support-only LE SSERAFIM. Use
   `material_delta`, `no_material_delta`, or `evidence_hold`; state the exact
   branding, styling, vocal, performance, audience, or member-coordinate
   consequence. Keep the open current-field scan separate. Never import i-dle
   member authorship, songwriting, or composition ownership into Loom.
4. **This week's debut, comeback, and activity map** — the observed release/activity state, evidence
   status, competitor pressure, and the PRE-00 question it raises.
5. **Group visual research** — a full visual dossier for every primary group and
   every idol used as a possible concept reference.
6. **Fandom gender and age proxies** — only source-backed public proxies, with the
   limit of each proxy made explicit.
7. **Loom target and album-demand weekly refresh** — separate observed Loom
   data, external comparison evidence, and D-grade hypotheses; report overall
   age/gender/region/persona movement; one `no_change`, `test`, `hold`, or
   `changed` row for each M01-M13 target/demand hypothesis; compare both active
   fan-persona hypotheses per member; report LSC0-LSC4 Loom-owned SNS cohort
   coverage with source hashes and same-cohort denominators; and report the
   temporary E0-E5 exposure-state delta separately from member identity. Keep
   E4 commerce-only. Show the exact rolling-28-day
   qualified reach × fan-intent conversion × buyer conversion × gross units per
   ordering buyer equation with inputs, same-cohort denominators, date window,
   evidence grades, and gross result. Show the separate availability ×
   (1-cancellation/refund) net-fulfillment layer only when those inputs exist.
   When Loom-owned data is absent, label the repeated scenario
   `not_a_forecast`.
8. **Member merchandise and gender proxies** — only when a trustworthy, relevant source exists;
   otherwise state `not_available`.
9. **Competition, comeback pressure, and schedule hypotheses** — confirmed dates versus clearly labelled
   estimates. Do not present an unverified comeback rumour as a fact.
10. **PRE-00 transition candidates** — repeated or Founder-relevant signals, the specific
   problem each could solve, the direct-reference application scope, and the question that must be
   answered before it becomes a concept route. These are report-scoped
   opportunities/questions, not unsolicited concept proposals.
11. **Interpretation cautions** — contradictions, unavailable data, proxy limits, and what
   should not be concluded from the report.

## Required Representative Idol Brand Deep-Dive Shape

1. **Core conclusion and this week's change** — strategic thesis, strongest evidence,

3. **Success, failure, and response evidence** — what visibly or measurably worked, what failed or

5. **Official release and activity chronology** — release, MV, performance, promotion, fashion,

6. **Target, audience, and fan-response structure** — core/adjacent audience, literacy and codes,

7. **Visual DNA by era** — era-by-era image boards and literal readings of
   silhouette, styling, set/world, camera, body/performance, light, and edit.
8. **Fashion and brand evidence** — official appearances, partnerships, styling
   language, what they prove, and what they do not prove.
9. **Activity, promotion, and performance history** — stage readability, broadcast and social
   cadence, derivative content, and visible/member consequences.
10. **Competitor and release calendar** — confirmed versus estimated timing, competitor

11. **Music, album, and release-structure implications** — evidence-bound questions about sound,

15. **Independent strategy alternatives** — `not_requested` unless the Founder explicitly requests

16. **Cautions and sources** — contradictions, unavailable data, proxy limits, dated
    source ledger, and prohibited conclusions.

## Image And Reference Dossier Requirements

The HTML must be self-contained and visibly image-led like the prototype. Keep
CSS in the file and embed every displayed report image as a decodable
`data:image/...;base64` URI. Remote or relative `img src`, external CSS/font/
script dependencies, and template SVG placeholders are forbidden in a
`complete` report. Source hyperlinks may remain clickable provenance links;
they are not report-image dependencies.

Before embedding, create the smallest derivative that preserves the literal
facts the dossier claims. Resize to the rendered evidence need rather than
embedding an original camera-sized file; use WebP or JPEG for photographic
content and retain PNG only when transparency, line work, or readable text
would be damaged. Each image caption must add an optimization receipt with
`embedded_mime`, `intrinsic_pixels`, `optimized_bytes`,
`embedded_derivative_sha256`, and the `compression_method` or a documented
`lossless_exception_reason`. The provenance row still names the original source
identity/URL or canonical local path. Optimization is invalid if it makes a
claimed face, styling detail, camera geometry, text, or other visible evidence
unreadable; retain a larger/lossless derivative and state why.

Every primary or concept-reference idol dossier needs at least two source-bound
images when eligible public/local source material is available: one identity or
release image and one stage, MV, styling, or performance image. Embed approved
report images directly in the HTML so a later reader can inspect the report
without a broken external thumbnail. Each image requires a caption containing:

- source identity and title or official account/page, publication date when
  known, URL or canonical local path, and verification status;
- what is literally visible (silhouette, material, pose, relation, space,
  object, framing, light, or performance action);
- why that visible choice supports the group's brand position and target
  audience or where that inference remains weak;
- its declared album/MV reference role and the current-title translation
  required before use; when composition, camera, pose, action, or sequence is
  selected, name that surface directly rather than excluding it by default;
- the transferable function, any non-transferable identity code, and any
  explicit Founder replacement/discard boundary;
- a direct-reference application scope: what follows the actual source and what
  current-run change is required. It must not impose a general ban on pose,
  composition, camera, framing, or sequence reuse.

An image without provenance, a visible observation, and an analysis caption
does not count. A source list without the image does not count. If an image
cannot be used with sufficient provenance or report permission, hold that
dossier rather than substituting a generic generated mood image.

For every idol used in a later concept recommendation, the dossier must also
state: existing brand identity, core/adjacent audience, release or performance
pattern, success and rejection pattern, current competitive position, and the
one precise reason it is a relevant reference. Never call an idol a reference
only because the styling feels similar.

## Conversion Into PRE-00 Concepts

- the audience situation and intended emotional reward;
- the proposed song/album, title-track, visual-world, fashion, and branding

  and how the route becomes AURORA-M/Loom rather than a copy;

  and the principal risk;
- source-backed evidence versus the team's inference or creative proposal.

The latest completed paired bundle is necessary current-market context when this route
uses current idol examples, visual references, proxy data, or comeback pressure.
It remains one input beside the active biweekly trend pulse, fashion record,
Founder references, and—after selection—the actual song.
