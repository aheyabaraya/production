# Research Radar Authoring Detail

Read this after `skills/idol-research-radar/SKILL.md` when building a full
structured PRE-00 radar, selecting research vectors, using Bilibili- or
Instagram-assisted public source mining, or normalizing one Founder-selected
route into a run.

## Boundary

PRE-00 combines audience and trend evidence, lets the Founder lock one primary
target, and then compares target-fit directions before song production or
selection. It may not author:

- 00A song-production truth;
- a final MV message;
- a current-title visual lock;
- member allocation;
- a section plan;
- 01 direction;
- imported 01 Official Cores or 02A Branch Cuts;
- 02B image prompts;
- 03V video prompts.

The active structured contract is
`schemas/pre-00-research-radar.schema.json` version `1.8`. Earlier radar files
remain historical records and are not valid authoring templates.

## Smallest Useful Route

| Route | Output | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Evidence And Connection Method

Do not collect an unbounded reference pool. Begin with:

1. audience evidence and the target question;
2. the active trend pulse and current platform/market evidence;
3. Founder references and local source material;
4. the song-direction need and one missing content function.

For a main reference with deeper source ancestry, trace:

```text
main reference
-> original myth, story, genre, historical source, or formal system
-> derived or adjacent work
-> current-route audience reward
-> candidate screen or song function
```

An encyclopedia or NamuWiki-style source may establish names, relationships,
episodes, myths, and derivative works. Confirm important meaning in the
original or an official source. AMVs, MADs, and fan edits require exact
current-task Founder permission naming the source identity/URL, purpose, and
allowed action before query, opening, inspection, response-pattern use, or any
other research action. A supplied link/file is not permission; incidental
results remain unopened. If indispensable and unapproved, return
`founder_permission_missing` and continue the official/original route.

Every connection states:

- source identity and locator;
- literal observation;
- inferred audience reward;
- candidate transferable function;
- counterevidence or mismatch risk;
- current-route validation question;
- whether it is an actual overlap, shared-reward adjacency, acquisition bridge,
  or surface-only similarity.

Surface-only similarity remains test-only or is rejected.

## Target And Trend Foundation

For reference-led work, begin with a song-specific lineage rather than a
generic demographic. Research the selected main idol song's audience as the
base profile: audience definition, viewer situation, desired reward,
literacy/codes, platform behavior, attraction/rejection triggers, evidence,
counterevidence, and freshness. `verified_current_run` means the exact current
run has enough source-backed evidence to reject a wrong target; a reusable
business-marketing row or a director inference alone remains
`working_hypothesis` or `research_gap`.

Then author one audience profile for each candidate visual scene/world
reference. Compare it to the song base across viewer situation, desired reward,
literacy/codes, platform behavior, and rejection triggers. A scene closes as
`aligned_narrowing`, `bounded_acquisition_bridge`, `surface_only_test`, or
`rejected`. Only the first two may enter the selected intersection, and only
with current-run verified evidence. If the intersection is empty or conflicts
with the song base, hold and change the scene reference or explicitly reopen
the main target; never solve it by unioning fandoms.

Draft two to four target connections before selecting one. For each, record the
viewer situation, desired reward, attraction and rejection triggers, platform
behavior, supporting evidence, counterevidence, freshness, and whether the
connection is actual overlap, shared-reward adjacency, acquisition bridge, or
surface-only similarity. The Founder locks one primary target; a surface-only
similarity cannot be promoted beyond test-only.

Attach one main music-video or other visual reference and one or two supporting
references to every connection. Record exact source id and locator, medium, why
the target responds, proposed transfer function, mismatch risk, and a comparison
note explaining why the main governs while each support fills a different gap.
Show reviewable media where available. This helps the Founder select a target;
it is not a final scene disposition or section binding.

Synthesize trend evidence beside that target, not on top of it. Separate durable
audience reward from current opportunity, record expiry or volatility, and name
the popular surface that must not be copied. The resulting content contract
states audience promise, content pillars, familiar codes, novelty/tension, song
reward requirements, and screen reward requirements. Song direction cannot
begin from a fast card alone.

## Reference Families

A full selected radar uses at least one source from each foundation family:

- `audience`: viewer situation, desire, identity, content expectation,
  participation behavior, attraction/rejection, and do-not-serve evidence;
- `trend`: durable signal, current opportunity, lifecycle, platform scope,
  evidence limitation, expiry/volatility, and do-not-copy boundary.

Every creative route then uses at least one source from each route family:

- `song`: phrase contour, hook mechanics, structure, rhythm pressure, vocal or
  rap color, message, or performance intensity;
- `media_narrative`: situation, relationship, character arc, world system,
  conflict, symbol, or scene grammar;
- `fashion`: silhouette, material, palette, accessory, construction, or
  seasonal context;
- `idol`: identity position, audience readability, performance behavior,
  brand bridge, or industry signal.

The two foundation families lock who the work serves and why the opportunity is
current. The four route families prevent a single reference from becoming the
whole creative answer. Neither rule requires equal blending.

## Weekly HTML Reference Use

When a route cites current idol positioning or imagery, read the completed
weekly HTML first and then its matching ledger row. Keep the embedded image,
literal observation, audience/brand inference, transferable function, and
direct-use scope together.

If the current route later selects that figure, Front Planning records it as a
reference candidate or a current-title asset-production candidate. The weekly
report does not itself create:

- a visual lock;
- a current-title asset;
- a prompt attachment;
- an automatic generation input.

When pixels are required for inspection, extract only the selected embedded
figure with the registered report extractor and preserve its report, ledger,
figure, and derivative hashes.

## Research Vectors

Choose only vectors that answer the current gap:

- `trend_hypothesis_fit`
- `song_hook_structure`
- `target_empathy_message`
- `choreography_performance_readability`
- `still_camera_composition_grammar`
- `mv_world_storyline`
- `wardrobe_material_motion`
- `unit_member_identity_fit`
- `generation_promptability_risk`
- `derivative_social_viability`
- `short_form_platform_behavior`
- `source_diversity_raw_trace`

Each vector states:

- the research question;
- sample rule;
- extraction fields;
- one or more downstream roles;
- the reject condition.

Allowed downstream roles are deliberately broad:

- song-direction design;
- candidate-audio evaluation question;
- selected-audio analysis question;
- PRE-00 target/trend lock;
- Front Planning locked-target translation;
- Front Planning visual-world candidate;
- Front Planning reference-connection candidate;
- Front Planning current-title asset candidate.

Do not write later schema paths from PRE-00.

## Candidate Route Standard

Declare a three-to-seven route count. Five is common, not a quota. Hold rather
than pad the set with duplicates.

Each route must:

- present a distinct situation and emotional reward;
- serve the Founder-locked primary target through a named response mechanism;
- connect target -> desired response -> song reward -> observable screen reward;
- name the world or relationship mechanism that causes visible change;
- carry a compact causal progression with a beginning, change, escalation or
  complication, peak/plateau, and ending afterimage;
- state participant functions, reference functions/exclusions, and which later
  owner receives each deferred decision;
- keep only the message provisional;
- name a possible song direction;
- name a current-unit fit without forcing every frame to be member-led;
- combine all four reference families;
- provide a visual-world candidate and first-screen hook;
- state why AURORA-M can own the route;
- state the principal risk or evidence gap;
- provide a `route_seed` for song and later Front Planning questions.

The `route_seed` contains:

- song-direction questions;
- candidate-audio validation questions;
- a Front Planning candidate with message translation, visual identity, whole-video
  progression, reference-connection, and current-title asset questions;
- exact source reference ids;
- risks;
- open questions.

It is not a partially authored Front Planning file.

Five to seven causal states is useful authoring guidance, never a schema cap or
padding quota. Use the PRE-00 1.8 route fields—especially situation,
locked-target fit, first-screen hook, risks/questions, and
`route_seed.front_planning_candidate.whole_film_progression_hypothesis`—before
proposing a new Story Spine schema. That progression hypothesis must state the
causal state chain, peak/plateau, and ending afterimage clearly enough for
Director review.

HOLD the candidate or selection when it has no beginning, change mechanism,
causal progression, peak/plateau, or afterimage; when noun swaps leave it
substantially intact; when Song Room would need to invent the dramatic premise;
when references provide vibe without function/exclusion; or when the audience
reward is only an adjective. Also HOLD PRE-00 overreach into exact timecodes,
final section allocation, camera, shot count, choreography, Core/Branch Cuts,
generation prompts, final member/wardrobe distribution, or any intermediate
videoization. These are contextual Director judgments, not semantic-similarity
or state-count scripts.

## Full-Storyline Original And Append-Only Revisions

For a selected route, `whole_film_progression_hypothesis` remains a compact
index only. Author the actual whole-film root under `storyline_lineage`:

```yaml
storyline_lineage:
  contract_version: pre00_storyline_lineage.v1
  pre00_storyline_original:
    version_id: PRE00_STORYLINE_V001
    parent_version_id: null
    parent_full_storyline_text_sha256: null
    full_storyline_text: |-
      Write the uninterrupted natural-language MV flow here, from the opening
      condition through the ending afterimage. Preserve causal or associative
      continuity and every important change of state.
    full_storyline_text_sha256: "<raw UTF-8 text SHA-256>"
    selected_route_ids: [ROUTE_EXAMPLE]
    reference_bindings:
      - source_ref_id: REF_MEDIA_NARRATIVE_EXAMPLE
        source_json_pointer: /research_inputs/REF_MEDIA_NARRATIVE_EXAMPLE
        source_value_sha256: "<canonical source value SHA-256>"
        source_snapshot:
          ref_id: REF_MEDIA_NARRATIVE_EXAMPLE
          family: media_narrative
          source_type: original_professional_primary
          title: "Exact copied research-input title"
          source_url: "https://example.invalid/exact-source"
          evidence_note: "Exact copied research-input evidence note"
        selected_locator: "Exact bounded locator"
        storyline_function: "What this reference changes or supports in the flow"
        transfer_boundary: "Function transferred; source identity excluded"
        do_not_transfer: ["source identity", "unselected world surface"]
        affected_story_scope: "Opening through first turn"
        disposition: applied_to_storyline
        direct_visual_use_authorized: false
  pre00_storyline_revisions: []
  active_version:
    version_id: PRE00_STORYLINE_V001
    version_json_pointer: /storyline_lineage/pre00_storyline_original
    full_storyline_text_sha256: "<same raw text SHA-256>"
```

V001 is immutable. Never edit it in place. A later change appends a complete
new version—not a patch or summary—with immediate parent id/hash, trigger,
reason, changed passages, preserved invariants, current reference bindings, and
authorship receipt. The active pointer advances only after contextual density
review passes. Density review is Director/CDD natural-language judgment, never
a numeric score or machine similarity check.

The original text contains no exact timecodes, camera geometry, final sections,
Core/Branch Cuts, prompts, choreography plan, or final member distribution.
Those belong to downstream execution layers. This exclusion does not make the
original optional or compact: it must still carry the entire MV story flow.

## Founder Selection Handoff

Preserve the full radar and add:

```yaml
status: selected
target_trend_foundation:
  lock_status: founder_locked
  # preserve the complete reviewed foundation above
founder_selection:
  action: select_one
  route_ids: [ROUTE_EXAMPLE]
  selection_note: "Founder decision"
  next_song_direction_action: design_song_direction
```

Normalize the complete Founder-locked target/trend foundation plus only the
selected route bytes and their cited sources into the run. Do not copy the
rejected pool or pre-author later stages. Song direction, candidate analysis,
and Front Planning may test whether their own output serves the lock; only an
explicit Founder reopen may replace the target.

Valid next actions are:

- `design_song_direction`;
- `prepare_manual_or_suno_candidate`;
- `analyze_founder_selected_audio`;
- `hold`.

## Public Video Capture Boundary

Use public video mining only with explicit approval and a registered
video-reference hub route under `source-materials/video-sources`:

- Bilibili: `scripts/yt-dlp-bilibili.sh`
- Instagram post/Reel/IGTV: `scripts/yt-dlp-instagram-reference.sh`

Public, no-cookie capture may retain one source-hub original plus bounded
frame/contact-sheet evidence as observation provenance only. It may not create
a generation input, edit source, publication source, unmanifested duplicate,
or duplicate run-local original. Bare `yt-dlp`, TikTok,
X, streaming services, and cookie/session capture remain closed unless a
separate registered route and Founder approval exist. YouTube still needs its
own separate Founder instruction and registered entry.

The structured radar records the exact public-source policy in
`research_policy.public_reference_mining_policy`. Missing provenance, account
boundaries, or source-hub ownership is a hold.

## Hold And Finish Checks

Hold a full radar when:

- fewer than four usable source identities exist;
- fewer than three distinct routes survive;
- a route lacks one reference family;
- a route repeats one source to fake coverage;
- a route cannot state an observable song or screen consequence;
- current-market claims lack fresh evidence or an explicit hypothesis label;
- selected-source provenance is unclear;
- the work would require unapproved paid generation, download, upload,
  publishing, or private/account-bound access.

Before finishing:

- validate the file against the active 1.7 schema;
- confirm the declared route count equals the actual route count;
- confirm every reference id resolves once;
- confirm each route's four family lists are subsets of its source ids;
- confirm every target connection has one main and one or two supporting visual
  previews with exact locators, distinct functions, and mismatch risks;
- confirm the main song audience is current-run verified, selected scene
  audiences are verified and non-surface-only, and the five-dimension
  intersection resolves without replacing or unioning the base;
- confirm each `route_seed` remains questions and hypotheses;
- confirm Founder selection routes only to song direction or selected-audio
  analysis;
- confirm no retired intermediate creative stage, prompt, or generation
  artifact was created.
