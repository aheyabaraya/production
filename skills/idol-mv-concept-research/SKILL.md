# IDOL MV Concept Research

Develop three complete, meaningfully different MV concept research sets from
the selected song and the Founder's initial feeling or references. Stop after
presenting the sets for Founder choice.

This is a standalone research skill. It is not PRE-00 Route Discovery, Front
Planning, 01R, or a graph-engineering stage. It creates no creative lock and does not
advance production.

Activate it only when the Founder explicitly requests this standalone deep
concept-research product or explicitly names this skill. The ordinary Creative
Development Director/ordinary `worker_high` discovery wave does not invoke it automatically, and
its three-set/nine-derived/seven-axis comparison profile must not be imported as
the normal studio candidate budget. If a set is later selected, the Overall
Director still admits and promotes only the exact useful elements through the
ordinary song-bound Front Planning route; the chat result never becomes
canonical by itself.

## Input Boundary

Start by declaring the research boundary:

- actual song/audio and lyric source;
- Founder feeling, request, and supplied references;
- permitted local reference pools;
- whether public research is authorized;
- whether existing Front Planning material may be consulted.

Default to `fresh_concept_research`. In that mode, do not read or reuse an
existing `front-planning-vN.md`, readiness artifact, storyline lock, storyboard,
or downstream direction as creative input. File presence is not permission.

Use prior planning only when the Founder explicitly asks to resume, compare, or
revise it. State exactly which prior surfaces are admitted and why.

## Founder Research Floor

Return exactly three complete candidate sets unless the Founder explicitly
changes the profile. Each set must contain:

- one core message;
- three main references;
- three derived references for each main reference, for nine derived references
  total;
- at least seven layered narrative axes; and
- integrated `narrative/relationship`, `world/event`, and `screen/music`
  development.

These counts are a research-comparison floor only. Never convert them into a
scene, Core-Cut, Branch-Cut, song-section, camera, or downstream Front Planning
quota.

## Research Workflow

### 1. Read the song before proposing a message

Extract concrete lyric situations, people, relationships, repeated objects or
gestures, time changes, withheld information, contradictions, and audible
turns. State a provisional listener situation and misread risk from the song.

Do not reduce the song to a mood label. Do not decide one message and then
search for works that merely decorate it.

### 2. Form three different dramatic questions

Turn the song tensions into three materially different questions. Each question
must change the central conflict, relationship choice, external event or world
pressure, and ending consequence.

Reject a branch when it differs only in title, setting, visual style, or main
reference while preserving the same story engine.

### 3. Select three main references for each set

Choose references because their characters, relationship mechanics, event
structure, world rule, temporal form, or audiovisual grammar can generate the
current song's story. Do not select them from visual freshness or popularity
alone.

Treat all three references as one integrated set. Do not assign one reference
only to story, another only to world, and another only to screen.

### 4. Trace and verify the source genealogy

For every main reference, research three useful derivatives or relatives and
record their relation type. Follow
`references/source-genealogy-method.md`.

Use NamuWiki, Wikipedia, or an equivalent encyclopedia to map titles,
characters, events, motifs, original works, and possible roots. Verify material
claims against an original, official, primary, or otherwise authoritative
source before treating them as established meaning. AMV, MAD, and fan edits
require exact current-task Founder approval before any query, search, opening,
download, capture, playback, inspection, analysis, registration, citation,
response-pattern use, nomination, binding, or direct use. A supplied link/file
is not approval. Permission must name exact identity/URL, purpose, and allowed
action; edit-rhythm permission is not scene-binding permission. Incidental
results remain unopened; if indispensable, return
`founder_permission_missing`.

Never claim direct influence when the evidence supports only a motif,
thematic, formal, or screen-function analogy.

### 5. Extract current-song functions

For each main and derived reference, record:

- source identity and evidence locator;
- relation to the main reference;
- exact motif, relationship, event, world rule, camera, edit, object, or music
  function being followed;
- current-song translation;
- explicit replacement or discard boundaries, if any;
- confidence and unresolved evidence.

When a visual source supplies screen direction, inspect the actual selected
image, frame, or time-bounded sequence. A title, synopsis, or whole contact
sheet is not pixel evidence.

### 6. Organize one whole-video story for each set

Author the story. Do not hand the Founder a reference pile and expect the
references to imply a narrative.

For each set, synthesize one coherent MV story with its own characters or
relationship positions, opening state, initiating disruption, causal
escalation, collisions among story layers, irreversible choice, ending
consequence, and emotional afterimage. Decide what actually happens, why the
next event follows, and how the ending changes the meaning of the opening.

The story may be nonlinear, poetic, ambiguous, or intentionally unresolved. It
must still make its state changes, relationship movement, and consequences
legible. Do not concatenate the plots of the three main references. Use only
their selected mechanisms, then compose a new current-song narrative.

Assign every applied or combined reference to a specific story job. Mark a
researched reference `held` or `rejected` when it adds evidence but does not
belong in the organized story. The numeric research floor is not permission to
force all reference content onto the screen.

### 7. Build at least seven narrative axes per set

Treat a narrative axis as a continuing layer, not as one scene. Give every axis
an initial state, pressure, turn, payoff or intentional non-resolution,
collision with another axis, audio anchors, and observable screen proof.

Layer relationship, character, world rule, external incident, object or motif,
memory or time, ethical choice, public/private tension, and ending consequence
as the song requires. Do not force these labels as a fixed seven-part formula.

Map where the axes intersect inside the whole-video story. Seven isolated
subplots do not count as layered organization.

### 8. Bind the story to screen and music

For each set, explain how the relationship, world, and events become visible
across the song. Name the relevant audio section or cue, visible action or hold,
space, camera or edit behavior, and resulting story change.

Do not turn every lyric into literal illustration. The message must progress
through the whole video; individual sections may carry character, relation,
world, tension, negative space, performance, transition, or afterimage.

### 9. Run the three-set comparison

Use `references/candidate-set-quality-contract.md`. Confirm that the three sets
have different dramatic questions, causal event systems, relationship choices,
ending consequences, and screen/music grammars.

If a set is thin, revise it before presenting. Do not ask the Founder to choose
from incomplete placeholders merely because the numeric floor is satisfied.

## Output And Stop Rule

Return the research in chat by default. For each set, present:

1. title and core message;
2. a complete, organized whole-video story in natural prose;
3. dramatic question, causal progression, irreversible choice, and ending
   consequence;
4. at least seven interacting narrative axes with screen proof;
5. three main references and nine derived references, each dispositioned and
   mapped to an exact story or directing job;
6. integrated narrative/relationship, world/event, and screen/music direction;
7. directorial strengths, weaknesses, production risks, and direct-reference application scope.

End with a compact comparison of the three sets. Invite Founder selection,
combination, revision, or rejection, but do not invoke Front Planning or write
any run file automatically.

Write a research note only when the Founder explicitly asks to preserve the
result. Place it under the owning run's `research/` or `research/drafts/` as
appropriate, mark it non-locking, and follow `wiki/artifact-management-system.md`.

## Hard Holds

Hold and correct the result when:

- an existing plan was reused during fresh research;
- the message was fixed first and references were retrofitted;
- a reference list or motif table substitutes for an authored whole-video story;
- the story merely concatenates or summarizes the source works;
- researched references are named without an assigned story job or explicit
  held/rejected disposition;
- the three sets share the same central conflict or ending choice;
- seven scenes or beats are miscounted as seven narrative axes;
- a set lacks any one of narrative/relationship, world/event, or screen/music;
- a genealogy claim lacks a clear relation type or verification status;
- an encyclopedia summary is treated as final original-work proof;
- a visual source is used as direction without actual visual inspection;
- references are listed without current-song consequences; or
- a direct source event is used without member, song, continuity, and explicit
  current-run translation.
