# Founder Visual Review Board Contract

Status: active
Schema: `idol-html-review-board.v12`
Renderer: `scripts/idol_html_review_board.rb`
Decision export: `idol-html-review-board.decision-export.v12`

## Purpose

Use one unified, source-bound local HTML workbench for Front Planning, 01, 02A,
02B, generated-branch-cut, 03V playback, and 04 edit review. Legacy schema
tokens `discovery`, `direction_synthesis`, and `fp_final` remain transport
compatibility values, but Founder-facing labels are Internal Discovery,
Direction Alignment, Internal FP Close, and 02A Final Planning. The board must
not call an intermediate direction choice FP PASS.

The active run owns one stable rendered path, ordinarily
`RUN/review/founder-workbench.html`. Styling review, reference additions,
Direction Alignment, the read-only FP Close snapshot, and official 01 Core plus
02A Branch-Cut review are new source-bound cycles inside that file. Do not
create sibling `member`, `styling`, `core-cut`, or stage-specific HTML boards.

The Creative Development Director curates Decision Sets and inspects all
returned department media before anything enters the board. The Overall
Director alone presents a recommendation, interprets Founder signals, promotes
canonical truth, and owns final creative acceptance. Department workers never
publish directly to this surface. Raw Discovery Reference Pools and rejected
candidate dumps stay out of the workbench.

The HTML is derived review material. Canonical creative truth remains in the
owning artifact.

## Founder Alignment Storyline

Place a recursively expanded, exact source-hashed storyline readout beside the
pixels on every card. The readout is authored while the Director inspects and
combines the selected references, and it increases in resolution across the
same stable workbench:

1. Internal Discovery: concrete whole-video route;
2. Direction Alignment: whole-video route, selected video form, message/Major
   choices, every section's visible progression, and each section's Pinterest
   comparison cohort;
3. Internal FP Close: exact whole-video, section, planned-cut, and Key Screen
   storyline, retained as a hash-bound read-only snapshot;
4. 02A Final Planning: that exact FP Close storyline beside the immutable 01
   Official Cores and the resulting 02A Branch-Cut plan.

The cut-level readout names visible subject and state, body/hand/face behavior,
action or hold, object/environment response, camera height/distance/direction/
angle/depth, placement, crop/negative space, lens feel, light/color/material,
reference contribution, and continuity. It must be detailed enough for 01 to
author Official Core boundaries and primary images, and for 02A to allocate
only Branch Cuts beneath those immutable imports without inventing a new story.

When the Founder says the displayed pixels do not match the Director's text,
set `mismatch_state: reinspect_required`, show a concrete mismatch note, and
reopen the exact bound image/cell/frame inspection action. Reconcile the
smallest owning storyline field in conversation, then reproject only affected
descendants. A summary from an earlier or compacted session cannot override
the reopened pixels.

## Fixed Reading Order

1. compact Founder startup input and the director-ready recommended sequence
   and reference-use map;
2. target and message summary;
3. whole-video and section flow;
4. the exact Founder Alignment Storyline at the current progressive depth;
5. a compact member face index for navigation only;
6. the current mode, decision epoch, Creative Development Director's curated
   Decision Set, and source-to-screen deltas when the mode calls for them;
7. for each Front Planning section: the Overall-promoted Major Scene, required
   current-title member visual, exact surviving evidence, Creative Development
   QA result, and Overall promotion state;
8. its coverage-complete Key Screen Anchors as supporting detail inside the
   one consolidated FP review, not a separate decision lane;
9. exception cards only for new/restyled member visuals or unresolved material
   conflicts;
10. proposed core-event cards in timeline order; and
11. imported 01 Official Cores and ordered 02A Branch Cuts when they exist.

The member face index never precedes target/message and whole-video context and
never becomes its own decision lane. It exists to keep member identity visible
while the Founder reads the actual MV/teaser flow.

## Front Planning Content

At planning stage, the board should show:

- selected-song and audience context;
- target and audience promise;
- message and emotional result;
- the current-title member visual and evidence attached to each applicable
  Major;
- member allocation by section;
- background/space direction as Major/Key Screen text and exact scene refs;
- whole-video message/content-role progression and explicit selected form;
- Creative Development Director-proposed Major Scenes, Major visual inputs,
  Creative Development QA, and Overall Director promotion state;
- the coverage-led set of Creative-Development-authored, quality-checked, and
  Overall-promoted Key Screen Anchors, without a separate Founder decision per
  anchor or set;
- each Key Screen's visible action/hold, full still-camera geometry,
  composition, light/material, current-title visual inputs, portrait-bias
  guard, and the decision-ready named reference-function bindings required by
  its screen job, without a fixed binding count;
- selected main/support reference relationships;
- exact sequence sheets and selected cells/crops;
- for every active visual section, one applied/combined exact-five contiguous
  Sequence Unit displayed in chronological `01 / 05` through `05 / 05` order
  at exact one-second cadence, with contextual flow reading, exact timestamps,
  frame paths/hashes, and Major eligibility; linked units show no duplicate
  boundary frame, while legacy three-second units are visibly read-only;
- section reference comparison units, purpose analyses, selected components,
  transfer boundaries, and dispositions;
- the curated Decision Set's actual media and Anti-Flat evidence: source-to-
  screen delta, current-song/member dependencies, observable
  before/trigger/after state, distinct contribution, whole-video consequence,
  execution strength, and concrete risk;
- explicit proof that Key Screens combine named components rather than
  recreating or vaguely reinterpreting a whole reference.

## Pre-Generation Scene Build Read

Before the first real image is generated, the active cycle must read as the
work we intend to make in time order, not as a reference gallery:

- an MV follows selected-audio section/time order and names the sound cue,
  message/content job, and visible screen reward for each beat;
- a short-form derivative follows its opening, retention turn, payoff, and loop
  order while preserving the approved master-video world and continuity.

For every planned beat, Major/Key Screen, official Core Cut, or Branch Cut that
appears on the board, show these items in this reading order:

1. the time/section and the beat's message, story, performance, or retention
   job;
2. the scene that will be made: visible subject, action or intentional hold,
   space/object response, camera geometry, composition, and continuity out;
3. one governing main reference when present, followed by supporting
   references whose individual gap-filling functions are named;
4. the exact selected cells, frames, crops, or bounded sequence derivatives in
   their intended consumption order; and
5. **Key image must carry**: identity when applicable, subject state, action or
   hold, camera height/distance/direction/angle/depth, placement, crop/negative
   space, environment response, light/color/material, and the transition fact
   the later motion or edit depends on.

The governing reference owns the declared scene or sequence grammar. A support
reference may supply an unfilled function such as body route, space, camera,
material/light, object response, or edit bridge, but it cannot dilute, average,
or reorder the governing reference. One source may appear at whole-video,
section, Major/Key Screen, Core, and Branch views through typed bindings; its
bytes remain with one canonical owner. The board repeats pointers and roles,
not media copies.

Major and Key Screen anchors are the pre-01 planning view. Official IDOL Core
ids and primary images appear in 01; 02A exact-imports them and adds only Branch
ids. A presentation anchor must never be mistaken for an approved generation
packet.

## Direction Alignment, FP Close, And 02A Final Planning

Internal Discovery prepares the recommendation. Direction Alignment is the
first ordinary Founder intervention: it decides the large message, Major,
video-form, section, and reference direction. Its selection establishes the
baseline for autonomous development but is not FP PASS, 02A PASS, paid
generation approval, or public-release approval.

After alignment, the same Overall/CDD epoch closes FP internally and preserves
the complete storyline artifact. It then advances through role-routed 01 and
02A without ordinary Founder refills. The stable board may expose the FP Close
snapshot read-only, but it must not add another decision lane.

02A Final Planning is the next ordinary Founder decision. In one ordered view,
show:

1. the exact hash-bound FP Close whole-video/section/planned-cut storyline;
2. the corresponding 01 action, staging, still-camera, reference application,
   and continuity expansion; and
3. the corresponding imported 01 Official Cores, ordered 02A Branch Cuts, input bindings, and
   coverage rationale.

PASS/HOLD/REJECT applies only to the named affected scope. PASS authorizes that
scope's 02B route and does not authorize paid/external generation. A held or
rejected dependency cannot appear as approved 02A truth.

## Review Asset Continuity

The manifest owns one pointer-only `review_assets` register. It does not own or
copy media. Every asset record binds a stable asset id, canonical path and
SHA-256, media kind, original owner, first-seen revision, most recent binding
revision, and zero or more typed bindings. Each binding names its `source_stage`
(`front_planning`, `01`, `02A`, `02B`, `generated_branch_review`, `03V`, or
`04`), exact target, lane role, declared order, lane-owned state, supersession,
and the receipt or canonical owner that explains that state.

The board displays `NEW SINCE LAST REVIEW` until the owning lane assigns both a
target and explicit state. Reusing one path/hash adds a typed binding to the
existing asset; it never creates another media owner or duplicate visible asset
record. Held, rejected, and superseded items remain in collapsed count-bearing
groups. The active cycle, new inbox, and last passed rollback stay available at
normal detail; older cycles load media only when opened.

The stable Workbench window and generated HTML consume the same manifest
revision, active cycle, asset register, role/state groups, and declared order.
A path/hash match without the same board revision is insufficient. Any manifest
hash, projection revision, or active-cycle mismatch produces a visible stale
review HOLD and requires reload before a decision can be accepted.

Post-02A IDOL material is read-only here. 01, 02A, 02B, generated review, 03V,
and 04 owners project status and canonical pointers; the Workbench never writes
their creative truth. A generated Branch result appears under its owning
section by canonical pointer and SHA-256, without a second original copy.

## Current-Title Member Visuals

Show title-local member assets from:

```text
assets/members/album-assets/ALBUM_KEY/TITLE_KEY/**
```

Every album/title creates or binds each required member visual before it becomes
the default Key Screen and downstream appearance input. A passed current-title
visual binds without repeat review; a new or fully restyled candidate requires
the smallest Founder exception selection. A previous album visual cannot
substitute for it.
No separate background/world asset card or package is created. INK is not a
reusable content package. A member visual card explains appearance continuity; it must
not imply that every section requires a visible, frontal, or readable face.

When the member visual is newly built or fully restyled, use
`idol-member-visual-identity`: one approved face controls only facial identity;
selected styling references replace hair, makeup, accessories, complete
wardrobe, materials, footwear, and styled silhouette; and the candidate is one
clean 16:9 card with face, upper-body, and uncropped full-body panels in that
order. Display the approved face, styling sources, and clean candidate as
separate indexed media in this workbench. Do not bake source strips or labels
into the clean card. Only a Founder-passed clean card is promoted; all other
media remain build provenance and selection evidence.

## Reference Media

Each reference image/video:

- binds actual path and SHA-256;
- names the selected cell/frame/segment;
- records disposition and directness;
- explains the section function;
- names the analyzed purpose, exact selected component, transfer boundary, and
  do-not-transfer facts;
- states what action, staging, still-camera geometry, placement, crop, and
  sequence grammar are preserved;
- separates reference pixels from generated tests.

A whole sequence sheet is navigation evidence and counts as zero. For every
active visual section, display all five frames of its selected contiguous
bundle as one chronological, non-wrapping reference row; do not replace that
row with the broad sheet or only the first/last frame. Show bundle id, same-
event range, exact one-second cadence, contextual flow, frame timestamps and
paths/hashes, section binding, and whether each Major binds the whole ordered
grammar or only proposes a later subset. The five frames count as one reference
unit and never imply five required downstream cuts. Display any further
selected derivatives individually when they govern a scene.

Every World/Still comparison involving two or more candidates must also display
the exact hash-bound uncropped contact sheet used for that judgment. This is
mandatory in bounded-worker shortlist pruning, CDD Pass A/Pass B comparison, and the
Founder `reference_discovery` projection. The sheet names and covers every
compared candidate, points to its mechanical receipt, and counts as zero
reference units. Preserve each candidate as a separately indexed media item
with its own actual path and SHA-256; the sheet never replaces those individual
bindings or becomes a generation attachment.

Pinterest comparison is a separate dedicated-worker return, not a World/Still
or per-section quota. Open it only for a named Major/Core styling gap or the
identity-specific missing/new/full-restyle member-card comparison. Show every
returned pin as a readable individual public-pin row with URL/id, local
path/hash, proposed function, and authored Major/Core role; a complete contact
sheet remains navigation only and counts as zero evidence units. The number of
workers, pins, sources, and accepted pins adapts to the named gap and never
becomes a general section discovery quota.

For every material Core Slot, display its two to four actual visual candidates
as separate component rows and show the selected exact reference binding or the
Founder-authored exact-Core waiver. Every active visual section also exposes at
least one applied/combined exact-five one-second Sequence Unit and its
contextual flow reading. The board must make it possible to verify which
action/pose, background/space, feeling/major grammar, camera/composition,
light/material, object response, or edit function came from which exact unit.

## Card Hierarchy

Each card declares one `review_target`:

- `discovery`;
- `direction`;
- `whole_film`;
- `section`;
- `core_cut`;
- `branch_cut`.

Discovery and direction cards use `revision_scope: none`; canonical cards match
their whole-video/section/core/branch level. Every card also declares the
applicable affected section ids, canonical field pointers, and media needed for
judgment. Internal Discovery may be sectionless; Direction Alignment requires
the active visual section map and names every affected section.

Before 01 creates official Core-Cut ids, a `CCxx_slug` used by a hand-authored
Front Planning board is only a stable presentation anchor. It must not be
written into canonical `core_cut_id` or `branch_cut_id` fields. When 01 arrives,
bind the official Core id and passed primary image beneath that same visible
parent. When 02A arrives, exact-carry the Core and append only its ordered Branch
children without renaming the existing deep link.

## Decisions

Every Front Planning cycle declares one legacy-compatible `review_mode`:

- `discovery` shows the smallest decision-ready curated source-backed set for
  unresolved named axes, with no fixed ingredient quota. Founder actions are
  `attracted`, `reject`, `hold`,
  `connect_these`, `keep_only`, `search_further`, or `direct_comment`.
- `direction_synthesis` renders as **Direction Alignment** and shows exactly
  three complete viable routes `FP-A`, `FP-B`, and `FP-C`, with one
  ranked recommendation. Each candidate carries its reference combination, whole-video
  and section/Major/Key Screen route, video form, any applicable named-gap
  Pinterest comparison or explicit not-applicable reason, viewer
  reward, gain, sacrifice, material risk, and any target/message/audio reading
  with supporting evidence, counterreading, and screen consequence. Founder
  actions are `select_candidate`, `lock_fields`, `hold`, `reject`, or
  `direct_comment`; only exact Founder-named fields become locks.
- `fp_final` renders as **02A Final Planning** for the pre-generation cycle and
  shows the exact FP Close storyline, 01 expansion, and 02A plan. Its decision
  values are `PASS`, `HOLD`, and `REJECT`. Internal FP Close itself is a
  read-only snapshot, not another `fp_final` decision.

The active v12 board keeps these compatibility modes and adds `cycle_kind` to name
the review stage:

| cycle kind | mode | Founder conversation/review stage | promotion/final acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `reference_discovery` | `discovery` | Creative Development Director (CDD) | Overall Director |
| `direction_synthesis` (Direction Alignment) | `direction_synthesis` | CDD | Overall Director |
| `member_visual_identity` | `fp_final` | CDD | Overall Director |
| `fp_final` (02A Final Planning) | `fp_final` | CDD | Overall Director |
| `generated_branch_cut` | `fp_final` | Overall Director | Overall Director |
| `03v_playback` | `fp_final` | Overall Director | Overall Director |
| `edit_shard` | `fp_final` | Edit Director | Overall Director |
| `edit_final` | `fp_final` | Edit Director | Overall Director |

Every decision-bearing cycle and card binds `decision_owner`,
`decision_question`, exact `legal_actions`, and an `affected_scope` with the
exact stage, section, chunk, and target ids. It also binds
`blocking_effect`, `decision_epoch`, `dispatch_id`, a returned output
path/SHA-256, a director-review path/SHA-256 whose row identifies the reviewed
attempt, and `worker_evidence`: `applicability`, lineage id, attempt id,
session id (nullable only for explicit `mechanical_no_worker` cards), plus an
exact Director Dispatch Index path/SHA-256/row/pointer binding. Finally it
binds Founder receipt/export/intake lineage. CDD and Edit Director are review
surfaces only; they cannot promote canonical truth. Overall owns promotion and
final acceptance. Optional `fragment_ownership` and `board_merge` rows are
accepted only when their paths and hashes resolve and the merge names one
single `board_coordinator` writer.

Internal Discovery has no Founder gate. Direction Alignment does
not create a stage PASS, canonical promotion, or paid/external approval.
`select_candidate` may name exactly one base card for Overall promotion and
only `lock_fields` binds exact Founder-named fields, never adjacent or inferred
fields. If another card supplies requested fields, CDD authors and rechecks a
new coherent candidate; scripts never merge cards. Target/message/audio tension
remains a comparison axis and cannot automatically reject a candidate. The
Creative Development Director curates the next Decision Set; the Overall
Director decides whether other feedback returns to a named gap or enters the
one recommendation.

Across modes, `hold`, `reject`, `search_further`, and `direct_comment` require a
concrete note. `connect_these` names at least two related cards, `keep_only`
names at least one, and `lock_fields` names the exact canonical field pointers.
At most one active Direction card may use `select_candidate`.
Decision intake routes the change to the smallest owner; it does not rewrite
creative truth or trigger an all-video rebuild. Later generated/playback/edit
cycles continue to use their own registered stage decision semantics on this
same board lineage.

For ordinary pre-generation planning, PASS/HOLD/REJECT appear at 02A Final
Planning on the legacy-compatible `fp_final` mode. Direction Alignment uses its
own selection/field-lock actions and Internal FP Close is read-only. An earlier
PASS/HOLD/REJECT appears only on a named new/restyled member-visual or material/
identity/safety/source/feasibility exception card. Major and Key Screen cards
remain inspectable inside the final package without separate decisions.

## Layout

The active renderer and the managed shell folder own the common layout. Do not
fork a Front Planning-specific page language. Each card:

- places the primary decision image or playback first, with the neighbor still
  and short event/axis/distance/crop difference beside it when judging a cut;
- places input references in declared order;
- shows direction and observable screen proof separately;
- keeps source/hash details available but visually secondary;
- keeps generator chat and skill full text off the Founder screen;
- supports local navigation by section and branch cut;
- remains usable on desktop, mobile, and print.

Direction Alignment renders its exactly three cards as DIRECTION A/B/C in one
cycle. The shared target/message/whole-video spine appears once in the header;
each card shows only its coherent route and tradeoff. Wide screens compare the
cards in columns, while narrow screens preserve the same order in a stack.

`verify_board_structure.py` auto-detects the managed hand-authored shell or an
active `idol_html_review_board.v12` projection. Shell-only anchors are not
required on generated v12 HTML; the v12 profile instead checks its renderer meta,
card/carousel/decision runtime markers, no-crop rule, and intact newline-list
regular expression.

### Fixed Core-Cut card anatomy

The visual baseline is the current SU `core-cut-visual-ref-board-v1/board.html`
shape. Preserve this order and geometry:

1. Core/event header, section/order, source status, and review scope;
2. first-read/source-observation band when references are active;
3. a desktop two-column visual row with an adaptive member block around a
   `280px` practical minimum and a bounded proportional width on the left, plus
   a fluid reference grid on the right;
4. primary face and primary title-local style side by side when space permits,
   with additional styling in one compact horizontal strip/carousel;
5. character, scene, selected-cell, and sequence references in declared order
   on the right; and
6. official 02A Branch-Cut children or later generated/playback material below
   the parent Core-Cut visual row.

Adding Branch Cuts, generated images, or later review cycles may extend the card
vertically. Additional member styling stays in its compact horizontal strip;
references beyond the visible right-side slot count extend that same reference
row horizontally through the managed carousel. Neither route may add wrapped
reference rows, mix a member face into the reference pool, or replace the right
side with another board.

The visible face tile is review-only identity evidence. It is not a separate
02B input lane, never forces a readable/frontal face, and is absent as an input
when the member is not meaningfully visible. Object/world Core-Cut cards keep
the left rail as an explicit no-member state rather than shifting the
reference grid into the identity position.

### Readable media gallery (mandatory)

`scripts/idol_html_review_board.rb` and **any hand-authored Founder visual /
styling / character-reference board** under a run must keep media readable as
count grows:

- every media group receives deterministic visible indexing in declared order
  as `01 / N`, `02 / N`, and so on; the index is presentation metadata derived
  by the renderer and never a new creative id;
- the right reference row shows up to four equal desktop slots. When the
  declared media count exceeds the visible slots, it remains one row and
  exposes left/right buttons, touch/trackpad horizontal scrolling, scroll-snap,
  and a visible range status such as `01–04 / 07`;
- at mobile width the same row uses one visible slot at a time. It does not
  shrink the member rail or silently crop a second reference into the slot;
- each reference image/video frame has a **minimum visible height** of about
  `220–240px` with
  **`object-fit: contain`** so the **full frame** stays visible;
- **`object-fit: cover` is forbidden** for visual-reference, styling-reference,
  character-reference, sequence-sheet, and outfit panels. Cover crops limbs,
  shoes, hems, hands, and board edges and makes Founder locks unstable;
- face-only identity tiles also prefer **contain** (do not top-crop hair/chin
  away) unless the asset is already a tight face plate and the card label says
  face-only;
- additional reference media never wrap to a second reference row; they stay
  reachable in original order through the horizontal carousel and never hide
  pixels behind path/SHA chrome;
- sequence sheets / storyboard boards may span full width;
- FILE/SHA binding stays under the image, secondary to the pixels;
- click-to-enlarge is mandatory. Clicking a thumbnail opens its full image and
  caption. Clicking that enlarged image again, the overlay background, or the
  close control closes it; `Escape` also closes it.

Prefer large current-title member assets and exact scene-reference images for
decision cards. Tiny crops may appear only when they are the **actual governing
selected cell/crop surface** already registered as a derivative; otherwise keep
them in text/facts rather than as unreadable gallery tiles. Never invent a
display crop that is not the bound derivative.

### Typography and responsive behavior

The shell owns two font tokens and run boards may not replace them ad hoc:

```css
--font-sans: Pretendard, Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", "Apple SD Gothic Neo", "Noto Sans KR", sans-serif;
--font-mono: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace;
```

Local review must not fetch remote fonts. If a font file is later vendored, its
repository-relative path and SHA-256 join the shell registry while these
fallback tokens remain stable. At `900px` and below, the complete member block
stacks at full width above the reference grid. Hash navigation must reserve the
complete sticky-nav height so a Core/Branch title is never covered.

## Boundaries

The board:

- does not auto-pass;
- does not replace Front Planning, 02A, review, 03V, or 04 artifacts;
- does not promote a generated test into a key visual;
- does not authorize generation or external action;
- does not change a reference interpretation;
- does not create a second visual asset package;
- does not allow local edits to rewrite unaffected upstream stages.
