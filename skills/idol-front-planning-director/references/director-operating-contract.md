## Studio Roles And Evidence Lifecycle

Exactly one Overall Director owns canonical promotion, Founder-facing
recommendation, and final creative acceptance. Until a verified Graph v2
switch, the legacy-pinned route keeps exactly one durable Founder-visible
compatibility CDD through 03V, with exactly one active physical session.
After that verified switch, Planning CDD Cell owners and later Section-Cluster
lanes follow `AGENTS.md` and `skills/idol-planning-cdd/SKILL.md`; do not
duplicate that topology table here, and never co-run the legacy compatibility
CDD. The active CDD directs bounded logical `worker_high` departments through
the pinned run-resolved provider, inspects every returned candidate, exposes
the single Founder workbench cycle, develops the recommendation, and reports
upward. CDD does not become a competing authority. There is no standing Final
Director, and Terra is not an ordinary creative-production route.

Before a song and section map exist, open only the bounded department searches
needed to close named, nonoverlapping evidence gaps. Worker and source counts
adapt to decision readiness and never become discovery quotas. When song work
is in scope, one
separate physical Founder-visible Song Room task opens under CDD for lyric,
arrangement, candidate, selected-audio, audio-motion, and 00A work. It resumes
in the same task after HOLD or a later 00A correction and returns only to CDD;
Overall does not duplicate the Song Room.

After the song and map exist, open the execution-contract post-song reference
fetch floor of six disjoint workers: the four generalist role checkpoints, one
physical World/Still room, and one physical Pinterest room. Skip a floor row
only with a closed `not_applicable` reason. Extra assignments remain named-gap
only. Each generalist may combine motion, ordered
sequence, styling, current-title member evidence, and selected stills around
one disjoint territory. Source counts adapt to decision readiness
and provider capacity; they are never discovery or quality quotas. CDD
dispatch and review inside the active CDD room uses Grok
`spawn_subagent` at the execution-contract floor of 4.
World/Still owns broad standalone-image discovery for space/set, world/system,
object/environment response, material/light, and still camera/composition. It
must not open, download, sample, extract, or analyze video. Every Pass-A and
Pass-B comparison from that department must include all compared candidates in
a hash-bound uncropped contact sheet; individual image `source_id`, path, and
locator remain the actual evidence units, scripts stamp SHA, and the sheet
counts as zero. Pinterest owns
only readable public-pin still cohorts for active visual sections and creates
no stage, identity lane, or promotion authority. After CDD reviews current
returns, any next assignment must own one named disjoint gap, remain under the
provider live cap, return only to CDD, and stop when that gap closes or holds.

A passed current-title member visual binds as appearance truth. If a required
card is missing or a full restyle is authorized, open the conditional physical
**Member Visual Identity** task under CDD; it works members sequentially, never
creates a separate Styling Room, and returns only to CDD. Member Visual Identity
controls appearance continuity, never pose, camera, crop, or a readable face; a
world/still source never overrides member identity.

Each group performs Pass A and returns a curated source-backed shortlist, not a
raw pool. CDD inspects every medium, chooses provisional governing/supporting
candidates, records named gaps, and sends Pass B to the same owning session
only for the smallest unresolved additions. Decision readiness for every
material Major/Core job—not a fixed per-section count—is the comparison floor.

Internal Discovery carries exactly three complete viable routes `FP-A`, `FP-B`,
and `FP-C`, with one ranked recommendation. Every candidate states its
reference combination, whole-video/section/Major/Key Screen route, viewer
reward, gain, sacrifice, material risk, and any target/message/audio reading
with supporting evidence, counterreading, and screen consequence. Such tension
is never an automatic filter. CDD recommends one coherent route; cross-candidate
field requests require a newly authored coherent candidate, never a scripted
merge.

Before any compact whole-video readout, display and inspect the complete
PRE-00-rooted full-storyline lineage from the selected v4 handoff. The immutable
`pre00_storyline_original` remains visible and byte-preserved forever. When
song, target, Founder direction, or a selected reference materially changes the
flow, Front Planning appends one new complete full-text version with its parent
id/hash, changed passages, preserved invariants, and exact reference-function
receipts; it never edits the original in place. The active full text is the
creative root. `whole_film_spine`, section maps, Founder Alignment Storyline,
and boards are navigational or execution expansions, never summary substitutes.

Author the Founder Alignment Storyline while the exact reference pixels and
their intended placement are open. Internal Discovery states the concrete
whole-video read and explicitly names whether the form is narrative,
performance-led, associative/poetic, world/system-led, object-led,
retention-led teaser, or hybrid. Founder Direction Alignment adds every
section's visible action, camera, light, reference contribution, continuity,
and section-specific Pinterest comparison. Internal FP Close adds every planned
cut/Key Screen with enough body/object/environment and still-camera truth to
expand directly through 01 and 02A. Bind every level to its exact source pointer
(`source_id`, path, locator); scripts stamp SHA. Later surfaces exact-project it
and may add only their named execution delta. The legacy `whole_film` transport token means this
complete MV, teaser, or selected-song video and never requires feature-film
plot, dialogue, realism, or three-act structure.

If the Founder reads a bound image differently, do not debate from the saved
summary. Reopen that exact image, selected cell, crop, or frame; compare it with
the displayed storyline in the active CDD/Director conversation; record the
reconciled smallest-owner change; and invalidate only its affected descendants.
An unchanged media hash and a complete prior read avoid routine rereads, but a
declared Founder mismatch explicitly requires this targeted pixel reopen.

Maintain one CDD-owned, pointer-only Reference Working Index:

```text
Discovery Reference Pool candidate
-> selected Evidence Card with reusable media-shaped read
-> exact section binding and decision epoch
```

Workers submit deltas; they never concurrently rewrite the index. Every Decision
Set survivor carries actual media, the source-to-screen delta, current-song and
member dependencies, observable before/trigger/after state, distinct screen
contribution, whole-video consequence, strongest feasible execution, and a
concrete risk. Missing evidence is an Anti-Flat hold. The Director Dispatch
Index and Reference Working Index expose only pointers, state, paths, hashes,
decision epochs, review disposition, and next actions; they never embed media,
raw pools, transcripts, or creative truth. The Overall Director sees the
survivors and their deltas, not raw or rejected pools.

## Department Dispatch v8 And Return Lifecycle

Every Song, integrated-reference, conditional Member Visual Identity, and
pre-02A development worker launches from one `idol-department-dispatch.v8`
Department Dispatch Contract. It names the exact task role and scope, Founder
epoch and locks, source paths/hashes, external-permission mode, required skills
and linked modules to read to EOF and apply, bounded output paths, Anti-Flat
evidence, write authority, stop conditions, logical dispatcher, and return
recipient. The worker's first runtime action opens the exact hash-bound Goal
before any required read or work. Its return includes model-authored skill-read
timestamps, the mechanical-compliance receipt, and one exact-cover fulfillment
claim for each required Founder/skill/policy/contract/schema/output/quality/
hold/Goal manifest row. The Workbench verifies those bytes and queues the
immutable return; it does not invent claims or perform Director review. A
separate historical skill-application receipt is not an active gate.

Stable `lineage_id` and physical `session_id` remain separate from each
immutable `attempt_id`/`attempt_number`. A correction or retry creates a new
attempt under the same physical child room and lineage/session, records the
correction evidence (problem, screen consequence, action, remaining risk,
affected owner, complete problem set, acceptance criteria, invalidated outputs,
and retry route), and retains the prior attempt. The initial return plus at most
two targeted corrections form one review cycle. Each correction opens a fresh
Goal for only the invalidated paths and hash-freezes unaffected outputs; a
third submission may pass but cannot spawn a fourth child submission. Never
overwrite a prior return or silently reset the counter through a new session,
provider, model, Founder epoch, or parallel pre-02A room. A multi-file return
exposes one aggregate manifest with exact child paths
and SHA-256 values; pointer-only indexes point to that manifest. CDD reviews
every return, carries the reviewed Founder-board cycle, and sends the milestone
upward. Only the Overall Director promotes canonical truth.

Every Song, reference, Member Visual Identity, and development worker returns
only to CDD. CDD opens the exact returned bytes and governing requirements,
records its own semantic judgment separately from mechanical validation and
worker claims, and closes one queued return fully before reviewing another
return addressed to that same Director. Only the Overall Director holds the
global canonical-promotion lease and promotes one reviewed result at a time.

Each CDD or worker launch consumes a Compiled Launch Prompt derived from the
hash-bound Studio Kernel, the exact selected profile/checkpoint in
`automation/harness-skill-load-profiles.yaml`, and current-run Creative Delta.
The fixed block order is dispatch identity; role/authority; current creative
job; exact scope/non-scope; input pointers; skill route; required work; output
contract; quality proof; return-ready conditions; HOLD/stop/split; and
return/wake. The current creative job binds Founder verbatim/locks, target
reward, song/audio cue, whole-video and assigned section/content role, and the
current screen/reference gap. A script may label and exact-copy only. It
must not author, infer, summarize, or repair current creative values.

After launch or return, wait without polling. Wake only on `final`, `hold`,
`authority_request`, or a declared `milestone`, carrying compact hash-bound
output/manifest, hold/rejection, and unresolved-decision pointers without raw
commentary. A complete return still requires CDD review and Overall promotion.
CDD successor rollover uses only the registered
`idol-director-boot-pack.v3` plus provider-neutral session-router route. It
requires first Founder confirmation, an immutable hash-equal checkpoint with no
active gate/review/writer/conflict, one fresh zero-turn read-only successor, one
exact seven-answer hash-bound final preflight envelope, provider-native Goal
admission, and a second Founder confirmation. The old CDD retains authority
until that pass. Embedded Workbench may use its native bridge; direct Codex-app
or Grok-CLI operation must compare-and-swap the exact router state SHA and
generation, deny A and authorize only B, then reverse those permissions on
rollback. Grok `complete/goal_completed` at its `/goal` terminal closes only
admission, not the CDD assignment. This transition never rewrites HTML,
review-board, canvas, storyline, Section, or canonical creative bytes. A route
without exact admission, access-denial, CAS, and rollback proof remains `HOLD`.

## Director Readout

Return the smallest useful version of these fields:

```text
Director read
- Disposition: accept | combine | challenge | hold
- Music truth: hook / section pressure / lyric guard / release
- MV truth: viewer receive / world or performance carrier / visible change
- Section route: audio cue -> screen event or hold -> camera/space -> handoff
- Reference functions: source -> followed surface -> target section
- Visual test: candidate purpose, not-yet-locked prompt direction, review test
- Founder choice: exact decision or open question
```

Do not present a long option list when one route is clearly stronger. Present a
primary recommendation, one meaningful alternative only when it changes the
decision, and the consequence of choosing each.

Review every recommendation in this order: causal plausibility; distinct
observable screen contribution; exact source and current-work evidence;
non-redundancy across the complete video; and feasibility that preserves the strongest
idea. Record the result in the existing Director readout, Decision Set, Front
Planning fields, and Founder workbench. Do not create a creative Judgment Card,
total score, or machine creative verdict.

## Music–MV Crosscheck

Check both sides before calling a route coherent.

| Music question | Required screen consequence |
| --- | --- |
| What sonic identity and setup develop across the opening, and where does the actual intro close? | The opening establishes one visual identity, completes a viewer promise or question across the intro boundary, ordinarily around 8-12 seconds when the song supports it, and places a visible hook seed or intentional hold. |
| What recognizable carrier links the first full hook to its middle return? | The music-and-screen carrier remains legible while meaning, relation, scale, world/object state, member ownership, or consequence develops. |
| What changes at the hook, drop, break, or held vocal? | A visible event, camera behavior, or intentional hold changes with it. |
| How do percussion, bass, texture, and vocal emphasis differ by section? | Screen density, body/object timing, transition pressure, or framing changes for a reason. |
| What does the listener receive as the song progresses? | The viewer's relation to the member, world, object, or system progresses rather than repeats. |
| Is a lyric the audible turn or only a meaning guard? | Use lyric text only when it causes the event; otherwise direct from sound and use it to prevent a wrong read. |
| Does the candidate song remain provisional? | Keep the plan as an audition hypothesis; do not claim a selected audio lock. |

## Challenge Protocol

Challenge a Founder proposal only when there is a concrete consequence such as:

- a music/video mismatch: the visual reaches release before the music does, or
  ignores a major hook/hold;
- whole-video flattening: the same pose, world state, color, camera, or thesis
  repeats without a changed job;
- target-empathy loss: a beautiful image does not let the intended viewer enter
  the song's feeling;
- reference replay: missing current-song or screen purpose, or unauthorized
  source-identity import; faithful preservation of a selected reference's
  action or emotion is not by itself a challenge;
- identity/feasibility risk: the route weakens member continuity, camera
  readability, generation reliability, or editability.

State the challenge in this order: **consequence → stronger alternative or
combination → Founder decision needed**. Do not say only that something “does
not fit,” and do not overrule an explicit lock silently.

## Visual Flow Rules

- Give each section one dominant screen job: seed, embody, complicate,
  contradict, release, resolve, echo, performance proof, world process,
  transition, negative space, or afterimage.
- Name observable truth: who/what, where, action or hold, audio cue, gaze when
  visible, composition, camera height/distance/direction/depth, and the next
  section relation.
- Use the canvas for spatial comparison and grouping. Use Sequence Rail for
  chronological flow. Do not infer order from layout.
- Treat reference cards and provider-native roughs as candidate evidence until
  the Creative Development Director curates them and the Overall Director
  promotes their function and placement. A strong-looking rough never outranks
  song function or source lineage.
- Use `All` view before a major checkpoint to look for visual sameness,
  premature peak energy, missing quiet, or a finale without payoff.
  Consecutive bound Sequence Units that keep the same observable scene flow
  are a hold unless the storyline job is echo or recurrence. Do not
  rank-recommend Direction Alignment `select_candidate` while that hold is
  open. Do not use a lexical sameness detector.
- For Founder visual review, use the stable HTML workbench defined in
  `founder-visual-review-board-contract.md` (global song/ref spine → member map
  → section member grids + generated cuts). Do not rely on chat-only image
  lists when packages exist. Internal Discovery is Director/CDD work. Founder
  Direction Alignment reviews the message, Major route, video form, every
  section, and section-specific Pinterest comparison. Then the same Director/
  CDD epoch closes FP, expands role-routed 01, and allocates 02A without Founder
  refill. Founder 02A Final Planning offers PASS/HOLD/REJECT on the exact FP
  Close storyline plus 01 and 02A; PASS unlocks affected 02B only.
- Pair every board image with the exact source-hashed Founder Alignment
  Storyline at the resolution required by the current checkpoint: whole video
  in internal Discovery; whole video plus sections and Pinterest cohorts at
  Direction Alignment; whole video plus sections plus planned cuts at internal
  FP Close; and that exact closed storyline plus 01/02A at 02A Final Planning.
- Board GENERATED key-cut tests, regardless of execution provider, are
  package-fit comparison only. **Do not use them as key visual.**

## Reference Interpretation Checkpoint

Governing, supporting, and section-bound evidence defaults to
official/original professional primary sources: official MVs, visual/concept
films, official teasers/trailers/highlight films, and—when functionally
necessary—official performance, campaign/editorial, director or
production-company sources, official film/series/anime/game sources, or
official/verified creator stills. A public readable Pinterest pin URL or pin
id plus `source_id`, path, and locator is eligible still identity; do not
return `unresolved_origin` HOLD solely for a missing non-Pinterest origin.
Other reposts and aggregators remain navigation only until origin verification
as that source class requires. Restricted edits remain permission gated. If a
Founder main reference exists, follow `main -> origin verification ->
genealogy discovery + functional discovery -> placement`, treating a public
Pinterest pin main as already origin-resolved at the pin. Without one, route
`need/function -> discovery (public Pinterest first for visual identity) ->
origin verification where the source class still requires it -> comparison ->
placement`. The no-main route still compares connections and closes exact
placement before a survivor can bind. This does not expand network permission.

AMV, MAD, and fan edits require exact current-task Founder approval before any
query, search, opening, download, capture, playback, pixel inspection,
analysis, registration, citation, response-pattern use, nomination, binding,
or direct use. A supplied link/file is not approval. Permission must name the
exact identity/URL, purpose, and allowed action; edit-rhythm permission does
not permit scene binding. Incidental results remain unopened. If indispensable,
return `founder_permission_missing`; if origin cannot be verified, return
`unresolved_origin`. Historical rows remain evidence-only and never authorize
active reuse.

Every selected unit closes one causal Reference Connection: section need ->
observed source evidence -> bounded transfer -> observable screen consequence
-> exact section placement. Project this through the existing Front Planning
`reference_connection_research.reference_units` and `section_plan` ids, then
01 `reference_applications` and 02A bindings. Do not create a parallel schema or
judgment surface.

The source hub records the reusable Layer A/B first semantic read on the
source-analysis row. The current run then records `interpretation_receipt` on
its reference row to bind that analysis revision to the selected locator,
disposition, transfer, and section/job use. It binds `source_id`, canonical source path, locator, and the exact owner-row
JSON pointer; scripts stamp SHA and the value digest. It also binds the
selected cell/crop when visual, and receipt revision. Front Planning exposes
those run receipts through
`interpretation_contract.reference_interpretation_index` and its no-reread
`reference_interpretation_policy`; it does not create a parallel semantic-cache
document.

An unchanged receipt is used through `consume_bound_interpretation`. Reopen only
when source bytes or the selected cell changed, the lock is incomplete,
selected-audio meaning changed, identity/safety/feasibility conflicts, or the
Founder asks for re-review. Reopening creates a new revision and invalidates
affected downstream projections. A model/session change is not a reason. For a
contact sheet, hand off the selected cell/crop only; the original remains the
provenance and separately approved attachment source.

**First-read trees are not in this section.** Jump to
[02B-Shaped Source Observation](#02b-shaped-source-observation-mandatory-first-read)
and complete that mandatory method before purpose-class or disposition
closeout. Conditional good/bad teaching examples live in
[source-observation-teaching.md](examples/source-observation-teaching.md)
and are not a default read.

When Front Planning must expand a partial Founder set, close a concrete 01R
search contract before handoff. Keep video candidates at a known `<=270`
seconds; use the video hub's `<=60` and `>60..270` capture/discovery classes only
to choose a legal source tool and duration path. Those classes do not require a
short-form output, hook quota, or derivative planning field. Each query must
join the selected-song target and message, a Founder source or bound reference
function, and one named missing screen job. Apply raw view count only after
relevance and diversity, with no upload-age normalization. The pool must cover
the minimum sufficient spread of story situation, space/background, subject
relation, body/action, camera route, object/world behavior, and light/material;
repeated same-space, same-people, same-action, same-camera evidence does not
become useful merely because it is popular.

## 02B-Shaped Source Observation (Mandatory First Read)

This section is the **single detailed method owner** for mandatory reference
first-read trees. Conditional good/bad teaching examples live in
[source-observation-teaching.md](examples/source-observation-teaching.md)
and are not a default read. `AGENTS.md`, policies, and other skill bodies keep
only the invariant and stage-specific hold/consumption rules; they must not
restate the full nested trees. Receipt binding and reopen rules stay in
[Reference Interpretation Checkpoint](#reference-interpretation-checkpoint).

Reference media are not all the same shape. Choose the correct first-read lane:

| Media shape | First-read lane | What it owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single still, crop, or one selected cell | **Still observation** only | One nested 02B-shaped tree for that surface |
| Broad contact / sequence / frame sheet | Navigation only until a bounded bundle is selected | Whole ordered grammar may guide selection, but the sheet counts as zero reference units |
| Active contiguous `sequence_direct` bundle | **Layer A flow** + **Layer B stills** | Exactly five consecutive same-event samples at exact one-second cadence; flow governs order/continuity and selected cells govern still binding and 02B |
| Legacy exact-five three-second bundle | **Read-only legacy reader** | Pinned evidence only; never newly authored, silently upgraded, or automatically promoted into an active Sequence Unit |

Still-lane nested trees are source observation, not early final-prompt
authorship and not a second schema. A frame sheet is never “one big still.”

### Lane 1 — Single still / selected cell nested tree

When the unit is one still, crop, or already-selected cell/frame, fill every
leaf from those pixels:

```text
Identity reference rule:
  source_cast_or_subject: [who/what appears in the source pixels]
  current_title_member_visual: not_attached
  identity_guard: source face/body is not Loom / current-title identity

Scene intent:
  who_or_what: [primary visible subject or object system]
  where: [physical place or space type proved by the pixels]
  single_event_or_hold: [the one visible event or intentional hold that makes
    this surface recognizable as a specific photo/scene]

Visible details:
  background_and_space: [room/exterior/set facts, surfaces, scale cues]
  still_camera_and_composition:
    camera_height: [eye / high / low / other proved height]
    shot_scale_distance: [scale + camera-to-subject distance]
    direction_angle: [front / three-quarter / side / rear / overhead + angle]
    depth_design: [foreground / midground / background layering]
    subject_placement: [where the subject sits in the frame]
    crop_negative_space: [what is cut off; open space and pressure]
    lens_feel: [wide / normal / long / compressed perspective character]
  lighting_color_material: [key light direction/quality, color, materials,
    reflections, haze, practicals when visible]
  face_gaze_when_visible: [expression/gaze if readable; else none]

Action cue:
  subject_state_or_pose: [stance, weight, hand/foot/torso state]
  visible_action_or_hold: [what is changing, contacting, or deliberately held]
  object_environment_response: [object/space reaction, or null when none]

Avoid:
  do_not_transfer: [source face as title identity; brand marks; unselected
    sheet cells; discarded source traits; mood-only paraphrase of this surface]
```

### Lane 2 — Frame / sequence sheet dual read (mandatory)

The broad multi-frame sheet owns navigation and cannot bind by itself. Select
one contiguous event and package exactly five ordered consecutive samples at
exact one-second cadence as one active Sequence Unit. Longer events use linked
Sequence Units without duplicated boundary frames. Legacy exact-five
three-second units remain pinned, read-only reader evidence and cannot be newly
authored or silently upgraded. Read the selected unit in two layers. Missing either layer, any frame
path/locator, or a coherent same-event five-sample run is a hold.

**Layer A — Sequence flow observation** (sheet-level; not a generation prompt):

```text
Sequence flow observation:
  sheet_role: navigation_only_bundle_is_the_one_reference_unit
  bundle_id: [stable SEQB id named by selected_locator]
  source_locator: [source id + selected event range]
  sampling_interval_seconds: 1
  cadence_reason: active_exact_one_second_sequence_unit
  manifest: [source_id + path]; scripts stamp SHA
  continuous_event: [one event/system the ordered cells still belong to]
  ordered_phase_route: [exactly five entries]
    - phase: [start | setup | escalation | impact | reaction | aftermath | ...]
      cell_or_frame_locator: [row/col, timestamp, or cell id]
      frame_source: [source_id + path + locator]; scripts stamp SHA
      what_is_happening: [observable change or hold at this phase]
  what_stays_continuous: [body route, object system, space, relation, still-
    camera family, light family that survives across phases]
  what_changes_across_cells: [pose/action, object state, space state, relation,
    still-camera difference, crop difference — named per transition]
  ordered_scene_grammar: [how the sequence should be followed: phase order,
    entry state, exit state, forbidden reorder]
  selected_anchor_cells:
    - cell_locator: [...]
      selection_reason: [why this cell is a still/02B or motion anchor]
  unselected_cells_policy: navigation_only_do_not_bind_or_attach
  downstream_count_rule: five_source_frames_do_not_require_five_cuts
  do_not_transfer_from_sheet: [source faces as title identity; whole-sheet mood
    paraphrase; inventing motion a sampled sheet cannot prove]
```

one sequence unit, which cells are only navigation, and which anchors will be

for the whole sheet.

```text
-> select one five-frame same-event bundle at exact one-second cadence
-> select any smaller anchor subset needed for still/02B use
-> Layer B nested 02B-shaped tree per anchor

```

For `sequence_direct`, Layer A ordered grammar is governing for phase order and

hash-bound subset without turning the evidence floor into five required cuts.
Support stills may fill an unowned leaf but may not erase the ordered grammar.

Temporal camera movement remains 03V truth after generated pixels exist. On a

angle/crop changes proved by successive frames). Do not invent a continuous
