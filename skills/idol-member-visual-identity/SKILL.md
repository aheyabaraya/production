# IDOL Member Visual Identity

Create one current-title Member Visual Identity authority from an approved face
and selected styling references. This is the reusable title-identity build
method; it does not create a separate graph-engineering stage or authorize generation by
itself. It is an exception-only Front Planning path: use it for a member who
has no passed current-title visual or whose current-title appearance is being
fully restyled. Do not reopen a passed current-title card merely because
another Major Scene or Key Screen uses it.

## Department And Member Visual Identity Boundary

The ordinary integrated reference wave may inspect styling and current-title
member evidence, but it does not create a standing styling lane. A missing,
new, or fully restyled appearance activates one conditional physical **Member
Visual Identity** Luna/Max task under the durable Founder-visible CDD. It works
members sequentially, inspects only the styling sources needed to close its
named component gaps, and returns candidate media and clean-card evidence only
to CDD. Source and candidate counts are adaptive to professional decision
readiness, not fixed quotas. A passed current-title card binds without
reopening. The task must
not decide scene pose/action, require a readable face, or dictate camera,
placement, crop, occlusion, or body proportion; world/still references cannot
override current-title appearance.

The CDD inspects the candidate Decision Set and curates it on the single
`RUN/review/founder-workbench.html` Founder-visible board lineage. There is no
separate Styling Room or sibling member board. Overall authorizes the curated
recommendation for Founder presentation; the Founder then records explicit
PASS/HOLD/REJECT on that board. CDD preserves the decision receipt and returns
the milestone, and only Overall may canonically promote a Founder-passed clean
card. Terra is not an ordinary owner and there is no standing Final Director.
Codex and Grok adapters preserve the same inputs, attachment order, review,
promotion, and Founder authority.

## Fixed Identity And Styling Split

Use exactly one approved face-only asset as the identity source. Resolve it
from that member's current `assets/member-registry.yaml` `face_reference`; the
active path must be the validated `member_face_only` local upscale when one is
registered. `assets/members/face-only-upscale-manifest.json`, validated as an
`idol_local_image_upscale_receipt.v1` manifest, must bind the
original generated bytes, the active PNG, a preserved aspect ratio, and no
crop, sharpening, or generative enhancement. Do not silently fall back to an
older raw face-only file when the registered upscale or receipt is missing or
stale. Preserve only the member's face identity: facial structure, eye shape
and spacing, nose, mouth, jaw, face length, stable skin tone, and registered
facial marks.

Replace from the selected styling references:

- hairstyle and hair color;
- makeup and beauty finish;
- accessories;
- every garment and layer;
- fabric, material, hardware, and color;
- footwear; and
- the resulting styled silhouette.

Do not preserve the face source's hair, makeup, clothing, pose, crop,
background, lighting, or body styling. Do not copy the styling source person's
face, body identity, pose, camera, background, text, interface, or logo.

If several styling references are used, assign each one an explicit component
role before generation. One component cannot have two unresolved authorities.
Hold on a hair, makeup, accessory, wardrobe, material, or footwear conflict
instead of averaging the sources silently.

When scene grammar stills are 2D, anime, illustration, or cel-style and the
title production look is photoreal, do not use those frames as wardrobe
material authority. Prefer Founder/CDD-selected photoreal fashion or
attribute-matched idol concept stills under the registered Pinterest worker
method
`skills/idol-pinterest-reference-worker/references/2d-source-to-photoreal-wardrobe-method.md`
(function extract → member attribute match → one garment noun → bandana/color
lock → thin wardrobe deck). Grammar stills may remain place/relation evidence
only and must not sit in face or fabric attachment slots.

## Fixed Three-Panel Card

Produce one clean 16:9 landscape card per member. The canvas contains exactly
three generated panels in this left-to-right order:

1. face identity and makeup proof;
2. upper-body hair, accessory, layering, and garment-construction proof; and
3. uncropped head-to-toe full-body outfit, silhouette, and footwear proof.

All three panels show the same member, face, hairstyle, makeup, accessories,
wardrobe, materials, and color treatment. Use a quiet neutral review setting
and coherent light so styling can be compared without a scene concept taking
over. The clean card contains no source strips, labels, typography, logos,
watermarks, weapons, performance action, or scene storytelling.

The face panel may be frontal or a clear three-quarter identity proof. That
review need applies only to the card and never becomes a downstream frontal-
face, gaze, pose, crop, camera, or proportion lock.

## Work Sequence

1. Verify the current title, member id, approved face asset path/SHA-256, and
   Founder-selected styling reference paths/SHA-256. For this missing/new or
   fully restyled member, the CDD comparison set must contain a professionally
   decision-ready set of readable public Pinterest styling/look candidates for
   the named component gaps. Each candidate carries pin URL/id, local
   path/SHA-256, component function, and take/discard reasoning before CDD
   selects the bounded styling inputs. Candidate count is adaptive and never a
   generation-attachment quota. A previously passed card never re-enters this
   comparison. Verify the active
   `idol-department-dispatch.v8` Goal-first Contract, required skill/module EOF reads and
   application instructions, Founder epoch, write scope, and input digest.
   Record stable `lineage_id`/physical `session_id` separately from the
   immutable `attempt_id`/`attempt_number`; a correction must be a new attempt
   under the same child room and must not overwrite an earlier return.
2. Author a component transfer map: `preserve face identity`; `replace hair`,
   `makeup`, `accessories`, `wardrobe`, `materials`, `footwear`, and
   `silhouette`; `reject style-source identity and scene grammar`.
3. Author or revise the generation prompt with every required category in
   `references/character-card-prompt-field-guide.md`. Fill its appearance and
   correction values only from the current transfer map, inspected inputs, and
   Founder locks; the guide supplies no styling defaults.
4. Work on one member at a time. Keep the attachment order as face identity
   first, then styling references in the declared component order.
5. Generate one clean three-panel card. Do not generate a separate face card,
   upper-body file, full-body file, or baked reference-comparison composite as
   canonical output.
6. Inspect the returned pixels at full size and record exact inputs, prompt,
   output path/SHA-256, dimensions, and findings.
7. Return the candidate to the Creative Development Director with actual media,
   component-by-component source-to-card delta, continuity result, concrete
   risk, and result digest. If multiple files are returned, expose one
   aggregate manifest with exact child paths and SHA-256 values. After CDD
   review, the Overall Director alone authorizes the curated candidate for
   presentation on the single Founder workbench lineage. Show the face and styling sources as
   review evidence beside the clean card; keep them out of the clean card
   pixels and keep raw/rejected pools out of the board.
8. Keep the card as a candidate until explicit Founder PASS. CDD records that
   exact receipt and returns it to Overall. The active Workbench decision
   intake then locally upscales only the exact passed generated clean card,
   validates the source/output receipt, and returns the result to Overall.
   Overall promotes only that receipt-bound upscaled three-panel card to the
   current-title member package; comparison evidence remains provenance. HOLD,
   REJECT, partial, stale, source/reference, and already-promoted assets create
   no upscale and no promotion.

Paid/API generation, external upload, public release, and replacement of an
already passed title asset each require the applicable explicit approval.

## Pixel Quality Gate

HOLD when any of these are true:

- the face is not recognizably the approved member in any panel;
- face geometry or registered facial marks drift between panels;
- hair, makeup, accessories, wardrobe, material, or footwear differs between
  panels or fails to follow its selected source;
- the styling source person's identity leaks into the result;
- the face source's old styling survives without an explicit Founder lock;
- the full-body panel crops the head, hands, hem, or footwear;
- anatomy, garment construction, layering, or accessories break;
- the card contains source strips, labels, text, logos, watermark, interface,
  props, weapons, performance action, or scene composition; or
- the review evidence or output lineage lacks an actual path or current hash.
- a correction is proposed without a new immutable attempt, retained prior
  output, affected-output invalidation, and evidence-bearing problem/action/risk
  record.

Model pixel PASS is not Founder selection. A contact or overview sheet is not
a replacement for individual member review.

## Output Contract And Canonical Promotion

Promote passed cards under:

```text
assets/members/album-assets/ALBUM_KEY/TITLE_KEY/member-key-visuals/
```

The current-title manifest records face provenance, styling-source provenance,
selection evidence, the original selected clean-card path/SHA-256, its local
upscale receipt, the promoted upscaled clean-card path/SHA-256, appearance lock,
and Founder state. The promoted receipt-bound upscaled clean card becomes the
only active appearance attachment for that member.

After promotion:

- face-only and styling sources remain provenance and review evidence only;
- comparison projections never become generation attachments;
- 01/02A/02B receive the passed clean card, not the build inputs;
- the card controls face continuity plus current-title hair, makeup,
  accessories, wardrobe, material, silhouette, and body continuity; and
- the exact Branch Cut still controls pose, action, gaze, camera, placement,
  crop, occlusion, and proportion in the scene.

The launch/return runtime record binds the Department Dispatch v8 Contract,
exact skill revisions, lineage/session/attempt, correction evidence when
needed, input/result digests, owner, and evidence that the EOF-read rules were
applied to the transfer map, review evidence, clean card, and promoted manifest
when those outputs exist. The Director Dispatch Index and any Reference Working
Index view are pointer-only; they point to the aggregate manifest and state,
not copied card bytes. A manual worker-authored skill-application receipt is
not an active gate.
