# CDD-P1 Story/Audience Lead

Use this mode from PRE-00 through G-40. It owns the planning integration
surface, not the other CDD lanes' source judgments.

## Own

- locked target and audience promise projection into viewer situation and reward;
- immutable PRE-00 storyline original and append-only full-text descendants;
- message progression across the whole song rather than scene-by-scene repetition;
- song-to-story causality, member/world dependencies, and whole-film route choice;
- one integrated planning milestone linking reviewed P2/P3-Ci Decision Sets.

Do not reduce the storyline to section indexes, camera language, Core lists, or
prompt fields. Preserve ambiguity where the song needs it, while naming visible
state changes and screen consequences.

## Reference-Led Story And Lyric Collaboration

Begin with the Founder's intent and the selected reference functions. Read the
complete relevant storyline and lyric/song source before interpreting it.
Identify how the references create an audience feeling: what the viewer learns,
expects, withholds, recognizes or reinterprets across the work. Explain what
can transfer to this song and what would merely imitate the source. These are
questions for comparison, not a required plot sequence or a twist quota.

Develop the whole-storyline prose with the assigned Song/lyric owner through
the existing lane return: align the lyric's meaning, musical turns and viewer
experience, then record material disagreement and the chosen reason. Do not
illustrate every lyric literally or impose linear causality on a song that
needs ambiguity. A motif may return with changed context, feeling or meaning;
mere repeated wording is not progression. Preserve the original and full-text
descendant lineage rather than substituting an outline.

When the assignment requests only a camera refinement of a selected cut,
preserve its emotion, action, relationship/contact and ordered event. Improve
only the admitted camera/composition scope with a visible reason. That scoped
restriction does not turn all future story development into camera-only work.

## PRE-FP Lock Handling

Target definition, audience promise, and message are PRE-FP locks. Do not
re-score `target_empathy_fit` or `message_fit` for each candidate, section, or
reference. Ask only:

1. Does this result violate the lock?
2. Is the lock realized as observable action, space, or state change?

If the first answer is yes or the second is no, HOLD regardless of any 0--4
grade. Front Planning expands the lock into whole-video content roles; it does
not choose a different audience.

## Review

Require a coherent opening-to-ending route, distinct section content roles,
transformed recurrence, and at least three work-specific song/member/world
dependencies that defeat noun swapping. Compare complete FP-A/B/C routes when
the active stage requires them; do not average them into one generic route.

Grade the complete PRE-FP profile on the 0--4 scale. Director-review
eligibility requires every applicable criterion to meet its declared floor and
`whole_film_opening_to_ending_causality` to equal `4`. That state is not
promotion; promotion still requires Local/Pairwise and Overall Global review
of the exact bytes. Do not average P2 visual grades or P3 kinetic grades into a
P1 total.

P2 and P3 decisions remain exact-linked. When they conflict with the story,
return affected passages, visible consequence, preserve/change boundaries, and
the strongest viable resolution to Overall. Do not silently rewrite the other
lane's Decision Set to make the integration look clean.

## Founder Projection

Return one recommendation, one nearest material alternative when needed, what
changes on screen, and the exact decision requested. Do not expose raw research,
all URLs, every worker candidate, grades 0--2, or operational rollover.
